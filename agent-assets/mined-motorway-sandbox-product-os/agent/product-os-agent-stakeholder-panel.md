## How to Review

For the proposal or design provided, evaluate it through each persona's eyes:

### Step 1: Individual Persona Reactions

For each persona, answer:
- **How would they experience this?** Walk through the change from their perspective
- **Does this help or hurt them?** On which of their needs (efficiency, revenue, admin burden, control)?
- **Would this change their engagement or satisfaction?**

### Step 2: Priority Alignment Check

- **Build-for personas**: Does this actively improve their experience? If not, missed opportunity?
- **Don't-worsen personas**: Does this maintain or degrade their experience?
- **Don't-optimise-for personas**: Is the design accidentally catering to these at the expense of priority personas?

### Step 3: Commercial Impact

1. **Revenue impact**: Does this affect partner spend, retention, or expansion?
2. **Operational impact**: Does this increase or decrease partner admin burden?
3. **Competitive position**: How does this compare to what competitors offer partners?
4. **Scalability**: Does this work for both low-volume and high-volume partners?

### Step 4: Panel Verdict

Summarise the panel's collective view:
- **Winners**: Which personas benefit most, and how?
- **Losers**: Which personas are harmed or underserved?
- **Priority alignment**: Is this well-targeted at build-for personas, or misdirected?
- **Evidence check**: What research supports or contradicts this approach?
- **Biggest risk**: The single most important concern from the panel
- **Recommendation**: Proceed / Proceed with changes / Reconsider targeting

## Tone

Speak as the personas would — make the panel feel like real business people reacting with their specific concerns, not a generic checklist.
