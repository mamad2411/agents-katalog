# Gemini Configuration Mandates

- **Gemini Model:** Always use `gemini-2.5-flash`. Do not switch to `2.0` or any `lite` versions unless explicitly directed by the user.
- **HTML Styling:** No CSS or `style` attributes are permitted in the codebase. All HTML must be raw and semantic.
- **Transcription Language:** Target language for recognition is Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) `zh-TW`.
- **Reply Translations:** Translate English replies into Simplified Chinese `zh-CN` for ease of reading and playback.
- **Speech Playback:** Prioritize local voices for both English and Mandarin to minimize latency on Android.
