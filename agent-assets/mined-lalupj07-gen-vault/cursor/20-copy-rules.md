# Copy Rules

- No fake reviews or testimonials. Only use real ones I provide, with name and permission. If none exist, omit the section.
- No fake metrics (no "10,000+ users", "99.9% uptime", "4.9 stars", "$2M saved") unless I provide a verified source.
- No fake customer counters, live activity popups, or auto-incrementing numbers (no `setInterval` or random number counters for social proof).
- Hero text must be specific: state what the product is, who it is for, and the concrete outcome in plain language. Headline under about 12 words, plus one supporting sentence. Reject vague headlines like "Supercharge your workflow" or "The future of X".
- No em dashes (U+2014) anywhere in user-facing copy. Use a period, comma, colon or parentheses instead.
- Banned AI-slop words and phrases:
  - seamless, seamlessly
  - elevate
  - unlock
  - leverage
  - revolutionize
  - cutting-edge
  - game-changer
  - next-level
  - supercharge
  - empower
  - "in today's fast-paced world"
  - "take it to the next level"
  - "dive into"
  - "tapestry"
  - "streamline your"
- Write short, concrete sentences with real nouns and verbs. Vary sentence length. No triple-adjective lists.
