# Executive Summary

Write a one-page executive summary for a senior technology leader.

Source the content from `reports/` and the repository. Do not invent status.

Structure:

1. **Outcome** - what was delivered and the business capability it enables. Two sentences.
2. **Status** - on track, at risk, or blocked, with the evidence for that judgment.
3. **Risk** - the top three risks, each with impact and the decision or owner required to close it.
4. **Cost and effort posture** - current trajectory and any variance.
5. **Decision requested** - exactly what you need from this audience, or state that no decision is required.

Rules:

- No implementation detail, no tool names, no jargon.
- Every claim traces to an artifact in `reports/` or the repository.
- If evidence is missing, say so explicitly rather than estimating.
- One page. No appendix.
