# Nurovelle Content Rules

This project contains Nurovelle content workflows.

Nurovelle is a B2B AI, automation and business-efficiency brand. The target audience is mainly business owners, managing directors, decision-makers and operational teams in small and mid-sized companies.

## Tone

Use:

- factual language
- clear B2B wording
- practical business framing
- source-bound claims
- concise explanations
- no hype

Avoid:

- exaggerated marketing language
- vague AI buzzwords
- invented examples
- invented numbers
- invented studies
- unsupported ROI claims
- generic motivational content

## Source binding

When generating or transforming Nurovelle assets:

- Stay bound to the supplied source material.
- Do not invent facts.
- Do not add new studies or external claims.
- Do not create fake examples.
- If a claim is not present in the source, leave it out.
- If the format needs more material than available, mark it as insufficient or review-required.

## Nurovelle content principles

Nurovelle content should focus on:

- real business process problems
- automation potential
- manual work reduction
- operational efficiency
- data and process maturity
- AI readiness
- practical implementation paths
- realistic risk and effort
- decision support for SMEs

## CTA rules

- Do not invent new CTA systems.
- Do not add aggressive funnel language.
- Use existing CTA logic only when already present in the asset/profile/source context.
- If CTA grounding is unclear, mark it for review.

## Format handling

For Nurovelle long-source content, preserve enough atom variety for:

- Leadmagnet
- Onepager
- E-Mail sequence
- YouTube Longform
- Newsletter
- LinkedIn assets
- Shortform video assets

Longform formats require broader atom selection than shortform formats.

## Review expectations

Mark for review when:

- CTA is unclear
- source binding is weak
- risk of unsupported claim exists
- format structure is incomplete
- asset is usable but needs editorial inspection

Reject when:

- unsupported claims are central
- source grounding is missing
- asset contradicts the source
- safety rules block usage
