# HGGT (High Grade Gundam Thunderbolt) — curation hints

The High Grade prefix for kits tied to the Mobile Suit Gundam Thunderbolt
manga/ONA. Flat — one directory, one file per kit, no further nesting, no
`variants` sub-entities, same reasoning as
[`../../rg/CLAUDE.md`](../../rg/CLAUDE.md).

## Naming files: no number prefix

**Files are `<slugified-name>.yaml`** — only 4 of 6 kits (67%) have a
documented lineup number, same MG/RG/EG/PG/SD/HG*-flat-naming exception.

## Sourcing

Same `allpages&apprefix=HGGT ` + `redirects=1` method as
[`../hguc/CLAUDE.md`](../hguc/CLAUDE.md) — 13 raw pages resolved to 6 real
unique kits, no unreleased/`{{Canceled}}` kits found.

**Two of the six real kits are wiki *subpages* (`<base title>/ONA Ver.`,
`<base title>/Bandit Flower Ver.`) rather than top-level titles** — unlike
every prior sub-line's `/Variants` subpages (which are navigational list
pages with no infobox, always excluded), these two both have full
infoboxes with their own `Release Date` and `image`, confirming they're
real distinct kit releases, not list pages. Don't exclude a `/`-subpage
title just because it looks structurally similar to a `/Variants` page —
check for an infobox first. Renamed to parenthetical form for the `name`
field (`FA-78 Full Armor Gundam (Thunderbolt Ver.) (ONA Ver.)`) for
consistency with how every other bracketed variant is named elsewhere in
this project, since the wiki's own alternate parenthetical title for the
same page (`... (ONA Ver.)`) was itself just a redirect alias, not a
missing kit.

**One kit deferred `Release Date` to a page section** (`Bandit Flower
Ver.`) — read by hand: "January 19, 2018 (First Batch)".

**Images**: 6 of 6 kits had a usable infobox `image`, all verified HTTP
200.
