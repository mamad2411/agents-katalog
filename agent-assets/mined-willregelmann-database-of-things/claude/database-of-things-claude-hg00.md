# HG00 (High Grade Gundam 00) — curation hints

Covers the 2007-2009 Gundam 00 anime timeline at 1/144 scale. Flat — one
directory, one file per kit, no further nesting, no `variants`
sub-entities, same reasoning as [`../../rg/CLAUDE.md`](../../rg/CLAUDE.md).

## Naming files: no number prefix

**Files are `<slugified-name>.yaml`** — 55 of 74 kits (74%) have a
documented lineup number, the same MG/RG/EG/PG/SD/HGUC/HGGS/HGCE flat-
naming exception for consistency.

## Sourcing

Same `allpages&apprefix=HG00 ` + `redirects=1` method as
[`../hguc/CLAUDE.md`](../hguc/CLAUDE.md) — 138 raw pages resolved to 75
real targets, no `/Variants` navigational subpages. One resolved page
(`GN-003 Gundam Kyrios (Bombardment Equipment)`) carries its own
`{{Canceled}}` template and was excluded; 7 more pages merely *mention*
canceled sibling kits in their own "Variants" section boilerplate
(`''Italicized items are exclusives, while strikethrough lines are
canceled items.''`) — don't treat a bare "canceled" keyword hit as
sufficient, always confirm the page's own infobox/template, same lesson
as PG's two `{{Canceled}}` kits.

**Images**: all 74 kits had a usable infobox `image` — no fallback
photos needed. All 74 also had a `Release Date`, though 9 resolve to
"See below" deferrals and stay undated, same as every other sub-line.
