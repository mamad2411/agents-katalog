# HGAW (High Grade After War) — curation hints

The High Grade prefix for Mobile Suit Gundam X's After War timeline. Flat
— one directory, one file per kit, no further nesting, no `variants`
sub-entities, same reasoning as [`../../rg/CLAUDE.md`](../../rg/CLAUDE.md).

## Naming files: no number prefix

**Files are `<slugified-name>.yaml`** — only 5 of 7 kits (71%) have a
documented lineup number, same MG/RG/EG/PG/SD/HG*-flat-naming exception.

## Sourcing

Same `allpages&apprefix=HGAW ` + `redirects=1` method as
[`../hguc/CLAUDE.md`](../hguc/CLAUDE.md) — 12 raw pages resolved to 7 real
unique kits, no unreleased/`{{Canceled}}` kits found.

`DT-6800A Daughtress` (October 2020 first batch, resold August 2026 —
recorded the first-release date) and `GW-9800-B Gundam Airmaster Burst`
(October 2020).

not `HGAW-...`) — a wiki upload-naming inconsistency, not a sign the kit
belongs elsewhere; it's still tagged `Category:HGAW` and titled `HGAW
