# Personal Growth Prompts

Source: "6 Claude Prompts to Rebuild Yourself in 2026" carousel (@aibuildguide). Six short persona prompts, each usable standalone by pasting into a conversation, a custom Project's instructions, or a project-level system prompt.

## Important distinction from `advisory-board`

`advisory-board` in this repo is built around **named, real advisors and their actual documented material** — it explicitly refuses to improvise a generic "expert" impression with no real source behind it. These 6 prompts are the opposite case, used honestly: they're **openly generic archetypal personas** (a mentor, a CEO, a coach), not standing in for a real person. Offer them as exactly that — a fast, no-setup starting point — and point to `advisory-board` instead when the user wants advice grounded in a specific real person's actual thinking rather than a generic archetype.

## The 6 prompts

### 1. Become My Elite AI Mentor
General mentorship across thinking, decisions, productivity, communication, and problem-solving — challenges weak ideas and names blind spots rather than just validating.

### 2. Think Like a CEO
Business-strategist framing for evaluating ideas/goals/problems — risk/opportunity identification and a recommended path, for "am I thinking about this at the right altitude" moments.

### 3. Upgrade My Life
Broad life-optimization coaching across health, productivity, finances, and learning — for a full-picture pass rather than one specific decision.

### 4. Brutally Honest Advisor
Deliberately un-hedged feedback — flaws in plans/assumptions, explicit "will/won't work" verdicts. Use when validation-seeking is the actual risk, not when a supportive tone is what's needed.

### 5. Personal Success Operating System
Ongoing accountability and prioritization across goals — best used as a standing/recurring frame (e.g. inside a Project's custom instructions) rather than a one-off question.

### 6. The 10x Improvement Coach
Leverage-focused: for any stated goal, finds the highest-leverage action and cuts the rest. Same underlying lens as this repo's `the-leverage-stack-auditor`, applied to a single goal in the moment rather than a full income/activity audit.

## How to use these

- **One-off question**: paste the relevant prompt at the start of the conversation, then ask the actual question.
- **Standing persona**: paste into a Project's custom instructions (e.g. Claude.ai Projects) so every conversation in that Project opens with that framing already active.
- **Picking which one**: match the prompt to the actual need — CEO-framing for a business decision, Brutally Honest Advisor when the risk is a plan that sounds better than it is, 10x Improvement Coach when the question is "what's the one thing," Personal Success OS for ongoing accountability rather than a single question.
- Don't stack multiple personas in one prompt — each is a distinct framing; combining them produces a muddier response than picking the one that actually fits.
