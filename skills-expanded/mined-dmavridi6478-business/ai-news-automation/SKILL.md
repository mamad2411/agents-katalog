# AI News Automation

Prompt for creating a daily AI morning brief automation in Codex or Claude Routines.

## Prompt

```
I want to automate staying updated with AI news and updates. Every day, search for the latest AI news, product launches, research papers, funding announcements, and important discussions from sources like Google News, Reddit, company blogs, and newsletters such as The Rundown AI and Superhuman. Filter out repetitive or low-value news and only keep updates that are important and relevant. Then create a summary with every news item in bullet points and add it to a Google Sheet titled "Daily AI News". Create proper columns: Column A for Date, Column B for News Summary, and Column C for Source Links.
```

## When to Use

Invoke `/ai-news-automation` when setting up an automated daily AI news brief. Paste the prompt into **Codex Automations** or **Claude Routines** (not a chat) and the tool will create the full automation for you.

## RTO Framework

- **R** (Repetitive task): Staying updated with AI news and updates
- **T** (Tools/steps): Googling AI news, checking Reddit, X, newsletters, etc.
- **O** (Output): All important updates in one place for content ideation

## Setup

1. Open Codex → Automations, or Claude → Routines
2. Paste the prompt above
3. The system creates the automation (runs daily at your chosen time)
4. Results land in a Google Sheet: Date | News Summary | Source Links
