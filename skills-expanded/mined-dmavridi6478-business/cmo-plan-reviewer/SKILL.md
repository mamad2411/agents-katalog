# CMO plan reviewer

Prompt (from "The CMO's ChatGPT Playbook", routine 06): "Review this plan against our goals and budget. List risks, gaps, and three questions I should ask."

## Procedure
1. Get the plan, the goals (with numbers and dates) and the budget. If goals or budget are missing, say which and review only what can be checked.
2. Check each plan element against a goal; list elements with no goal ("orphan activity") and goals with no element ("uncovered goal").
3. Check spend against budget by channel and period; flag anything unfunded or over-funded.
4. Output exactly: **Risks** (ranked, each with evidence from the plan), **Gaps** (missing owners, dates, metrics, assumptions), **Three questions** the leader should ask before approving.
5. Do not rewrite the plan. Do not invent benchmarks; mark unsupported assumptions as "assumption".
