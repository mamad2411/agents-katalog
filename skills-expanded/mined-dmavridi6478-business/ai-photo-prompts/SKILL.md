# AI Photo Prompts

9 professional AI photo prompts by @simplydigital. Upload your photo and swap the bracketed placeholders to generate studio-quality images with any AI image tool.

## 1. DSLR Photographer Mode

```
Transform my uploaded photo into a professional DSLR portrait. Keep my face, facial structure, skin tone, hairstyle, and identity recognizable and natural. Place me in [location] with realistic lighting, natural skin texture, shallow depth of field, subtle background separation, realistic lens perspective, professional. Make it look like a real photographer took the photo, not an AI generated image.
```

## 2. Cinematic Photoshoot

```
Turn my uploaded photo into a cinematic professional photoshoot. Place me in [scene] with dramatic but realistic lighting, natural shadows, shallow depth of field, subtle background blur, cinematic composition, realistic skin texture, detailed clothing, and professional colour grading. Keep my identity and facial features consistent. The final image should look like an actual photograph from a high end cinematic photoshoot.
```

## 3. Luxury Instagram Photoshoot

```
Act as my private [LUXURY INSTAGRAM] tutor. Transform my uploaded photo into a premium lifestyle photograph suitable for a high end Instagram profile. Put me in [luxury location], wearing [outfit], with sophisticated lighting, realistic environmental details, natural pose, professional composition, shallow depth of field, and authentic skin texture. Make it look expensive and editorial without making it look artificial or overly edited.
```

## 4. Travel Influencer Look

```
Turn my uploaded photo into an authentic professional travel photograph taken in [destination]. Keep my face and identity consistent while naturally integrating me into the location. Match the lighting, shadows, perspective, weather, and environment realistically. Adjust my clothing to suit the local climate. Use a professional travel photography composition with realistic depth, natural colours, detailed surroundings, and subtle background separation.
```

## 5. Magazine Cover Look

```
Transform my uploaded photo into a high end fashion editorial photograph. Keep my identity recognizable and natural. Give me a sophisticated pose, [outfit/style], professional studio or editorial lighting, realistic skin texture, carefully controlled shadows, premium composition, subtle depth of field, and polished but believable colour grading. Make it look like a real fashion photographer shot it for a major magazine.
```

## 6. Restore Old Photos

```
Restore my uploaded photo into a clear, high quality portrait. Preserve my actual facial identity, expression, hairstyle, and clothing exactly while improving lighting, sharpness, skin detail, exposure, background, and overall photographic quality. Do not invent details that are not visible in the original. Make it look as though the original photo had been captured today using a professional camera and high quality portrait lens.
```

## 7. Change Your Location

```
Keep me exactly recognizable from the uploaded photo, with the same pose and expression, but completely change the environment to [location]. Make the lighting, shadows, perspective, reflections, atmosphere, clothing interaction, and background naturally match the new location. Photograph me as if I was genuinely there with a professional DSLR camera. Preserve realistic skin texture and natural facial details.
```

## 8. Professional Headshot

```
Act as my private [PROFESSIONAL HEADSHOT] tutor. Transform my uploaded photo into a professional headshot suitable for LinkedIn, a company website, or a personal brand. Keep my face, facial structure, skin tone, hairstyle, and identity recognizable and natural. Use clean professional lighting, realistic skin texture, natural expression, subtle background separation, sharp facial details, and polished composition. Make it look like a real portrait taken by a professional photographer using a high end camera.
```

## 9. Golden Hour Photoshoot

```
Transform my uploaded photo into a professional golden hour photograph. Keep my face, facial structure, skin tone, hairstyle, and identity recognizable and natural. Place me in [location] with warm natural sunlight, realistic shadows, soft highlights, natural colours, subtle background separation, and authentic environmental details. Match the lighting and perspective realistically with the location. Make it look like a genuine photograph taken by a professional photographer during golden hour.
```

## Usage

Replace bracketed placeholders before submitting:
- `[location]` → e.g. "a rooftop in Manhattan at sunset"
- `[scene]` → e.g. "a foggy Parisian alleyway"
- `[destination]` → e.g. "Santorini, Greece"
- `[outfit/style]` → e.g. "tailored black suit"
- `[luxury location]` → e.g. "a Monaco yacht deck"
- `[outfit]` → e.g. "cream linen co-ord"
