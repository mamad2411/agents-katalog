# The 3 Levels of B2B Sales on LinkedIn

Source: ContentSmash infographic, "The 3 Levels of B2B Sales on LinkedIn."
A maturity ladder — each level assumes the previous one is already solid.
Diagnose which level the user is actually operating at before recommending
tactics from a higher one; skipping levels is the most common failure mode
(e.g. running DM campaigns off a profile with no clear ICP or CTA).

## Level 1 — Beginner

| Step | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Profile Setup** | Professional headshot, clear headline, benefit-focused About section |
| **Content Creation** | Post consistently with value — share problems the ICP actually faces |
| **Profile Optimization** | Compelling headline, clear CTA, make the next step obvious |
| **ICP Clarity** | Define industry, role, and the pain points solved for |
| **First Impression** | Answer in 10 seconds: what do you do? who do you help? why care? |
| **Call To Action** | Give people direction — lead magnet, book a call, be explicit |

## Level 2 — Intermediate

| Step | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Lead Magnet Strategy** | Create a valuable resource, build an email list, own the distribution |
| **DM Follow-Up System** | Track engagement, send personalized DMs, start conversations first |
| **Email Sequences** | Balance awareness, credibility, and conversion messaging |
| **Engagement Tactics** | Comment consistently, build relationships, show up strategically |
| **Content Repurposing** | Reshare best posts, double down on what converts |
| **Conversion Tracking** | Monitor profile clicks, track sales conversations, measure what matters |

## Level 3 — Advanced

| Step | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Sales Positioning** | Create frameworks, develop unique IP, own a perspective |
| **Strategic Partnerships** | Partner with creators, expand reach, drive results collaboratively |
| **Unique Point Of View** | Be known for something specific, build a brand around conviction |
| **Brand Authority** | Consistent visuals, memorable messaging, distinctive content |
| **High-Ticket Sales System** | Convert cold traffic into premium clients, build sophisticated frameworks |
| **Community Building** | Scale beyond transactions, create lasting value at scale |

## How to use this

1. Ask which of the 6 items at the user's current (or claimed) level are
   actually in place — don't assume. A missing ICP or CTA at Level 1 breaks
   everything built on top of it.
2. Fix the lowest broken item first, even if the user wants to jump ahead
   to Level 3 tactics (partnerships, high-ticket systems) — those fail
   without the Level 1/2 foundation (clear ICP, a working lead magnet,
   a DM follow-up habit).
3. Once a level's 6 items are solid, move to the next.
