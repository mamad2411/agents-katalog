# The 2026 GTM funnel - 7 stages and the tools on the slide

| # | Stage | Goal | Tool categories and examples on the slide |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Traffic generation | Create demand across channels | Content (LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Instagram); SEO and GEO (Google, ChatGPT, Gemini); Ads (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn); Communities (Reddit, Product Hunt, Slack) |
| 2 | Lead capture | Turn visitors into qualified leads | Landing pages (Webflow); forms (Typeform); chat and AI (Intercom); calendars (Calendly); lead magnets (ConvertKit); gated content (HubSpot) |
| 3 | Lead nurture | Educate, build trust, stay top of mind | Email (Instantly); newsletters (beehiiv); drip campaigns (HubSpot); webinars (Zoom); content sequence (ConvertKit) |
| 4 | Outbound | Proactively reach the right buyers | Prospecting (HeyReach); cold outreach (Lemlist); B2B database (Apollo); enrichment (Clearbit); LinkedIn outreach (Sales Navigator) |
| 5 | Sales process | Manage pipeline and close deals | CRM (HubSpot); pipeline (Pipedrive); meetings (Calendly); proposals (PandaDoc); e-signatures (DocuSign) |
| 6 | Conversion | Turn opportunities into revenue | Demos (Demostack); trial/signup (Productboard); payments (Stripe); onboarding (Appcues); activation (Userpilot) |
| 7 | Retention and expansion | Drive usage, renewals and growth | Support (Intercom); product analytics (Amplitude); NPS and feedback (SurveyMonkey); community (Circle); upsell (HubSpot) |

## Connectable from this session (checked against the tools listed in this session, 3 Oct 2026)
- **Connected:** HubSpot, Calendly, Amplitude, Slack, Gmail, Klaviyo (email, an alternative to the slide's email tools), Notion, Zapier (reaches thousands of apps, including most of those above), Semrush and Ahrefs (stage 1 SEO), Clay and Vibe Prospecting (stage 4 data and enrichment, alternatives to Apollo and Clearbit), Fireflies (calls).
- **Not connected here:** Webflow, Typeform, Intercom, ConvertKit, Instantly, beehiiv, HeyReach, Lemlist, Apollo, Clearbit, Sales Navigator, Pipedrive, PandaDoc, DocuSign, Demostack, Productboard, Stripe, Appcues, Userpilot, SurveyMonkey, Circle. Reach them through Zapier if a Zap exists; otherwise they need their own connector. Nothing was installed for them in this batch.

## Use
1. List what you run today per stage; mark empty stages. Most small teams are weakest at stages 3 and 7.
2. Do not add a tool for a stage until the stage's input volume exists (a nurture tool with no leads is shelfware).
3. One system of record: pick the CRM first (HubSpot or Pipedrive on the slide), then fit the rest around it.
