# 6 ChatGPT Prompts That Turn Overwhelm Into An Action Plan
*From @the21.i — AI That Matters*

Apply sequentially or pick the one that matches the current blocker.

---

## 1. Empty Your Brain

```
Here is everything currently taking up space in my head: [paste your messy brain dump].

Turn this into a clean action system. Separate it into:
- urgent tasks
- important tasks
- things to schedule
- things to delegate
- things to ignore
- things that are not actually tasks

Then tell me the three things I should focus on first.
```

---

## 2. Plan the Week for Me

```
These are the things I need to accomplish this week:

[paste list]

My available time is:

[paste schedule]

My biggest priorities are:

[priorities]

Create a realistic weekly plan.
Protect time for the highest-value work first.
Include buffer time and recovery.
If everything cannot reasonably fit, tell me what should be delayed instead of pretending it can.
```

---

## 3. Just Tell Me What to Do Next

```
I feel overwhelmed and I am procrastinating. This is what I need to get done:

[paste task or project]

Break it into actions small enough that I can start immediately. Then give me only:
- what to do in the next 10 minutes
- what to do in the next 30 minutes
- what to do after that

Remove anything non-essential.
```

---

## 4. Find What AI Can Do for Me

```
Here is my current workload:

[paste tasks]

Analyse each task and classify it as:
1. I should do it myself
2. AI can assist me
3. AI can produce a first draft
4. It should be automated
5. It should be delegated to another person
6. It probably should not be done at all

For anything AI can help with, write the exact next prompt I should use.
```

---

## 5. Turn Notes Into Action

```
Here are my notes from a meeting, call or conversation:

[paste notes]

Extract:
- decisions made
- actions required
- responsible person
- deadlines mentioned
- unresolved questions
- risks or blockers
- anything I promised to follow up on

Finish with a short priority list for me personally.
```

---

## 6. Find the Bottleneck

```
I am trying to achieve: [goal].

This is where I currently am: [current situation].

Ask me up to five questions if necessary. Then identify the single biggest bottleneck preventing progress. Explain why it matters more than the other problems. Give me the smallest practical action that would start removing that bottleneck today.
```
