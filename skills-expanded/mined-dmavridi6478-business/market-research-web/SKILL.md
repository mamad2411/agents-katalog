# Market Research (Web Search)

Run a live web search to get the current state of a niche market — stats, trends, and growth areas.

## Usage

```
/market-research-web [your niche] [country/region]
```

## Prompt

What is the current state of [your niche] market in the [country] in 2026? Search for recent stats, trends, and growth areas.

## Steps

1. Web-search for market size, growth rate, and forecasts for the niche.
2. Search for recent industry reports, news, and analyst commentary.
3. Identify top 3 trends driving the market.
4. Identify top 3 growth areas or underserved segments.
5. Return a structured report:
   - **Market size & growth** — current figures and CAGR
   - **Key trends** — what's shaping the market
   - **Growth areas** — where the opportunity is
   - **Sources** — list the sources cited
