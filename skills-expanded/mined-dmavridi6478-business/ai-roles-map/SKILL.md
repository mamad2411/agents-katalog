# 21 AI specialisms and the Capability Integrator

The infographic's idea: 21 specialist roles, and one role, the **Capability Integrator**, that connects the disciplines to business outcomes. The descriptions below are the infographic's own, shortened.

| # | Role | Does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | AI Solutions Architect | Designs scalable systems and integrates models into real applications |
| 2 | AI Trainer / Annotator | Prepares and labels data to improve model performance |
| 3 | AI/ML Engineer | Builds, trains and deploys machine-learning models in production |
| 4 | Prompt Engineer | Crafts prompts that improve output quality and reliability |
| 5 | MLOps Engineer | Manages deployment, monitoring and model lifecycle |
| 6 | AI Ethics Specialist | Keeps AI fair, transparent and compliant |
| 7 | AI Research Scientist | Develops algorithms that advance AI capabilities |
| 8 | AI Product Manager | Defines strategy, use cases and business impact |
| 9 | Data Scientist (AI focused) | Extracts insights and builds predictive models |
| 10 | Computer Vision Engineer | Builds systems that understand images and video |
| 11 | Robotics AI Engineer | Creates intelligent robots for automation and control |
| 12 | Natural Language Processing Engineer | Builds systems that understand and generate language |
| 13 | AI Security Specialist | Protects AI from attacks, misuse and vulnerabilities |
| 14 | Speech Recognition Engineer | Converts speech into text and commands |
| 15 | Reinforcement Learning Engineer | Builds models that learn through rewards and decisions |
| 16 | AI Cost Optimization Engineer | Reduces inference cost while maintaining performance |
| 17 | AI Agent Engineer | Builds agents that plan, act and use tools |
| 18 | AI Integration Engineer | Connects models with APIs, tools and enterprise systems |
| 19 | LLM Engineer | Fine-tunes, optimizes and deploys large language models |
| 20 | AI Data Engineer | Builds pipelines for clean, AI-ready data |
| 21 | AI Knowledge Engineer | Structures knowledge bases using RAG and embeddings |
| 1* | **Capability Integrator** | Connects disciplines to business outcomes |

Use: when asked "who do I need for this AI project?", name the 2 or 3 roles from the table that the work actually needs rather than all 21, and say which can be a skill or agent here versus a person.
