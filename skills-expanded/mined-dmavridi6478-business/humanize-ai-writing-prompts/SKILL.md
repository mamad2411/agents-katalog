# 8 Prompts to Humanize AI Writing
ChatGPT and Claude have a writing style people can recognize. Use these to fix it.
*From @the.wealth.lab*

Apply these sequentially or pick the ones that fit your text. Each targets a specific AI writing pattern.

---

## 1. Break the Perfect Structure

```
Rewrite this without forcing every idea into a neat, predictable structure. Vary sentence lengths, paragraph sizes, and pacing. Keep some ideas concise and give others more room when needed. Remove unnecessary transitions and avoid making every paragraph follow the same pattern. Do not change the meaning, facts, or key information.
```

## 3. Make it Sound Like a Real Person

```
Rewrite this in a natural conversational voice. Use the kind of language a knowledgeable person would use when explaining the topic to someone else. Prefer clear, direct wording over formal, corporate, or overly polished phrasing. Do not add fake experiences, emotions, opinions, slang, or mistakes.

```

## 4. Give it a Distinctive Voice

```
Edit this so it has a stronger point of view instead of sounding neutral and generic. Make the writer's existing priorities, opinions, and emphasis more noticeable. Do not invent opinions, experiences, credentials, or claims that are not supported by the original text.
```

## 5. Cut the "Too Perfect" Language

