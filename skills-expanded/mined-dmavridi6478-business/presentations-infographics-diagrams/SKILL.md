## Workflow
1. Identify audience and the one message; choose form from the table.
2. For data charts, apply the Tableau Visual Vocabulary logic (comparison, distribution, change over time, part-to-whole, relationship) before choosing a chart.
3. Draft structure in Markdown; hand to the tool; review every number against the source.
4. Deliverables from Claude Code in this workspace are published as Artifacts (Aptos font, body 11, titles 32, headings 14, table cells top-aligned and centred).
