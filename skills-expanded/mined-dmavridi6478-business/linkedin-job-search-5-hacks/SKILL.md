# 5 LinkedIn job-search hacks

1. **Profile formula.** Headline = Role + Industry Focus + Your Impact (example on the slide: "VP of Sales | SaaS | Grew ARR 3X to $100M"). About = who you are, what you do, why it matters. Featured = proof (slides, wins, media, PDFs): "show, don't just tell". Include the exact job titles, industries, skills and tools you want to be found for.
2. **Strategic commenting.** Comment on exec posts (seen by decision-makers); add value, not just praise (shows expertise); tag someone or ask a question (keeps the conversation going).
3. **Post weekly**, even if you are not a "creator": start with a lesson, tip or insight; add a short story or stat; end with a simple call to action. Slide claim: weekly posters get 5X more profile views and 7X more connection requests (unverified; LinkedIn is cited as the source but no link is given).
4. **The DM that gets responses.** Template: "Hi [name], I saw your post on [topic]. Really appreciated your viewpoint. I've worked on something similar and was curious how your team is approaching [X] right now." Low pressure, easy to reply to, personal and relevant, opens doors without asking for a job.
5. **Open to Work without the banner.** Profile, "Open to", choose "Recruiters only" visibility, add job titles, locations and types of roles. Slide claim: this boosts you in recruiter search without the public badge. Caveat: "recruiters only" hides the badge from most viewers but LinkedIn states it cannot guarantee that it is hidden from people at your own company, so do not rely on it to hide a search from your employer.
