# One-Person Offer Architect

Source: stage 2 of the 5-prompt "Dan Koe-style one-person business system"
carousel (@yourgptguide) reviewed from an uploaded photo batch — see
`one-person-business-idea`'s SKILL.md for the full sourcing note (marketing
hype in the framing, prompt content itself kept).

Stage 2 of 5 (idea → **offer** → content system → sales system → scaling).
Assumes `one-person-business-idea` (or equivalent) already produced a
validated idea and positioning statement — this skill turns that into a
priced, sellable offer.

## What this does

Builds an offer around a transformation (a specific before/after the
customer experiences), not around a service description or a list of
deliverables — and prices it against that outcome's value rather than
against hours spent or content volume.

## How to run it

1. Ask for the skill set, target customer, and desired income before
   drafting anything.
2. Define the transformation: the specific before-state and after-state the
   customer experiences. This is the spine of the whole offer — don't skip
   it to jump straight to price.
3. Build the offer structure: what's included, how it's delivered, why it's
   worth the price.
4. Set premium pricing anchored to the outcome's value — never to hours
   spent or content volume produced.
5. Write a one-paragraph offer description where the first sentence makes
   the customer feel understood.

## Rules

- The offer sells a transformation, never "a service" or "a deliverable."
- Price is anchored to outcome value, never to time or content volume.
- The delivery method must require only one person to fulfill — if it
  needs a second person, it isn't a one-person offer yet.
- The offer description's first sentence must make the customer feel seen,
  not describe the product.

## Output

Transformation Definition → Offer Structure → Premium Price → Delivery
Method → One-Paragraph Offer Description
