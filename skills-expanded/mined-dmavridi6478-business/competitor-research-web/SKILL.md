# Competitor Research (Web Search)

Run a live web search to pull current pricing, services, positioning, and customer sentiment for a named competitor.

## Usage

```
/competitor-research-web [competitor name]
```

## Prompt

Search for [competitor name] and give me their current pricing, services, positioning, and any recent changes in 2026. What are customers saying about them?

## Steps

1. Web-search for the competitor's website, pricing page, and LinkedIn.
2. Search for recent news or product updates (last 6 months).
3. Search for customer reviews on G2, Trustpilot, Reddit, or similar.
4. Return a structured report:
   - **Pricing** — current tiers and prices
   - **Services / product** — what they offer
   - **Positioning** — how they describe themselves
   - **Recent changes** — any 2026 updates
   - **Customer sentiment** — what people praise and complain about
