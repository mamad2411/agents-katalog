# Inbound Content Playbook

Source: reviewed from an uploaded infographic — "CEO's Playbook: Generating
Inbound Leads Using Content" (smarterwithai.news). No repo or literal AI
prompt here — it's a sequential content/growth methodology, written up as
a skill the same way the source presented it: 12 numbered steps forming a
loop, each with an example.

## The loop

Visibility → Trust → Conversations → Inbound → Feedback → (back to
Visibility). Two principles carry the whole thing: **consistency creates
compounding** (momentum, not one viral post, is what produces inbound),
and **clarity beats volume** (one sharp audience and message beats posting
more often about more things). The trust arrow specifically runs
Engagement → DMs → warm inbound — conversations are the mechanism, not a
side effect.

## The 12 steps

1. **Pick one clear audience.** Choose exactly who inbound should come
   from (e.g. founders / CTOs / buyers / operators), identify their
   biggest business pain, understand what keeps them stuck today, and
   create content only for that audience.
   *Example: CTOs at B2B SaaS teams struggling with onboarding
   reliability.*

2. **Share while building.** Document the journey rather than waiting for
   perfection — product decisions, lessons learned, customer feedback,
   experiments, failures and course corrections all count as content.
   *Example: "What we changed after 5 onboarding calls this week."*

3. **Define your core message.** State the problem solved in one
   sentence, highlight the outcomes enabled, share the founder's unique
   perspective, and repeat it consistently across posts rather than
   varying the message post to post.
   *Example: "We cut reporting time from days to minutes for ops teams."*

4. **Stay consistent.** Inbound comes from repetition, not one viral
   post — show up weekly, build familiarity over time; visibility
   compounds quietly, not dramatically.
   *Example: 3 posts/week — one insight, one story, one framework.*

5. **Engage before selling.** Relationships before revenue: reply
   thoughtfully to comments, join discussions on relevant posts, build
   rapport publicly before any pitch.
   *Example: add a helpful follow-up question on every thread.*

6. **Move to DMs naturally.** Continue discussions that started publicly
   in messages, ask open-ended questions, learn about their challenges —
   understand before offering solutions.
   *Example: "What's the biggest bottleneck in your workflow?"*

7. **Teach simple concepts.** Explain complex topics in plain language,
   break ideas into practical frameworks, share actionable insights
   regularly — help people understand, not just consume.
   *Example: a 3-step checklist to diagnose churn drivers.*

8. **Use LinkedIn as distribution.** Buyers already spend time there —
   each post is a discovery channel, the profile becomes a living landing
   page, and content gradually replaces cold outreach.
   *Example: pin a "start here" post with a clear headline.*

9. **Add your perspective.** Thinking is the differentiator — explain why
   a decision was made, share surprises and trade-offs, show how the CEO
   actually approaches problems, not just the polished outcome.
   *Example: "Why we chose reliability over features this quarter."*

10. **Let authority pre-sell.** The content does the convincing before the
    call — prospects already know the expertise and values going in, so
    sales conversations start warmer.
    *Example: a lead references a specific post before the first call.*

11. **Track what works.** Optimize on real engagement, not vanity metrics
    — monitor replies and inbound DMs specifically, identify which topics
    spark actual conversations, refine weekly and double down.
    *Example: track saves, comment quality, and DM intent — not just
    likes/impressions.*

12. **Repeat the growth loop.** Build visibility → earn trust → start
    conversations → improve with feedback → attract inbound, then go
    again with what was learned.
    *Example: turn top-performing posts into a series, and update the
    profile's social proof to reflect what's landed.*

## Quick self-check

Before starting a cycle (or when it's stalled), five questions to answer
plainly:

- Who is my one audience?
- What is my one-sentence value message?
- What's my weekly posting cadence?
- What question will I ask in DMs?
- Which 2 topics generated the most conversations?
