# McKinsey 7S model

Source: infographic by Eric Partaker ("The 7 Strategic Levers of High-Performance Companies" and "6 Actionable Tips"). The 7S framework is a McKinsey consulting model (Waterman and Peters, 1980s); the scoring protocol and the tips below are Partaker's practitioner advice, not validated research.

## The seven S's
- **Hard:** Strategy (how you win), Structure (how work is organised; clear roles, fast decisions), Systems (repeatable processes that drive execution).
- **Soft:** Shared Values (core beliefs, centre of the model), Style (leadership approach people follow), Staff (talent aligned with the mission), Skills (capabilities that drive performance and are hard to replicate).

## The six tips as a procedure
1. **Audit first.** Score each S 1–10. Ask 3–5 key leaders to score independently, before discussing. Gaps of 3+ points between scorers reveal blind spots and priority areas.
2. **Shared Values first.** If values are not lived daily the other six will not stick. Define 3–5 core values; audit every major decision against them.
3. **Structure before Staff.** Hiring into a broken structure multiplies problems. Map decision rights, remove overlapping roles, clarify reporting lines before the next hiring wave.
4. **Align Systems to Strategy.** Quarterly planning, budgeting and performance reviews must support the strategy; if they do not, you are rewarding the wrong behaviour.
5. **Make Style measurable.** Define what good leadership looks like with behavioural anchors, then measure and coach against them in regular 360 reviews.
6. **Review all 7S quarterly.** 30 minutes in each quarterly leadership meeting: "What's out of sync?" Small misalignments compound.

## Output format
1. Score matrix: rows = 7 S, columns = each leader, plus mean and spread. Mark spread ≥ 3 with ⚠.
2. Top three blind spots (largest spread) with the quote or evidence that explains each.
3. Fix order: Shared Values → Structure → Systems → Style → Staff → Skills, but only for S's scoring ≤ 6 or flagged.
4. A 30-minute quarterly agenda: 5 min recap, 15 min on flagged S's, 10 min owners and dates.
Do not invent scores; if only one scorer exists, say the blind-spot test cannot run.
