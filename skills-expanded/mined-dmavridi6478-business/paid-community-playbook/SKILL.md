# Paid Community Playbook

## 1. Pick the platform by audience, not by hype

| Platform | Best for | Cost | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Skool** | Creators/educators wanting community + courses combined | $99/mo host fee, no per-member fee | Cleanest UX, serious-community feel |
| **Discord** | An audience already on Discord (tech, gaming, crypto, younger) | Free | More setup work, less structured |
| **WhatsApp + Beehiiv** | Under 50 members, genuine existing relationships | Low/free | Lowest barrier, doesn't scale past ~50 |

## 2. The first-20-members launch funnel

1. **Charter price**: "First 20 members get £[X]/month for life" — urgency + reward for early adopters.
2. **Warm audience first**: DM 30 people who already engage with your content before announcing publicly. A personal invite converts better than any public post.
3. **Public CTA**: End 3 consecutive pieces of content with "paid community launching [date] — early access in my bio."
4. **Free trial**: One week free for anyone who signs up in the first month, to lower the barrier. Most trial users convert to paid.

## 3. Onboarding sequence (send this to every new member)

```
Write a 3-message welcome sequence for someone who just joined my paid community.
Community name: [your community name]
What we are about: [mission and vibe]
The rules: [key expectations]
First win: [what should they do in their first 48 hours to get value fast?]

Message 1 (sent immediately): Warm welcome + where to start
Message 2 (sent day 2): One quick win — the most useful resource in the community
Message 3 (sent day 5): Introduce yourself in the community thread — [specific prompt to encourage sharing]

Tone: [warm / professional / energetic — match your community vibe]
```

## 4. Monthly content calendar (paste into Claude at the start of each month)

```
Help me plan a month of content for my paid community.
Community niche: [what it is about — e.g. AI side hustles for UK freelancers]
Member level: [beginner / intermediate / mixed]
How often I post: [daily / 3x week / weekly]
Formats available to me: [text posts / voice notes / video / live calls / resource drops]

Plan:
1. 4 weekly themes (one per week this month)
2. For each week: 3 post ideas with hooks and key points
3. One "community challenge" per month that drives engagement
4. One live session topic per month — with 5 questions to ask the group
5. One resource to share this month (prompt pack / template / checklist)
```

## 5. The 3 things that make members renew every month

1. **Forward motion** — members stay if they feel they're progressing. A monthly check-in ("what have you done this month?") makes progress visible.
2. **Access to you** — they're not paying for content, they're paying for your attention and perspective. Show up consistently.
3. **Community relationships** — members who know each other stay for the people, not just the content. Facilitate introductions every week.

## 6. The actual economics (run this math before committing to a price)

- 50 members × £20/month = £1,000/month recurring.
- Minus platform hosting (Skool: ~$99/month ≈ £79) → net ~£920/month.
- At 15–20 hours/month of AI-assisted content work: **£46–61/hour effective rate, compounding monthly** (a member who renews costs zero re-acquisition cost the following month, unlike a one-off client).
- Sensitivity check before promising this to yourself: the math holds only if churn stays low — the retention section above is not optional polish, it's what keeps the 50 members at 50 rather than replacing 10 of them every month just to stand still.
