# Life Map

Generate an in-depth psychological and personality map from a birth date.

## Prompt

```
Using my date of birth [DD/MM/YYYY], create an in-depth psychological and personality map.

Analyze my strengths, challenges, values, decision-making style, emotional needs, and potential internal contradictions.

I don't want a generic description. Connect the patterns to each other and explain how they might influence my life, relationships, and decisions.

End with the 5 strengths I should build on the most, the 5 challenges I should work on, and 3 questions I should ask myself.
```

## When to Use

Invoke `/life-map` when you want a structured self-knowledge exercise that connects personality traits to real decisions and relationships. Replace `[DD/MM/YYYY]` with your birth date before sending.

## Usage

1. Invoke the skill
2. Paste the prompt into Claude, replacing `[DD/MM/YYYY]` with your actual date of birth
3. Claude will produce a connected personality analysis with actionable strengths, challenges, and reflection questions
