## Workflow
1. Clarify: format (9:16 / 16:9), length, whether a real person's likeness or voice is involved, budget, commercial licence needed.
2. Shortlist 2 tools from the table; fetch each pricing/terms page and state the licence terms for commercial use.
3. Write the prompt with `ai-video-image-prompt-structure` (subject, action, camera, lighting, style, duration).
4. Generate 3-4 variants, then pick; never promise a model's output quality in advance.
5. For avatar, voice-clone or face-swap output, require consent from the person depicted and disclose AI use where the platform or law requires it.
