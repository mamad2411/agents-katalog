# AI Image and Design Tools

Source: iCloud note "Links and sites". Capability notes are general knowledge, not verified live; confirm pricing and commercial-use licence on the site before recommending. (unverified) = saved without a description.

## Generate images
- **Midjourney** (saved as "ai speech to photos") - stylised, high-aesthetic images.
- **Ideogram** (ideogram.ai) - best at text inside images; also logos/posters.
- **Leonardo.ai**, **Krea.ai**, **OpenArt** (openart.ai), **Flux** (flux1.ai), **Freepik AI** (freepik.com/ai), **Promeai** (promeai.com), **Raphael** (raphael.app), **stocking.ai** (picture generator), **mylens.ai**, **freefaces.gallery** (AI face library), **apob.ai** (images/avatars).
- **Blockade Labs** - 360-degree skyboxes.
- **backflip.ai** - 3D models; **zoo.dev/text-to-cad** - text to CAD; **formula.so** (2D to 3D logos).

## Clean up / edit
- **Cleanup.pictures**, **remove.photos**, **picwish.com**, **bgsub.com** (swap backgrounds), **dewatermark.ai** - remove objects/backgrounds/watermarks. Only remove watermarks from images you own or have rights to.
- **iloveimg.com**, **imagestool.com**, **insmind.com**, **luminarbrush** - general editing.

## Logos, mockups, brand
- Logos: logopony, logo.ai, logodiffusion, logosystem.co, zarla.com, Adobe free logo maker, pngmaker.io.
- Mockups/packaging: contentcore.xyz (mockups), pacdora.com (packaging).
- Palettes/colour: khroma. Design inspiration: eyecannndy.com.
- Stock: Pixabay, Freepik, flaticon.com (icons).
- Asset library manager: en.eagle.cool.

## UI / motion design
- **Uizard** (uizard.io), **Galileo AI**, **Flair AI** (product shots), **Spline** (3D web), **Jitter** (motion), **magicpatterns.com**, **uiverse.io**, **reactbits.dev** (see `repo-react-bits`), **liquid.paper.design**, **lovart.ai**, **designhill**, **Designs.ai**.

## Selection workflow
1. Ask the output: photo-real, illustration, text-heavy poster, logo, UI, 3D.
2. Pick one generator from the matching group, one cleanup tool if needed.
3. Write the prompt with `ai-photo-prompts` or `ai-video-image-prompt-structure`.
4. Check licence for commercial use; do not generate real people's likenesses without consent.
