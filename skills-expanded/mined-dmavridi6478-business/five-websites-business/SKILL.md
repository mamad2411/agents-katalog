# 5 websites most business owners do not know exist

| # | Site | Slide says | Check before you rely on it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wave (`waveapps.com`) | free invoicing and accounting software, no monthly fee | The slide's claim. Payments, payroll and some regions carry fees and Wave's availability varies by country. Confirm on its pricing page and check it supports VAT in your country. |
| 2 | SERPtag (`serptag.com`) | find and track your search ranking | Rankings differ by location and device; treat as a trend line. |
| 3 | Looka (`looka.com`) | AI logo and full brand kit in minutes | Check the licence and trademark position before using a generated logo commercially. |
| 4 | Google Business Profile (`business.google.com`) | manage how your business shows in local Google search and Maps | Free. Verification is required; keep name, address and hours consistent everywhere. |
| 5 | Manychat (`manychat.com`) | automations for Instagram, WhatsApp, TikTok and Messenger | **The slide's caption is a copy of slide 4's ("manage how your business shows up in local Google search and Maps") and is wrong.** The screenshot shows chat automation. Messaging rules (opt-in, templates) apply. |

## Where this repo helps

- Local visibility and rankings: `seo`, `seo-audit`, `os-seo` (drafts briefs), the Ahrefs and Semrush connectors.
- Branding: `brandkit-generator`, `brand-board` template.
- Invoicing and books: `os-invoice`, `os-bookkeeping`, `invoice-receipt-processor` (drafts only).
- Chat automation: `os-chat-triage` and the chat SOP in `docs/ai-os/ops/chat-channels-sop.md`.

Honest note: this is a five-item awareness list, not a vetted recommendation. None of these tools was tested.
