# How I Use Perplexity For Research
*From @earchoe — 3 repeatable workflows*

Perplexity finds facts with sources. Claude turns facts into usable output. The pair removes both hallucination risk and blank-page friction.

---

## Workflow 1 — Research to Content

**Step 1 — In Perplexity:**

```
What are the latest statistics and trends on [topic] in 2026? Give me 5 data points with sources.
```

**Step 2 — In Claude (paste Perplexity output):**

```
Here are 5 data points on [topic]:

[paste Perplexity output]

Use these to write a [TikTok carousel / LinkedIn post / newsletter section] for my audience of [describe audience]. Lead with the most surprising stat as the hook.
```

---

## Workflow 2 — Client Research Before a Pitch

**In Perplexity:**

```
Research [Company Name / Client's Business] and give me:
1. What they do and who their customers are
2. Recent news or changes in their business or industry
3. Who their main competitors are
4. What challenges businesses like theirs typically face
5. Any public reviews or reputation signals

Sources: their website, LinkedIn, Google reviews, news articles from the last 12 months
```

---

## Workflow 3 — Competitor Analysis + Positioning Gap

**In Perplexity (run both):**

```
What are [Competitor]'s prices, packages, and positioning in 2026?
```

```
What are the most common complaints about [Competitor] in reviews and forums?
```

**In Claude (paste both results):**

```
Based on this competitor analysis:

[paste Perplexity output]

What gap could I fill and how should I position myself differently?
```
