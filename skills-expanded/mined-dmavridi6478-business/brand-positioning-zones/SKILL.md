# Brand Positioning — the Three-Circle Zone Map

Source: Beloved Brands infographic. Three overlapping circles:

- **A. Consumers want**
- **B. Your brand does best**
- **C. Competitor does best**

| Zone | Where | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Winning** | A ∩ B, outside C | Consumers want it, you do it best, competitors don't own it | Build the positioning here; make it the headline |
| **Risky** | A ∩ B ∩ C | Wanted, you're good, but a competitor is equally strong | Only stay if you can out-execute or own it first; needs proof |
| **Dumb** | B only (outside A and C) | You are good at it but nobody wants it | Stop leading with it; keep only as a supporting fact |
| **Losing** | A ∩ C, outside B | Consumers want it and the competitor owns it | Do not fight here; reframe or concede |

Nothing in the C-only or A-only area is a positioning option: C-only is irrelevant, A-only is an unmet need (an *opportunity* to build capability, not a claim to make today).

## Procedure

1. Get the category, the target customer segment, and 2–4 named competitors. Ask for them if missing.
2. List 8–15 candidate claims/benefits (from product-marketing-context, reviews, sales calls).
3. For each claim, score with evidence (not opinion): **Want** (customer research, search demand, sales-call frequency), **Best** (proof rows: data, awards, testimonials, feature comparisons), **Competitor strength** (their site, reviews, pricing pages). Mark each "verified" or "assumed".
4. Assign each claim a zone. Show the table.
5. Pick ONE Winning claim as the lead position; choose ≤2 supporting Winning/Risky claims. Everything in Dumb/Losing is cut from headlines.
6. Draft a positioning statement: *For [segment] who [need], [brand] is the [category] that [Winning claim], because [proof].*
7. Flag "assumed" ratings as research to do before publishing.

## Rules

- A claim without proof for "Best" cannot be Winning — it is at most Risky. (Consistent with the proof-row gate in `governed-marketing-team`.)
- Competitor strength must be observed from public sources, never guessed.
- Re-run every 6–12 months; zones drift as competitors copy.
