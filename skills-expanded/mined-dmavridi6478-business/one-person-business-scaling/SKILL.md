# One-Person Business Scaling

Source: stage 5 of the 5-prompt "Dan Koe-style one-person business system"
carousel (@yourgptguide) reviewed from an uploaded photo batch — see
`one-person-business-idea`'s SKILL.md for the full sourcing note (marketing
hype in the framing, prompt content itself kept).

Stage 5 of 5 (idea → offer → content system → sales system → **scaling**),
the last stage. Assumes `one-person-sales-system` (or equivalent) is
already converting subscribers into clients — this skill scales the
revenue that produces without adding a team.

## What this does

Audits which of the operator's hours are currently traded directly for
money, then designs a digital-product/passive-income layer that delivers
the same outcome as the current offer at scale — explicitly refusing the
"just hire people" answer, since that stops being a one-person business.

## How to run it

1. Ask for current revenue, offer, and available time before planning
   anything.
2. Identify every hour currently traded directly for money and flag it as
   a productization candidate.
3. Design one digital product that scales the operator's knowledge without
   requiring their live presence.
4. Build a passive income layer: revenue that compounds while the operator
   is doing other things.
5. Map a 12-month scaling roadmap from current revenue to a target,
   explicitly without hiring.

## Rules

- Every scaling move must remain executable by one person.
- The digital product must deliver the *same outcome* as the current offer,
  just at scale — not a watered-down version.
- The passive income layer must be built before scaling outreach or content
  volume, not after — trying to scale attention onto a system that still
  requires 1:1 delivery just produces more manual work.
- The 12-month roadmap needs monthly revenue milestones, not vague
  direction.
- Never recommend hiring — one person stays one person. (If the plan
  genuinely requires a team, that's a signal this is no longer a
  one-person-business problem — say so rather than forcing a solo answer.)

## Output

Time Audit → Productization Plan → Digital Product Design → Passive Income
Layer → 12-Month Scaling Roadmap
