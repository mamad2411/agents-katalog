# SEO vs AEO vs AIO vs GEO

| | SEO | AEO | AIO | GEO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stands for | Search Engine Optimization | Answer Engine Optimization | AI Optimization | Generative Engine Optimization |
| Primary goal | Rank higher in search results | Get featured as the direct answer | Help AI understand and use your content | Get your brand or content cited in AI-generated answers |
| Search focus | Google, Bing | Search engines and featured snippets | AI assistants and AI-powered search | ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and similar |
| Content style | Keyword focused, comprehensive | Concise, factual, structured | Clear and structured, human-friendly, in-depth and relevant | Authoritative, original insights, conversational |
| Optimization focus | On-page, off-page, technical SEO | Schema markup, FAQs, structured data | Multi-format content, entity clarity, AI-readable structure | Semantic clarity, entity authority, brand signals and mentions |
| Users targeted | People searching online | People seeking instant answers | People using AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini) | People using generative AI for research |
| Success metrics | Rankings, organic traffic, conversions | Featured snippets, voice search visibility, zero-click visibility | AI citations, content usage, brand mentions | AI citations/mentions, referral traffic from AI, brand visibility |
| Key tactics | Keyword research, quality content, link building, technical optimization | Optimize for questions, use structured data, create clear answers | Create comprehensive content, use entities and schema, keep content updated | Create authoritative content, build brand mentions, optimize for entities |
| Key benefits | Long-term traffic, stable visibility, brand authority | Higher visibility, more brand trust, capture zero-click traffic | Higher AI visibility, more brand mentions, future-proof growth | Stronger brand presence, new traffic opportunities, long-term authority |

## Reading it honestly
- AIO and GEO overlap almost completely on the slide (entities, citations, mentions). Treat them as one workstream unless a client's vocabulary demands both.
- The shared base for all four: crawlable, fast, well-structured pages with clear entities and fresh content. Do SEO hygiene first; AEO/GEO tactics sit on top of it, they do not replace it.
- Measurement is the weak point: AI citations are hard to measure; Ahrefs Brand Radar and Semrush tools in this session offer AI-visibility reports (`mcp__Ahrefs__brand-radar-*`).
