## Workflow
1. Name the business outcome (leads, retention, cost, time saved) before picking a tool.
2. Check whether an installed MCP connector or existing skill (`competitor-analysis`, `gtm-strategy`, `market-research`) already does it.
3. For any outreach tool: confirm opt-in / CAN-SPAM / GDPR basis and never scrape or email lists you cannot lawfully use.
4. Report tools used and what was verified versus assumed.
