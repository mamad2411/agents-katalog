# One-Person Content System

Source: stage 3 of the 5-prompt "Dan Koe-style one-person business system"
carousel (@yourgptguide) reviewed from an uploaded photo batch — see
`one-person-business-idea`'s SKILL.md for the full sourcing note (marketing
hype in the framing, prompt content itself kept).

Stage 3 of 5 (idea → offer → **content system** → sales system → scaling).
Assumes `one-person-offer-architect` (or equivalent) already produced a
priced offer — this skill builds the content engine that drives attention
toward it.

## What this does

Builds a content system around one owned core thesis, expanded across
platforms, rather than a grab-bag of unrelated posts — with an explicit
repurposing map (one idea → many pieces) and a schedule sized to what one
person can sustain, not what looks impressive on a content calendar.

## How to run it

1. Ask for the niche, core idea, and target platforms before drafting
   anything.
2. Define the core content thesis: the one idea this person owns and
   expands indefinitely. Specific enough to own — not so broad that
   everyone already "covers" it.
3. Build a content expansion map: how that one idea becomes ~30 pieces of
   content (angles, formats, objections addressed, stories, data points).
4. Design a cross-platform repurposing system: one piece of source content
   → adapted versions on every chosen channel.
5. Create a weekly content schedule that builds authority without burning
   the person out.

## Rules

- The core thesis must be specific enough to own, not broad enough that
  everyone already covers it.
- Every piece of content must trace back to the core thesis — this is what
  keeps the system from becoming scattered.
- The repurposing system must be executable by one person in under 2 hours
  a week — if it isn't, redesign it, don't just note the gap.
- The weekly schedule must be sustainable for months, not just impressive
  on paper before collapsing in 30 days.

## Output

Core Thesis → Content Expansion Map → Cross-Platform System → Weekly
Schedule → Authority Building Arc
