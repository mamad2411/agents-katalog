# Faceless YouTube Channel Builder — 5 Strategic Prompts

Five battle-tested Claude prompts that cover the full lifecycle of building a profitable faceless YouTube channel. Each prompt maps to a slash command.

---

## /yt-niche — Niche & Channel Strategy

```
Act as a YouTube channel strategist with 10 years of experience building faceless channels. I want to start a faceless YouTube channel. My interests/skills are [YOUR INTERESTS]. My available time per week is [HOURS/WEEK]. My budget for tools is [BUDGET].

Analyze the current YouTube landscape and recommend:
1. The top 3 niche options with monetization potential above $10 RPM
2. For each niche: estimated monthly views needed to earn $1,000, competition level (low/medium/high), content difficulty, and required tools
3. My ideal niche based on my profile above, with a 3-sentence positioning statement
4. Channel name ideas (5 options) that are brandable, searchable, and niche-clear
5. The single biggest mistake new creators make in this niche and how to avoid it

Format as a clear report I can act on today.
```

## /yt-calendar — Content Calendar

```
Act as a YouTube content strategist. I have a faceless YouTube channel in the niche of [YOUR NICHE]. My posting goal is [X videos per week/month].

Create a 30-day content calendar that includes:
1. 12–16 video ideas ranked by estimated search volume potential (high/medium/low)
2. For each video: title, hook angle (curiosity/controversy/tutorial/listicle), target keyword, and estimated length
3. A content mix: [40% evergreen search SEO, 40% trending topics, 20% community/engagement content]
4. Optimal posting days and times for my niche
5. A content batching schedule so I can film/produce multiple videos in one session

Make titles click-worthy but not clickbait. Each title should create an information gap.
```

## /yt-seo — SEO & Growth Strategy

```
Act as a YouTube SEO and growth expert. My faceless YouTube channel is about [YOUR NICHE]. Current subscribers: [NUMBER]. Average views per video: [NUMBER].

Build me a complete SEO and growth strategy:
1. Top 10 seed keywords for my niche with low competition and decent search volume
2. How to structure video titles using the [Hook Keyword — Promise] formula with 3 examples
3. Description template I can reuse for every video (first 150 characters critical, CTA, timestamps, keywords)
4. Tag strategy: how many tags, what types, in what order
5. Thumbnail strategy: 3 proven thumbnail formulas for my niche with color/text/emotion guidance
6. How to use the community tab, cards, and end screens to maximize watch time
7. A 90-day growth roadmap with milestones at week 4, 8, and 12

Be specific. Give me templates I can copy, not general advice.
```

## /yt-script — Video Script Formula

```
Act as a professional YouTube scriptwriter for faceless channels. Write a complete script for a video titled: "[YOUR VIDEO TITLE]" for a channel in the [YOUR NICHE] niche. Target length: [8–12 minutes]. Tone: [informative/entertaining/authoritative].

The script must follow this structure:
- HOOK (0:00–0:30): Open with a shocking stat, bold claim, or question that creates an information gap. Do NOT say "welcome back." Get into the content immediately.
- INTRO PROMISE (0:30–1:00): Tell viewers exactly what they'll learn and why it matters to them right now.
- MAIN CONTENT (1:00–9:00): [5–7 sections with clear transitions, real examples, and specific data/stories]
- PATTERN INTERRUPTS: Every 2 minutes, add a reengagement line like "but here's where it gets interesting..." or a quick statistic drop
- CTA (9:00–9:30): Specific call to action — subscribe reason + next video recommendation
- OUTRO (9:30–10:00): Tease next video, no generic "like and subscribe" without reason

Write in a conversational, direct tone. Short sentences. No filler. Every line earns its place.
```

## /yt-monetize — Monetization Roadmap

```
Act as a YouTube monetization expert. I have a faceless YouTube channel in the niche of [YOUR NICHE]. Build me a realistic monetization roadmap for 2026 starting from 0 subscribers.

Include:
1. Step-by-step path to qualify for the YouTube Partner Program (watch hours + subscribers + timeline estimate)
2. Top 5 affiliate programs best suited to my niche with commission rates and how to integrate them naturally into videos
3. Sponsorship outreach strategy for small channels (under 10K subs): who to contact, what to say, realistic rates
4. 3 digital product ideas I can sell to my audience (ebook, template, course, etc.) with pricing suggestions
5. How to diversify income beyond AdSense: Patreon/memberships, Gumroad, email list strategy
6. Milestone targets:
   - Month 1: [subscriber/view/revenue goal]
   - Month 3: [subscriber/view/revenue goal]
   - Month 6: [subscriber/view/revenue goal]
7. The #1 monetization mistake faceless channels make and how to avoid it

Be specific with numbers. Give me a roadmap I can print and follow.
```
