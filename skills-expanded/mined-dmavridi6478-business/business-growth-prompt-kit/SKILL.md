# 20 Claude Prompts for Business Growth

Source: "20 Claude Prompts For Business Growth" (Matic Pogladic). Each
prompt below is reproduced verbatim, ready to copy-paste — fill in the
bracketed placeholders before sending. Where this repo already has a
deeper, multi-step skill for the same job, that's noted after the prompt:
reach for the prompt when a quick single-shot answer is enough, the skill
when the job needs research, iteration, or a structured deliverable.

## 1. Market Opportunity Analysis

```
Act as a senior market research analyst. I am building a business in
[industry] targeting [target audience]. Conduct a comprehensive market
opportunity analysis including market size (TAM, SAM, SOM), current
trends, demand drivers, emerging opportunities, underserved segments,
and future predictions. Identify at least 5 high-potential niches within
this market and explain why they are attractive. Provide structured
reasoning and provide data-backed assumptions wherever possible.
```
Deeper version: `market-sizing-analysis`, `startup-analyst` agent.

## 2. Ideal Customer Persona Deep Dive

```
Act as a behavioral psychologist and growth strategist. Create a highly
detailed ideal customer persona for my business in [describe offering].
Include demographics, psychographics, goals, fears, desires, buying
triggers, objections, daily routines, and online behavior. Also include
messaging angles that would resonate with this persona and examples of
persuasive hooks tailored to them.
```
Deeper version: `product-marketing-context`, `customer-research`.

## 3. Value Proposition Optimization

```
Act as a conversion-focused copywriting expert. Analyze my current value
proposition: [insert value proposition]. Improve it to make it clearer,
more compelling, and differentiated. Provide 5 improved versions, each
optimized for a different angle such as emotional appeal, logical
reasoning, urgency, simplicity, and uniqueness. Explain why each version
works.
```

## 4. Competitor Breakdown and Positioning

```
Act as a competitive intelligence expert. Analyze my top competitors:
[list competitors]. Break down their strengths, weaknesses, pricing,
positioning, messaging, and customer perception. Identify gaps in their
strategy and recommend how I can position my business uniquely to
outperform them. Provide a clear differentiation strategy.
```
Deeper version: `competitor-profiling`, `competitive-landscape`.

## 5. Business Model Expansion

```
Act as a business strategist. My current business model is: [describe
model]. Suggest 10 additional revenue streams or monetization strategies
that align with my business. For each, explain implementation steps,
required resources, expected ROI, and scalability potential.
```

## 6. Offer Creation

```
Act as a high-ticket offer architect. Based on my business in [industry]
and my target audience [describe audience], design a compelling offer
that is difficult to refuse. Include core offers, bonuses, pricing
structure, urgency mechanisms, risk reversal, and positioning. Ensure the
offer maximizes perceived value.
```
Deeper version: `offers`, `pricing`.

## 7. Sales Funnel Design

```
Act as a funnel optimization expert. Design a complete sales funnel for
my business: [describe business]. Include awareness stage, lead capture,
nurturing sequence, conversion mechanism, and retention strategy.
Provide detailed steps, tools, and messaging for each stage.
```
Deeper version: `funnel`, `gtm-outbound-engine`.

## 8. Content Strategy for Growth

```
Act as a content strategist specializing in growth. Create a 30-day
content strategy for my business in [industry]. Focus on lead
generation, authority building, and audience engagement. Include content
types, topics, hooks, formats, and distribution channels. Ensure content
aligns with business goals.
```
Deeper version: `content-strategy`.

## 9. Viral Hook Generation

```
Act as a viral content expert. Generate 25 high-converting hooks for my
business in [industry] targeting [audience]. Each hook should be
attention-grabbing, curiosity-driven, and aligned with my niche.
Categorize them into emotional, controversial, curiosity-based, and
value-driven hooks.
```
Deeper version: `hook-writer`, `hook`.

## 10. Lead Generation System

```
Act as a growth hacker. Design a scalable lead generation system for my
business: [describe business]. Include organic and paid strategies,
tools, automation workflows, and outreach scripts. Focus on high ROI
channels.
```
Deeper version: `gtm-outbound-engine`, `lead-intelligence`, `lead-scraper`.

## 11. Pricing Strategy Optimization

```
Act as a pricing strategist. Analyze my current pricing: [describe
pricing]. Suggest optimized pricing strategies including tiered pricing,
anchoring, bundling, and psychological pricing. Explain how each
improves conversions and revenue.
```
Deeper version: `pricing`, `pricing-strategy`.

## 12. Conversion Rate Optimization

```
Act as a CRO expert. Analyze my landing page or sales process: [describe
process or paste]. Identify friction points, weak messaging, and missed
opportunities. Suggest specific improvements to increase conversions.
```
Deeper version: `cro`, `landing-page-cro-expert`.

## 13. Customer Retention and LTV Growth

```
Act as a retention strategist. Suggest strategies to increase customer
lifetime value for my business: [describe business]. Include upsells,
cross-sells, loyalty systems, and engagement tactics. Provide
implementation steps.
```
Deeper version: `churn-prevention`, `retention`.

## 14. Brand Positioning

```
Act as a brand strategist. Define a strong brand positioning for my
business in [industry]. Include brand identity, tone, messaging pillars,
and unique selling points. Ensure differentiation in a crowded market.
```
Deeper version: `positioning`, `brand-guidelines`.

## 15. Growth Experimentation

```
Act as a growth scientist. Suggest 15 growth experiments I can run for
my business in [industry]. For each experiment, include hypothesis,
execution steps, success metrics, and expected outcomes.
```
Deeper version: `growth-operating-framework`, `growth-strategist`.

## 16. Go-To-Market Strategy

```
Act as a GTM expert. Create a go-to-market strategy for launching my

```
Deeper version: `gtm`, `gtm-strategy`, `launch`.


## 18. Email Marketing System

```
Act as an email marketing strategist. Design a complete email marketing
system for my business. Include welcome sequence, nurturing sequence,
sales emails, and re-engagement campaigns. Provide sample email
frameworks.
```
Deeper version: `emails`, `email-ops`.

## 19. Automation and AI Integration

```
Act as an AI automation consultant. Suggest ways to automate key
processes in my business: [describe business]. Identify workflows that
can be automated using AI tools and explain implementation.
```
Deeper version: `ai-automation-blueprints`, `automation-blueprint`.

## 20. Scaling Strategy

```
Act as a scaling strategist. My business is currently at [stage/
revenue]. Create a roadmap to scale it to the next level. Include team
structure, systems, processes, and growth levers. Provide a step-by-step
plan.
```
Deeper version: `one-person-business-scaling`, `growth-operating-framework`.

## How to use this kit

1. Pick the prompt matching the job, fill in every bracket — a prompt run
   with a bracket left in place produces generic output.
2. If the answer needs iteration, structure, or real data (not just a
   one-shot opinion), switch to the cross-referenced skill instead of
   re-running the prompt with more adjectives.
3. These are independent, single-purpose prompts, not a sequential
   pipeline — running #1 doesn't require running #2-20 first.
