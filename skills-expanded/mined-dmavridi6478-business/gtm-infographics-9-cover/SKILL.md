## How to use it

The three-mode idea (Hustle, Focus, Expansion) is a reasonable frame: keep pricing simple and flip it only with evidence early, narrow to one customer profile before widening. It does not replace customer interviews. For full detail get the author's own graphics; related skills here: `gtm-strategy`, `saas-founder-led-gtm` and `gtm-5-stages-data-layer`. Use `/gtm-stage`.
