# 7 photoshoot prompts (upload your own photo)

Quoted from the slides. Fill the [BRACKETS]. Run `/photoshoot <1-7>` to get the filled-in prompt.

1. **DSLR Photoshoot Mode.** Transform my uploaded photo into a professional DSLR photoshoot. Keep my identity, facial structure, skin tone, and natural features same without any alteration. Improve the composition, lighting, depth of field, pose, background, wardrobe, and overall atmosphere while keeping everything physically realistic. Make it look like a professional photographer actually captured the image using a high-end camera and portrait lens. Avoid plastic skin, excessive retouching, distorted features, or an obvious AI-generated appearance.
2. **Change Your Location.** Use my uploaded photo as the identity reference and place me naturally in [LOCATION]. Don't simply replace the background. Reconstruct the entire photograph so my lighting, shadows, perspective, reflections, clothing, depth, and color temperature naturally match the environment. Make it look as though I genuinely traveled to this location for a professional photoshoot.
3. **Luxury Photoshoot Mode.** Transform my uploaded photo into a luxury editorial photoshoot. Give me a sophisticated [OUTFIT], elegant [LOCATION], flattering professional lighting, realistic skin texture, cinematic depth, premium composition, and subtle high-end color grading. Keep my face and identity recognizable. The final result should look expensive, sophisticated, and believable rather than overly edited.
4. **Fashion Magazine Transformation.** Transform my uploaded photo into a high-end fashion editorial photograph. Keep my actual identity and facial characteristics intact. Give me a professional model-like pose, [FASHION STYLE], sophisticated lighting, controlled shadows, realistic skin texture, carefully composed framing, and editorial-quality styling. Make the photograph look like it was captured by a professional fashion photographer for a premium magazine.
5. **Fix an Ordinary Photo.** Take my uploaded ordinary photo and turn it into an extraordinary professional photograph. Keep my face and body same without any alteration. Identify the weaknesses in the original image, including lighting, framing, background, pose, clothing, distractions, and composition, then correct them through a realistic photoshoot transformation. Preserve my recognizable features and natural appearance. The final image should look dramatically better but still believable.
6. **Travel Influencer Photoshoot.** Turn my uploaded photo into an authentic professional travel photograph taken in [DESTINATION]. Give me a natural, confident pose and appropriate [OUTFIT]. Match the sunlight, shadows, weather, environment, perspective, and color temperature to the location. Use realistic depth of field and professional travel photography composition. Make it feel spontaneous but professionally captured.
7. **Social Media Profile Photoshoot.** Transform my uploaded photo into a professional personal-brand photoshoot for my [INSTAGRAM/LINKEDIN] profile. Create a polished but natural appearance that communicates [PERSONALITY/PROFESSION]. Improve the pose, wardrobe, lighting, background, framing, facial expression, and composition. Make it look professionally photographed while keeping my appearance authentic and recognizable.

## Use honestly
- Only edit photos of yourself, or of people who agreed. Do not use #2 or #6 to fake a trip or a credential you will present as real (a travel image on a dating profile, a "luxury" shot to support a sales claim).
- For a LinkedIn profile photo (#7) keep it a true likeness: recruiters and clients meet the real person. Many platforms require labelling AI-edited images; check the platform rule.
- Upload the photo to a tool whose privacy terms you accept. Faces are biometric data in the EU (GDPR), so do not upload other people's photos without consent.
