# 20 sales methodologies - pick by situation

Source: SalesDaily.co one-page infographic. The slide gives one line per method, "when to use it" and "best for". It does not give the steps; where this skill adds steps it says so. Run `/sales-method <your situation>` to get a ranked pick.

| Method | What it is (slide) | When to use it | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPIN Selling | Question-based method that surfaces real buyer pain | You need to guide discovery through logic and insight | SaaS, agencies, solution-led sellers |
| SNAP Selling | Speed-based approach for distracted buyers | Buyers are overwhelmed and decisions stall from friction | Inside sales, high-velocity SDR/AE teams |
| GAP Selling | Focuses on the gap between current state and desired outcome | You are selling ROI, outcomes or transformation | SaaS, agencies, solution-led sellers |
| SPICED Framework | Uncovers urgency and qualification through 6 buyer-focused lenses | Early discovery, to surface impact and timing | SaaS, outbound, multi-threaded deals |
| MEDDIC | Qualification system for complex sales with many stakeholders | Deal risk is high and misalignment kills forecasts | Enterprise AEs and deal desk teams |
| MEDDPICC | MEDDIC plus Paper Process and Competition for deeper control | Legal, security or red-tape blocks velocity | Late-stage pipeline and large-deal operators |
| CHAMP | Qualifies on challenge and authority before budget | Budget is unclear but urgency can be created | SDRs, outbound AEs, early-stage discovery |
| NEAT Selling | Business pain and economic value | You need to tie discovery directly to impact | SaaS reps handling large-volume outreach |
| Sandler Selling System | Psychology and control techniques to lead the process | Buyers hold power and pressure needs to be reversed | High-ticket closers, services, complex solution sales |
| Challenger Sale | Reframes buyer thinking with tailored insight and control | Breaking status quo and positioning disruptive ideas | Enterprise teams, competitive markets |
| Insight Selling | Leads buyers with tailored insight to help define their needs | Buyers are unsure what to prioritise or how to decide | Advisory-style sales, consultants, B2B SaaS |
| Command of the Sale | Process-control system to drive deal momentum | Too many deals stall or go dark after demo | Teams that need discipline and follow-through |
| Strategic Selling | Maps influence, politics and power in multi-threaded deals | More than 4 stakeholders are involved in the decision | Enterprise sellers, key account managers |
| Target Account Selling | Coordinates cross-functional effort toward named high-value accounts | Deals need personalisation, orchestration and patience | ABM teams, enterprise AEs, field sales |
| Winning by Design | Scalable system around recurring revenue and data-driven sales | You are building repeatable, scientific sales workflows | SDRs, outbound AEs, early-stage discovery |
| Buyer Facilitator Framework | Helps the buyer buy by reducing internal friction and confusion | Deals stall due to buyer-side complexity or fear | Customer-centric AEs, consultants, B2B advisory sales |
| Sales Acceleration Formula | Metrics-driven system for scalable sales hiring and process | You are building a team from zero or scaling inside sales | Founders, VPs of Sales, RevOps |
| Buyer-Centric Selling | Builds trust by aligning the sales process to buyer psychology | Consultative value matters more than pitch volume | Long-cycle sales, renewals, retention-led orgs |
| The Science of Selling | Uses behavioural science to influence how buyers decide | Logic alone is not enough and emotional trust matters | Reps selling abstract, risky or unfamiliar solutions |
| Value Selling Framework | Links solution capabilities to measurable business outcomes | You are defending price or selling premium positioning | Competitive SaaS, MarTech, FinTech, B2B tech vendors |

Two rows on the slide look duplicated or transposed (CHAMP and Winning by Design share a "best for", NEAT's "best for" reads like an outbound role rather than a pain-led method). Treat the "best for" column as the author's opinion, not a rule.

## How to choose (judgement, not from the slide)

1. **Stage first.** Early discovery: SPIN, SPICED, GAP, CHAMP, NEAT. Qualification and forecasting: MEDDIC, MEDDPICC. Stalled mid-funnel: Command of the Sale, Buyer Facilitator. Late-stage paperwork: MEDDPICC.
2. **Stakeholder count second.** More than 4 stakeholders: Strategic Selling or MEDDIC. Named accounts: Target Account Selling.
3. **Buyer state third.** Distracted: SNAP. Unsure what to prioritise: Insight Selling or Challenger. Holding all the power: Sandler.
4. **Do not stack more than two.** One for discovery, one for qualification is the realistic ceiling for a team.
