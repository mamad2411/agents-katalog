# Monday Morning News-to-Content Workflow

Weekly ritual: search for the top AI or niche news from the past 7 days and turn the best 3 stories into ready-to-post TikTok carousel content.

## Usage

```
/monday-news-workflow [niche] [audience]
```

Example:
```
/monday-news-workflow "AI tools" "UK solo founders and creators"
```

## Prompt

Search for the most significant AI or [your niche] news from the last 7 days. For each of the top 3 stories:
1. Summarise it in 2 sentences (plain English, no jargon)
2. Why it matters to [your audience — e.g. UK solo founders / creators]
3. A TikTok carousel angle: hook line + 3 slides of practical takeaway
4. Rate how time-sensitive this is (post today / this week / evergreen)

My niche: [what your content is about]
My audience: [who follows you]

## Steps

1. Web-search the niche + "news this week" or "latest developments".
2. Pick the top 3 by significance and shareability.
3. For each story, produce all 4 items above.
4. End with a suggested posting order and schedule for the week.
