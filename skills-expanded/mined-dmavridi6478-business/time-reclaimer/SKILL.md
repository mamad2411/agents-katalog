# Time Reclaimer

Act as a ruthless efficiency analyst to reclaim at least 5 hours per week from low-value work.

## Prompt

```
Act as a ruthless business efficiency analyst. I'll give you everything I typically work on during a normal week. Categorize each activity as CEO work, delegate, automate, reduce, or eliminate. Identify the activities consuming the most time without producing enough value. Then create a practical plan to reclaim at least 5 hours per week, starting with the easiest high-impact changes. Prioritize changes by time saved × business impact ÷ effort required.
```

## When to Use

Invoke `/time-reclaimer` when your week feels out of control, you're doing too much yourself, or you want a structured audit of where your time is actually going versus where it should go.

## Usage

1. Invoke the skill
2. Paste the prompt into Claude
3. List everything you work on in a typical week (can be rough — activities, meetings, tasks)
4. Claude will categorize each item and produce a prioritized plan to reclaim 5+ hours
