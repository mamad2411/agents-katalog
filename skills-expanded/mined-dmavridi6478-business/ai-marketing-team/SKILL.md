# AI Marketing Team — 10 Role Prompts

**Invoke:** `/ai-marketing-team`

Source: Anna Bilan — 10 prompts replacing core marketing functions (~$8,300/month value)

## The 10 Marketing Roles

### Role 1 — Content Strategist ($1,200/month)
Create a 30-day content calendar for [industry]. Include topics, hooks, formats, and posting times. Focus on [specific goal]. Flag any gaps.

### Role 2 — Copywriter ($1,500/month)
Write 5 LinkedIn posts about [topic] for [target audience]. Use a direct, conversational tone. Each post needs a scroll-stopping hook and clear CTA.

### Role 3 — Email Marketer ($800/month)
Write a 5-email nurture sequence for [product/service]. Goal: move cold leads to booked calls. Include subject lines, preview text, and send timing.

### Role 4 — SEO Specialist ($1,100/month)
Analyze [competitor URL]. List their top 10 ranking keywords, content gaps I can exploit, and 5 blog topics that could outrank them.

### Role 5 — Ad Creative ($900/month)
Write 10 Facebook ad variations for [offer]. Include 3 hooks, 3 body copy options, and 4 CTAs. Target: [audience]. Goal: [conversion type].

### Role 6 — Landing Page Writer ($700/month)
Write landing page copy for [product]. Include headline, subhead, 3 benefit sections, social proof placement, and 2 CTA variations.

### Role 7 — Brand Strategist ($600/month)
Define brand voice guidelines for [company]. Include tone attributes, words to use, words to avoid, and 3 example sentences for social, email, and website.

### Role 8 — Market Researcher ($500/month)
Research [industry] trends for 2026. Include 5 opportunities, 3 threats, top competitors, and what customers complain about in reviews.

### Role 9 — Social Media Manager ($800/month)
Repurpose this [blog post/video transcript] into 10 social posts. 3 for LinkedIn, 3 for X, 2 for Instagram captions, 2 for email snippets.

### Role 10 — Analytics Translator ($400/month)
Here's my marketing data [paste metrics]. Tell me what's working, what's failing, and 3 specific changes to make this week. Skip the fluff.

## Recommended Role Chains

- **New product launch:** 8 (Research) → 7 (Brand) → 1 (Content) → 2 (Copy) → 6 (Landing Page) → 5 (Ads)
- **Content engine:** 1 (Content) → 2 (Copy) → 9 (Social) → 10 (Analytics)
- **Demand generation:** 4 (SEO) → 1 (Content) → 3 (Email) → 5 (Ads) → 10 (Analytics)

## Usage

When invoked:
1. Ask which role(s) are needed, or suggest a chain based on the goal
2. Identify and ask for all [bracketed variables] in the selected prompt
3. Execute the role fully with the filled-in context
4. Deliver the complete output (calendar, emails, copy, etc.)
5. Offer the next role in the logical chain
