# Life Purpose Path

Analyze a birth date to propose 3 life paths aligned with deep personality needs and growth environments.

## Prompt

```
Analyze my birth date [DD/MM/YYYY] from a personality and self-knowledge perspective.

Don't just tell me what profession I should choose.

Identify what kinds of problems I might enjoy solving, what environments might help me grow, what needs I need to satisfy, and what might make me feel empty even if I'm seemingly doing well.

Then propose 3 different life paths that might suit me and explain why; what they would bring me; what I would sacrifice; what their biggest risk would be; how I could try them out in real life.
```

## When to Use

Invoke `/life-purpose-path` when exploring major life or career direction, wanting to understand what environments and problem types fit you, or when a path feels successful on the outside but empty on the inside. Replace `[DD/MM/YYYY]` with your birth date.

## Usage

1. Invoke the skill
2. Paste the prompt into Claude, replacing `[DD/MM/YYYY]` with your actual date of birth
3. Claude will produce 3 life path options with what each brings, costs, risks, and how to test each in real life
