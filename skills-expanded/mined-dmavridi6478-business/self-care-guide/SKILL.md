# Self-Care Guide

Generate a personalized self-care plan from a birth date — daily, weekly, monthly, and crisis protocol.

## Prompt

```
Use my birth date [DD/MM/YYYY] to create a personalized self-care plan.

I don't want generic advice.

Analyze what I might need to feel grounded, regain energy, manage my emotions, maintain healthy boundaries, and feel like I'm moving forward.

Differentiate between:

what recharges me, what stabilizes me, what makes me grow.

Then create a daily, weekly, and monthly plan, plus a protocol for those times when I feel overwhelmed or losing my way.
```

## When to Use

Invoke `/self-care-guide` when you want a self-care plan tailored to your personality type rather than generic wellness advice. Replace `[DD/MM/YYYY]` with your birth date before sending.

## Usage

1. Invoke the skill
2. Paste the prompt into Claude, replacing `[DD/MM/YYYY]` with your actual date of birth
3. Claude will distinguish what recharges vs stabilizes vs grows you, then produce daily/weekly/monthly plans + a crisis protocol
