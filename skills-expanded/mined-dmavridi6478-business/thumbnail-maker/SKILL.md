# Thumbnail Maker

Source: @the.wealth.lab "6 Favorite Claude Skills That Automate Almost Anything."

> Generate high-CTR thumbnail concepts, layouts, visual directions, facial expressions, color combinations, and design prompts optimized to grab attention.

## What this skill produces

For any video topic, this skill outputs:
- **3 concept directions** (safe/proven, bold/contrarian, curiosity gap) — not just one option
- **Layout spec** for each: what goes left, center, right; text placement; image crop guidance
- **Color palette** per concept: 2–3 hex codes, contrast rationale
- **Text overlay copy**: headline (5 words max), optional sub-text
- **Facial expression / body language direction** (if the creator appears on screen)
- **AI image generation prompts** for Midjourney / DALL-E / Firefly — ready to paste

## The CTR formula this skill applies

1. **Curiosity gap** — The thumbnail implies something the viewer doesn't know yet. Never fully reveal the answer.
2. **Visual contrast** — Thumbnail must pop against a white or dark YouTube background. High contrast wins.
3. **One focal point** — One face, one object, one text block. Never split attention three ways.
4. **Emotion over information** — A shocked face outperforms a stat graphic on most platforms.
5. **Text as amplifier** — Text adds context the image can't carry alone; never redundant with the title.

## Copy-paste prompt to use

```
You are a YouTube thumbnail strategist who has studied thousands of high-performing thumbnails.

My video topic: [DESCRIBE THE VIDEO IN ONE SENTENCE]
My channel niche: [E.G. personal finance, AI tools, fitness, productivity]
Do I appear on camera? [YES / NO — if yes, describe my look briefly]
Platform: [YouTube / Instagram Reels / TikTok]

Give me 3 thumbnail concepts for this video:

For each concept:
1. Concept name and the psychological trigger it uses (curiosity gap / shock / social proof / fear / aspiration)
2. Layout description: what goes left, center, right; background; text placement
3. Text overlay: headline (5 words max) + optional subtext
4. Color palette: 2–3 hex codes with contrast notes
5. Facial expression / body language (if I appear)
6. AI image prompt (Midjourney or DALL-E 3 style) I can paste directly

After the 3 concepts, tell me which one you'd bet on for highest CTR and why.
```

## What makes a thumbnail fail

- Too much text (more than 6 words total)
- Colors that blend into YouTube's white background
- No clear focal point — the eye doesn't know where to land
- Thumbnail matches the title exactly — no reason to click for more
- Generic stock-photo feel — nothing specific to this creator or video
