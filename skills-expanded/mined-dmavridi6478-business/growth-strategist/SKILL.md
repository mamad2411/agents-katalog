# Growth Strategist

Act as growth strategist to identify the 5 strongest revenue or retention opportunities and build a 30-day action plan for the top one.

## Prompt

```
Act as my growth strategist. Analyze my business, target audience, products or services, current marketing, customer problems, and available resources. Identify my 5 strongest opportunities to increase revenue, attract customers, or improve retention without significantly increasing my workload. Rank each opportunity by potential impact, ease of implementation, cost, and time to results. Recommend the one opportunity I should pursue first and give me a simple 30-day action plan.
```

## When to Use

Invoke `/growth-strategist` when you want a structured analysis of your growth levers rather than adding more effort, or when you need to prioritize between several possible growth directions.

## Usage

1. Invoke the skill
2. Paste the prompt into Claude
3. Describe your business, audience, products/services, current marketing, and available resources
4. Claude will identify 5 ranked opportunities and produce a 30-day action plan for the top one
