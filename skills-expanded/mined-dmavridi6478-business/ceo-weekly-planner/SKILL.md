# CEO Weekly Planner

Act as chief of staff to prioritize the week and build a day-by-day action plan.

## Prompt

```
Act as my chief of staff. I'll give you my current projects, deadlines, meetings, problems, and open decisions. Ask only the questions you need before analyzing them. Identify the 3 priorities that deserve my attention this week. For each, explain why it matters, what I should personally own, what I should delegate, automate, reduce, or stop doing. Then create a simple Monday–Friday action plan with clear outcomes for each day.
```

## When to Use

Invoke `/ceo-weekly-planner` at the start of each week or whenever you have too many competing priorities and need a structured weekly plan with clear ownership and delegation decisions.

## Usage

1. Invoke the skill
2. Paste the prompt into Claude
3. Share your current projects, deadlines, meetings, problems, and open decisions
4. Claude will ask clarifying questions, then output your 3 top priorities + Mon–Fri action plan
