# Claude Writing Prompts

Four prompts from @jessiekimbooks "Claude, Uncomplicated" series for book authors. Each targets a distinct stage of writing a non-fiction book.

## Prompt 1 — Before You Outline: Find the real problem

> Research what people say about [topic] on Reddit and in reviews. Give me the 10 complaints that repeat most — in their words.

Their words become your chapter titles.

## Prompt 2 — Structure: Make chapters earn it

> Build an outline where each chapter changes one belief. Then cut the chapters that don't earn their place.

The second sentence is the whole trick.

## Prompt 3 — Voice

> Extract the rules of my voice from these 500 words. Then write Chapter 1 following them.

## Prompt 4 — Editing

> Don't improve this chapter. Show me what to cut.

## When to use which

| Stage | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Before outlining | 1 — Find the real problem |
| Outlining | 2 — Make chapters earn it |
| Drafting | 3 — Voice |
| Revision | 4 — Editing |
