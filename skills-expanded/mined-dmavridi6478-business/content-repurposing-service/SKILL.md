## The core positioning

**Don't sell AI posts. Sell a content system.** The sellable unit isn't a post — it's a repeatable weekly service built on the client's own real expertise, not generic AI filler. A literal, named bundle (the "7-asset pack") is what makes a weekly service buyable instead of an abstract promise.

## The 5-step pipeline

1. **Record one real idea** — Start from a call, voice note, lesson, or interview where the client explains something they actually know. Capture real expertise, keep the original recording, mark any private sections before processing.
2. **Find the strong moments** — Use AI to locate surprising claims, useful explanations, stories, and objections in the source material. Not every sentence deserves a post: look for a specific insight + a clear audience problem + a complete thought.
3. **Build for each format** — Turn one idea into a carousel, short-video outline, email, and text post *without* copying the same words across formats. One promise per asset, native structure per platform, different depth per format — repurpose the idea, not the formatting.
4. **Restore the human** — Edit out generic AI phrasing, invented claims, and fake certainty until the draft sounds like the source person. Use their vocabulary, keep their real examples, remove any claim they didn't actually make. AI should not erase the reason people follow the client in the first place.
5. **Deliver the 7-asset pack** — 1 carousel + 2 short-video outlines + 1 email + 3 text posts, each with source links back to the original recording and a clear approval status. One review round; the client approves every claim; explicitly define who publishes and schedules.

## Weekly cadence

| Day | Step |
|---|---|
| Monday | Record |
| Tuesday | Extract (find strong moments) |
| Wednesday | Draft (build for each format + restore the human) |
| Thursday | Approve (client reviews, approves every claim) |
| Friday | Schedule — only if publishing is actually in scope |

The client is buying seven consistent assets from real expertise every week — not a folder of generic AI filler.

## Notes

- Source: an "AI Income Playbook" screenshot carousel (@zhiprompts). Treat the specific cadence (Mon–Fri) and the "7 assets" figure as a customizable template, not a fixed requirement — adapt scope, review rounds, and pricing to the actual client engagement.
- The core discipline that makes this defensible against "just use ChatGPT yourself" is steps 1 and 4: real recorded expertise in, and a human-authenticity edit pass out. Skipping either turns it back into generic AI content, which is the thing this whole service is positioned against.
