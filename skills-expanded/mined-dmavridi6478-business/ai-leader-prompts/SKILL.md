# AI Prompts for Top 1% Leaders

**Invoke:** `/ai-leader-prompts`

Source: AIForLeaders.com — 25 prompts across 5 leadership categories

## Category 01 — Strategic Thinking & Vision

01. Which three strategic risks are we underweighting or ignoring?
02. How would a well-funded rival disrupt us in two years?
03. Which decisions could look wrong in five years?
04. Which assumptions need testing, and how would we validate them?
05. How could we achieve three-year goals in 18 months?

## Category 02 — Decision Making Under Pressure

06. If this decision fails, why? How can I reduce that risk?
07. What would I choose if the decision were irreversible — or easy to undo?
08. What is the strongest argument against my preferred direction?
09. With incomplete information, which questions must I answer or can I skip?
10. What would I choose if I had to explain it publicly to my team?

## Category 03 — Team Development & Coaching

11. What five root causes could explain this person's struggle?
12. How can I give direct feedback while protecting trust and confidence?
13. Which one-to-one questions would reveal what is really happening?
14. Why might a high performer disengage, and how should I discuss it?
15. Design a 30-day plan to improve this person's specific skill.

## Category 04 — Communication & Influence

16. Rewrite this for a sceptical board member. What objections will they have?
17. Help me deliver difficult news honestly, clearly and with trust.
18. Explain this complex strategy so everyone understands why it matters.
19. I have five minutes with executives. What three things must I communicate?
20. How can I refuse this request firmly while preserving the relationship?

## Category 05 — Execution & Accountability

21. Which five causes of delay could explain this project's slippage?
22. What should I stop doing so my team can execute without bottlenecks?
23. How could this strategy fail, and which checkpoints would catch it early?
24. Which early warning signs should I watch weekly?
25. Which two or three priorities matter most, and what should we pause?

## Usage

When invoked:
1. Ask which category: Strategic / Decision / Team / Communication / Execution
2. Ask for the specific context (the decision, person, project, or situation)
3. Apply the relevant prompt(s) with the user's context filled in
4. Deliver structured analysis
5. Offer to run adjacent prompts from the same category or the next logical category

For a full leadership audit, run all 5 categories sequentially.
