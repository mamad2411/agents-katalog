# Decision Partner

Act as executive decision partner to challenge assumptions, compare options, and recommend a course of action.

## Prompt

```
Act as my executive decision partner. I'm considering this decision: [INSERT DECISION]. First ask about my goals, constraints, alternatives, timeline, financial impact, and concerns. Challenge my assumptions and biases. Compare the realistic options, including their upside, downside, risks, opportunity costs, and second-order consequences. Then recommend the option you would choose and explain why. Finally, tell me what new information would change your recommendation.
```

## When to Use

Invoke `/decision-partner` before any significant business decision — hiring, investment, strategy pivot, pricing change — to get adversarial analysis and a clear recommendation before committing.

## Usage

1. Invoke the skill
2. Paste the prompt into Claude, replacing `[INSERT DECISION]` with your actual decision
3. Claude will interview you about goals, constraints, and alternatives
4. You receive a comparison matrix, a recommendation, and the conditions that would change it
