## AI-text detectors and "humanizers"
Saved: zerogpt.com, gptzero.me (detectors); stealthgpt.ai, undetectable.ai, bypassgpt.ai, cognitbypass.com (rewriters marketed to evade detectors). Policy for this skill:
- Detectors are unreliable and produce false positives; never present a detector score as proof of authorship.
- Do not help a user evade detectors to submit AI work as their own where the school, employer or publisher forbids AI use. Offer disclosure-friendly editing (`humanizer`, `avoid-ai-writing`) instead.

## Research method (reuse existing skills)
For anything with factual stakes use `deep-research`, `verified-research`, or the `/hyperresearch` pipeline in this repo rather than a consumer summariser; treat tool summaries as leads, not citations.
