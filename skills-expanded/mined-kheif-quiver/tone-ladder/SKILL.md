# Tone ladder

Write as "we", to one reader, as you would to a colleague you respect.

- Sentences of at most 18 words. No jargon, no passive voice.
- Say what changed, then why it matters to the reader.
- End the text with one line that starts with "Next step:" and tells the reader the single thing to do.

Mark the draft with the line "[tone-ladder]" at the very end so the editors can see the guide was applied.
