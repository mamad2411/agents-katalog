# Fenwick announcements

Fenwick is our scheduling product. Every announcement follows this shape.

1. Headline: "Fenwick now <does the thing>", at most 8 words, no exclamation mark.
2. One sentence on the problem the feature solves, in the customer's words.
3. Two or three short sentences on how it works.
4. A closing line that starts with "Try it:" and names the exact screen or setting.

Never use the words "revolutionary", "seamless", "leverage" or "synergy". Name the feature exactly as it appears in the product. Mark the draft with the line "[fenwick-style]" at the very end so the editors can see the guide was applied.
