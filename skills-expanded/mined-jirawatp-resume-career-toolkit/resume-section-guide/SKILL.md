# Resume Section Guide

Build each resume section to serve its purpose for the target role and career stage.

## Professional Summary

**When to include:** Career changers (to explain the pivot), senior professionals (to distill a long career), people with non-obvious fit (to frame relevance). Skip if you are early career with a straightforward background.

**Formula:** "[Role] with [X years] in [domain]. [Top achievement with a number]. Skilled in [3-5 relevant keywords]."

**Length:** 2-4 sentences. No more.

**Never write:** "Results-oriented professional seeking challenging opportunities" (says nothing specific).

## Skills Section

**Placement:** After summary, before experience, so ATS scans it early.

**Organization:** Group by category for technical roles (Languages, Frameworks, Tools, Cloud). Use a flat comma-separated list for non-technical roles.

**Include:** Skills you can discuss in an interview. Use exact phrasing from target job postings.

**Exclude:** Microsoft Office (assumed), "soft skills" as a list (demonstrate these in bullets instead), anything you cannot actually do.

## Experience Section

**Bullet count by recency:** 4-6 for current/recent role, 3-4 for previous role, 2-3 for older roles. Cut ruthlessly for relevance.

**Each bullet:** Start with a strong verb, include at least one number, show result or scale.

**Date format:** Be consistent. "Jan 2020 - Present" or "01/2020 - Present". Never mix formats.

**Multiple roles at one company:** List each role separately under the company name to show progression.

## Education Section

**Early career:** Include GPA (if above 3.5), relevant coursework, academic projects, honors.
**Mid-career:** Degree, school, year. Drop GPA and coursework.
**Senior:** Degree and school only. May drop graduation year to avoid age bias.

## Additional Sections

**Projects:** Include if you are junior, changing careers, or the role values hands-on work. Format: name, tech stack, link, one-line description, key metric.

**Certifications:** Include if the job posting mentions them. List certification name, issuing body, and year.

**Languages:** Include only if relevant to the role or above conversational level. State proficiency clearly: Native, Professional, Conversational.

**Volunteer:** Include if it fills a gap, demonstrates leadership, or is directly relevant to the target role.

## Section Order by Situation

**Standard:** Contact, Summary, Skills, Experience, Education
**Technical:** Contact, Skills, Experience, Projects, Education
**New graduate:** Contact, Education, Skills, Internships/Projects
**Executive:** Contact, Executive Profile, Career Highlights, Experience, Board Roles, Education
**Career changer:** Contact, Summary (pivot narrative), Skills (transferable), Experience (reframed), Bridge Experience, Education
