# Resume Tracker

Keep a single source of truth and track every tailored version.

## Master Resume

Maintain one comprehensive document containing every experience, every bullet you have ever written, every skill, and every achievement. This is never sent to employers. It is your library to pull from.

**Master resume structure:**
- All contact information
- Multiple summary versions (one per target role type)
- Complete skills list by category
- Every role with every bullet (even ones you usually cut)
- All education, certifications, projects, volunteer work

**Update the master:** Immediately when you complete a major project, earn a certification, finish a role, or hit a significant metric. At minimum, review quarterly.

## Tailored Version Workflow

1. Copy the master to a new file (never edit the master for an application)
2. Analyze the target job posting
3. Select the right summary version
4. Choose the most relevant bullets for each role
5. Reorder skills to match the posting
6. Add any missing keywords that truthfully apply
7. Trim to 1-2 pages
8. Save with a clear filename

## File Naming Convention

`LastnameFirstname_Role_Company_YYYYMM.pdf`

Examples:
- PattanayutachotJirawat_SeniorSWE_Google_202604.pdf
- PattanayutachotJirawat_DevOps_Stripe_202604.pdf
- PattanayutachotJirawat_Master_Resume.docx (never sent)

## Application Tracker

Maintain a simple spreadsheet:

| Company | Role | Resume Version | Cover Letter | Date Applied | Method | Status | Notes |
|---------|------|---------------|-------------|-------------|--------|--------|-------|
| Google | Senior SWE | SeniorSWE_Google | Yes | 2026-04-01 | Referral | Phone screen 4/15 | Referred by Alex |

## Version Categories

Organize tailored versions into folders by target role type:
- Engineering/
- Product/
- Leadership/
- General/

Within each category, you may only need small adjustments per company rather than full rewrites.
