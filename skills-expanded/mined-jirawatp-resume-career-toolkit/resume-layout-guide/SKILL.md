# Resume Layout Guide

Format a resume that passes ATS parsing and is easy for humans to scan in 6 seconds.

## Document Setup

**Page count:** 1 page for under 10 years experience, 2 pages for 10+, never 3 unless executive or academic CV.

**Margins:** 0.5 to 0.75 inches on all sides. Smaller margins give more space; never go below 0.5 inches.

**Fonts:** Use one of these ATS-safe fonts at 10-12pt for body text and 14-16pt for your name: Arial, Calibri, Georgia, Garamond, Helvetica, Times New Roman.

**Line spacing:** 1.0 to 1.15 for body text. Add 6-12pt of space between sections.

## ATS Rules

**Required:**
- Single-column layout
- Contact info in document body (not in header/footer)
- Standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills, Summary)
- Simple bullet characters (round bullet, hyphen, or asterisk)
- Consistent date format throughout (Jan 2020 - Present)
- Text-based PDF or .docx format

**Forbidden:**
- Tables used for layout
- Text boxes
- Multi-column designs
- Images, icons, or graphics
- Skill bars or rating scales
- Content in headers or footers
- Non-standard or decorative fonts

## Visual Hierarchy

Create a scannable structure where the eye naturally flows:
1. **Name** -- largest text on the page
2. **Section headings** -- uppercase or bold, clearly separated
3. **Job titles and company names** -- bold, easy to locate
4. **Dates** -- right-aligned or on the same line as the role, consistent
5. **Bullet points** -- the detail layer, indented under each role

## Section Order

**Standard:** Contact, Summary, Skills, Experience, Education, Additional
**Technical roles:** Contact, Skills (prioritized), Experience, Projects, Education
**Recent graduates:** Contact, Education (prioritized), Skills, Internships, Projects
**Career changers:** Contact, Summary (explaining pivot), Skills, Experience, Bridge Experience, Education

## File Output

- Save as both .docx (for ATS) and .pdf (for direct sends)
- Name the file: FirstnameLastname_Resume.pdf
- Keep file size under 2 MB
