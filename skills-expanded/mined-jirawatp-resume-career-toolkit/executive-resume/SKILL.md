# Executive Resume

Write a resume that speaks to boards, executive recruiters, and PE/VC partners.

## How Executive Resumes Differ

An executive resume does not list what you did. It communicates your leadership brand: what kind of leader you are, what transformations you drive, and what scale you operate at.

**Length:** 2-3 pages (acceptable at this level)
**Audience:** Executive recruiters, board members, CEOs, CHROs
**Tone:** Strategic, outcome-focused, transformation-oriented

## Structure

### Executive Profile (replaces Summary)

3-5 sentences establishing your leadership identity:
- Your function and operating level
- Scale (revenue, headcount, geography)
- Signature achievements (2-3 with numbers)
- Leadership philosophy in one line

### Career Highlights

A standalone section of 4-6 bullets representing your most significant career achievements, pulled from across all roles. These are your headline numbers.

### Professional Experience

For each role, include:
- Company name with context (revenue, headcount, stage, industry)
- Your reporting structure and span of control
- P&L or budget responsibility
- 4-6 bullets focused on transformation narratives: "Inherited [situation]. Drove [strategic change]. Achieved [result at scale]."

### Board and Advisory Roles
List separately with company, your role (Board Director, Advisor, Observer), and one line of context.

### Education and Executive Development
Degrees, executive programs (Harvard AMP, Stanford SEP, etc.), board certifications.

## Executive Language

Use strategic verbs: Spearheaded, Orchestrated, Championed, Architected, Steered, Transformed, Scaled

Avoid tactical verbs: Managed, Handled, Assisted, Helped, Worked on

Every bullet should answer "so what?" at the organizational level. Not "improved the process" but "redesigned operations, improving EBITDA margins from 12% to 28% and positioning the company for a $400M exit."

## Confidentiality

When current or former employer data is sensitive:
- Use percentages instead of absolute numbers
- Use ranges for revenue and headcount
- Describe relative improvement without revealing proprietary figures
- Never name undisclosed acquisition targets or strategies
