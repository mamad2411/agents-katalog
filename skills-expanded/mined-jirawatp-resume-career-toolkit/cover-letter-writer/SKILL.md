# Cover Letter Writer

Write a cover letter that gives the hiring manager a reason to read the resume.

## What a Cover Letter Must Do

A cover letter has one job: explain why this specific person is a strong match for this specific role at this specific company. If you could swap in a different company name and it still reads fine, the letter is too generic.

## Structure (250-400 words, 3-4 paragraphs)

### Paragraph 1: The Hook (2-3 sentences)

Open with something specific: a company initiative you admire, a product you have used, a mutual connection, or a concrete achievement that maps directly to their top requirement. Never open with "I am writing to apply for..."

**Strong openers:**
- "[Company]'s recent expansion into [area] caught my attention because I spent the last 3 years building exactly that kind of [product/service] at [my company]."
- "After [mutual contact] described the challenges your [team] is tackling, I knew my experience with [relevant skill] would be directly applicable."
- "Last year I led a project that [achievement closely matching their top need]. When I saw this role, the overlap was immediate."

### Paragraph 2: The Evidence (3-4 sentences)

Connect your strongest relevant experience to their top requirement. Use one specific example with a metric. This is not a summary of your resume -- it is one targeted story that proves you can do the job.

### Paragraph 3: Additional Value or Gap Handling (3-4 sentences)

Either add a second relevant proof point, or proactively address a gap. If you are missing a listed requirement, acknowledge it briefly and pivot to your compensating strength. If there are no gaps, use this paragraph to show company-specific knowledge or cultural alignment.

### Paragraph 4: The Close (2-3 sentences)

Express genuine enthusiasm for something specific about the role or company. State a clear next step ("I would welcome the chance to discuss..."). Thank them.

## Tone Calibration

- **Startup:** Conversational, show initiative and adaptability
- **Enterprise:** Professional, emphasize process and scale
- **Creative agency:** Show personality, reference their work specifically
- **Finance/legal:** Formal, credential-forward

## Things to Avoid

- Opening with "I am writing to apply for..."
- Restating the resume line by line
- Generic flattery ("I admire your company's commitment to excellence")
- Apologizing for missing qualifications
- Closing with "I look forward to hearing from you" (passive)
- Exceeding 400 words

## Output Format

Provide the complete cover letter text, followed by:
- 2 alternative opening hooks
- Key talking points to prepare for interview based on what the letter claims
