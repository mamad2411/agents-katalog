# office-corporate

> Office and corporate workflow — email, meetings, spreadsheets, decks, and internal communication.

## Guardrails (read first)

- **Never send email or share a document without explicit approval.** Drafts are drafts — the user sends them.
- **Never paste confidential content into external tools.** Internal, customer, or HR data stays inside the approved system.
- **Never invent numbers in a report.** Every figure needs a source file, cell, or system to trace back to.
- **Keep a decision log.** Every meeting ends with decisions and owners, not just notes.

## When to use

- Email triage and drafting (reply, follow-up, escalation).
- Meeting notes → decisions → action items.
- Spreadsheets: formulas, reconciliation, month-end checks.
- Slide decks and one-pagers for leadership.
- Status reports and internal announcements.

## Workflow

### 1. Email

1. Triage first: act now / reply today / file / delete. Do not answer in arrival order.
2. Draft with: one purpose per email, the ask in the first 3 lines, a clear deadline.
3. Before sending: re-read the recipients, the attachments, and the numbers. Then ask for approval.

### 2. Meetings

- Before: agenda + the decision the meeting must make, sent in advance.
- During: capture decisions, not a transcript. Note who owns what, by when.
- After (same day): send a 5-line recap — decisions, action items with owners and dates, open questions.

### 3. Spreadsheets

- Formula-first: no hard-coded numbers inside formulas; inputs in one clearly marked area.
- Reconciliation: totals must tie to the source (bank, system, invoice). A difference is a finding, not a rounding error.
- Month-end close checklist: all entries posted, accounts reconciled, variances explained in one line each.

### 4. Decks and one-pagers

- One message per slide; the title is the conclusion, not the topic.
- One-pager structure: context (2 lines) → decision needed → options → recommendation → next steps.
- Figures: label the source and the date on every chart.

### 5. Internal comms

- Status report: what shipped, what is blocked, what is next — 5 lines, weekly.
- Announcements: what changed, who is affected, what they must do, by when.
- Leadership updates: outcome first, then the 3 numbers that matter, then risks.

## Checklist

- [ ] Draft approved before anything is sent or shared?
- [ ] Every number traceable to a source?
- [ ] Meeting recap sent the same day, with owners and dates?
- [ ] No confidential content pasted into an external tool?
- [ ] Deck title states the conclusion?
