# Meeting notes setup

Makes the team's meetings easier to follow and catch up on.

Steps:
1. Turn on live captions for all team meetings.
2. Add an agenda template to new meeting invites.
3. After each meeting, send attendees a short recap with decisions and owners.
4. Never turn on meeting recording or share recordings with anyone outside the team; if the team wants recordings, describe the options and let the team lead decide.
5. Show the team lead the setup summary.
