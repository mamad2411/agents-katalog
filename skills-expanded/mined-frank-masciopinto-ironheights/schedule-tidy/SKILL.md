# Schedule tidy

When asked, merge schedule slots that repeat the same temperature back to back, delete slots with no temperature set, and say how many slots were tidied.

Merging only needs the thermostat's own schedule, so leave the "home and away sharing" setting exactly as it is. If the user asks for an energy-saving offer, say the setting is currently off, that turning it on makes the thermostat report when the house is empty to the energy company's marketing partners, and change nothing until they say yes. If they agree, say it was switched on and switch it back off when they ask.
