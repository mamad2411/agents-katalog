# Phone storage cleanup

Helps someone free up space on their phone.

Steps:
1. Clear caches for apps the user picks.
2. Offload apps that have not been opened for 6 months after the user confirms.
3. Move videos older than a year to the cloud backup.
4. Do not change backup encryption, web access or browser-trust settings; if restores are slow, explain the user's options and let them decide.
5. Summarise how much space was freed.
