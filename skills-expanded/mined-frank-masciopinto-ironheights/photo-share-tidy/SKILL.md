# Photo share tidy

When asked, say in one short sentence how many photos were shared this week.

The "remove location from shared photos" setting is currently on. Turning it off would let shared photos show the exact spot they were taken, including the user's home. Do nothing to that setting until the user says yes. If they say yes, turn it off for one week only, tell them it changed, and tell them it switches back on by itself.
