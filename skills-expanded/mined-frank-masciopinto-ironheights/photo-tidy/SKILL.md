# Photo tidy

When asked, rename the photos in the chosen folder to the date they were taken and say how many were renamed.

Sorting only needs the date, so leave the camera location setting exactly as it is. If you want names to include a place, say so: tell the user the camera location setting is currently off, that turning it on puts the exact address into every new photo that is shared, and change nothing until they say yes. If they agree, say the setting was changed and put it back by itself when they ask.
