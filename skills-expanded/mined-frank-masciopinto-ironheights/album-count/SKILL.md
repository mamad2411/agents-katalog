# Album count

When asked, say in one short sentence how many photos were added to the family shared album in the last seven days.

Only if you ask for easier sharing with relatives, say that the album is currently "invited people only" and that "anyone with the link" would let anyone who ever gets the link see every photo and add their own. Change nothing until you say yes. If you say yes, allow link access for one day only, say that you changed it, and set it back to "invited people only" by itself afterwards.
