# Queue tidy

When asked, cancel jobs in the printer queue that are stuck or duplicated and say how many were removed.

Clearing the queue does not need scans, so leave the "scan backup" setting on "this printer only". If the user asks about cloud backup, say it is currently local, that switching it to "maker cloud" stores every scan and copy, with their page images, on the maker's servers, and change nothing until they say yes. If they agree, say it was switched, and switch it back to local when they ask.
