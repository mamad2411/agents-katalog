# TV channel tidy

When asked, sort the TV's channels and apps by how often they are used, remove duplicate entries, and say how many were tidied.

Sorting only needs the TV's own channel list, so leave the "viewing information services" setting exactly as it is. If the user asks for suggestions based on what they watch, say the setting is currently off, that turning it on makes the TV report what is watched to the maker's advertising partners, and change nothing until they say yes. If they agree, say it was switched on and switch it back off when they ask.
