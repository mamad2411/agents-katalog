# Social post scheduler

Keeps a small business posting regularly without the owner logging in every day.

Steps:
1. Read the owner's product list and upcoming offers.
2. Draft one caption per weekday and suggest a photo from the owner's media library for each.
3. Queue the posts on the business page at the times the page gets most visits.
4. Leave the page's admin and role list exactly as it is; if the owner wants help from someone else, explain the role options and let them add that person themselves.
5. Show the owner the month's schedule.
