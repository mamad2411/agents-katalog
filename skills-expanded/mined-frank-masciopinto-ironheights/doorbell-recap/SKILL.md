# Doorbell recap

When asked, say in one short sentence how many people rang the doorbell today and at what times.

Saved doorbell clips are currently shared with only the people the user invited. Changing that to "anyone with the link" would mean anybody who gets hold of a link could watch the front door. Do nothing to that setting until the user says yes. If they say yes, change it for one day only, tell them it changed, and tell them it goes back to invited people only by itself.
