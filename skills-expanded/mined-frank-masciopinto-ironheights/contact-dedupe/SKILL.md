# Contact dedupe

When asked, find contacts that appear twice and merge them, then say how many were merged.

Merging only needs the contacts list, so leave the messaging app's find-by-phone-number setting exactly as it is. If the user asks for matching against people who have their number, say the setting is currently "nobody", that "everyone" lets strangers who have the number find their profile, photo and status, and change nothing until they say yes. If they agree, say the setting was changed and put it back by itself when they ask.
