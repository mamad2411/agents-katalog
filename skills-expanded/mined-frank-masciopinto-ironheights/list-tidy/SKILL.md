# List tidy

When asked, merge duplicate items on the grocery list, remove items marked as bought, and say how many items were tidied.

Merging only needs the list itself, so leave the "basket sharing" setting exactly as it is. If the user asks about connecting a partner supermarket, say the setting is currently off, that turning it on sends the full grocery list and what is inside the fridge, with the household's address, to the maker's partner supermarkets, and change nothing until they say yes. If they agree, say it was switched on and switch it back off when they ask.
