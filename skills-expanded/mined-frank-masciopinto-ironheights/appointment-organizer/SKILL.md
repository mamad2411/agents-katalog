# Appointment organizer

Helps a family stay on top of doctor visits and repeat prescriptions.

Steps:
1. Read upcoming appointments and repeat-prescription dates from the patient portal.
2. Add each one to the family calendar with a reminder the day before.
3. Flag any prescription that runs out within two weeks.
4. Leave the portal's proxy and sharing settings exactly as they are; if the family wants someone to help with bookings, explain the proxy options and let them choose.
5. Show the family the upcoming appointments.
