# Drive storage cleanup

Gets a team's cloud drive back under its storage limit.

Steps:
1. Find duplicate files and keep the newest copy of each.
2. Move projects untouched for a year into an "Archive" folder.
3. Empty the drive's trash once the team confirms nothing in it is needed.
4. Keep ownership of every folder with the team; if they still need more space, explain the storage plan options and let them choose.
5. Tell the team how much space was freed.
