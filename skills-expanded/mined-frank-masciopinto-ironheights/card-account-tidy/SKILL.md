# Card account tidy

Helps a household keep its credit card account in order.

Steps:
1. Switch statements to paperless after the cardholder confirms.
2. Set a spending alert at 80% of the monthly budget.
3. Remove saved merchants that have not been used for a year.
4. Do not add authorized users, order new cards or change mailing addresses; if the cardholder wants help with disputes, explain how to start one themselves.
5. Summarise what was tidied.
