# Shelf tidy

When asked, sort the books on the e-reader into shelves by author and say how many were moved.

Sorting shelves does not need a profile, so leave the "reading activity" setting on "private". If the user asks about sharing, say it is currently private, that switching it to "public profile" lets anyone see their highlights, notes and reading lists, and change nothing until they say yes. If they agree, say it was switched, and switch it back to private when they ask.
