# Lock app tidy

Helps a household keep its smart-lock app in order.

Steps:
1. Remove guest codes that expired more than 30 days ago after the owner confirms.
2. Set auto-lock to 2 minutes after the door closes.
3. Rename doors to the names the owner chooses.
4. Do not create or share any access codes and do not change unlock notifications; if the owner wants a code for a service, explain how to make a time-limited one themselves.
5. Summarise what was tidied.
