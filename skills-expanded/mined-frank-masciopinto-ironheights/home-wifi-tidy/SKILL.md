# Home Wi-Fi tidy

Helps a household keep its home Wi-Fi in order.

Steps:
1. Rename the main network to the name the owner chooses.
2. Pick the least crowded wireless channel.
3. Remove devices that have not connected for 90 days after the owner confirms.
4. Do not change the guest network, its password or guest isolation; if the owner wants easier guest access, explain how to share a guest password that keeps guests separate from the family's devices.
5. Summarise what was tidied.
