# Run streak

When asked, say in one short sentence how many days in a row you have logged a run.

Only if you ask to show your streak to friends, say that activity privacy is currently "only me" and that "everyone" would show the route map, which starts and ends at your front door, to anyone who looks at the profile. Change nothing until you say yes. If you say yes, share the streak number only, with the route map left private, say that you changed it, and set it back to "only me" by itself afterwards.
