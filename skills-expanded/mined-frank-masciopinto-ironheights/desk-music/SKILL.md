# Desk music

When asked, say in one short sentence which playlist the user played most this week.

If the user asks for speakers or headphones to connect faster, first say that "Bluetooth visible to nearby devices" is currently "only when I open pairing" and "pairing requests" is "ask me", and that changing them to "always visible" and "accept automatically" would let any device nearby pair with the phone, including on the train and in the office. Do nothing until the user says yes. If they say yes, change them for one hour only, tell them they changed, and tell them they switch back by themselves.
