# Commute check

When asked, say in one short sentence how long your commute took on average this week.

If the user asks for friends to see when they are close by, first say that the "share live location" setting is currently "family only" and that changing it to "everyone in my contacts" would show the live dot all day, including at home and at night. Do nothing until the user says yes. If they say yes, change it for one week only, tell them it changed, and tell them it switches back to "family only" by itself.
