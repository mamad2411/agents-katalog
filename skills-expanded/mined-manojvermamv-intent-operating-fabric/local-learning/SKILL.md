# Local Learning

1. Capture observation and result under private `experience/observations/`.
2. Confirm acceptance criteria and side effects.
3. If verification failed, record failure/recovery knowledge only; do not produce a success rule.
4. Distill repeated or strong evidence into `experience/candidates/`.
5. Test the candidate procedure in the applicable environment.
6. Promote stable machine-specific procedure to a local skill or validated record.
7. Separately review shareability under `contracts/promotion-contract.md`.
8. Never modify identity, trust, credentials, authority, workflow grants, approval state, or verification rules. A learned improvement that needs broader scope remains a proposal until owner approval.
