# Physics/Math Validation

1. Validate only serious surviving candidates.
2. Use only relevant checks from the validation list.
3. Mark each `PASS`, `CONCERN`, `FAIL`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or `NOT_YET_TESTABLE`.
4. Suggest the smallest repair and new assumptions; do not rewrite the whole theory.
5. Block promotion on fatal failures.
