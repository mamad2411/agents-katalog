# Pentest-Report-on-a-lab-from-Inveteck-Global
Demo pentest report to share my findings

I’ve been working on a lab system built by **Inveteck Global** to test and improve my skills in system setup, configuration, and penetration testing. While analyzing the system’s open ports, I focused on two key services: **FTP and Jetty**. After detailed exploration and careful exploitation, I successfully gained access to the system and achieved a reverse shell.
This project has been both challenging and rewarding, providing me with deeper insights into identifying and exploiting vulnerabilities. I’ve put together a demo pentest report to share my findings and would greatly value any feedback or suggestions to help me improve and refine my approach.
