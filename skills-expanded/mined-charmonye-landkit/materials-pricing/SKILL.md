# Materials Pricing

## Goal

Prepare an implementation estimate with dated prices, sources, and realistic labor ranges.

## Process

1. Use quantities from `07_estimate_and_phasing/01_quantities`.
2. If quantities are missing, make a list of needed quantities and do not fake a precise estimate.
3. Search current prices through `web-research` or local price lists.
4. Fill `07_estimate_and_phasing/ESTIMATE_STRUCTURE.md`.
5. For every price, record source and date.
6. Split implementation into stages: preparation, engineering, surfaces, structures, planting, lighting, final finishing.

## Rules

- Do not present an estimated range as a final contractor quote.
- Separate materials and labor.
- Mark positions with high price volatility.
- If price depends on delivery, region, or currency, record it.

## Output

- Materials table.
- Labor table with range.
- Implementation stages.
- Assumptions and risks.
