# Web Research

## Goal

Find current and verifiable data for the project while preserving sources and access date.

## When Search Is Required

- Prices, availability, suppliers, delivery timelines.
- Current rules, regulations, restrictions, if they may change.
- Real analogs, references, images of objects or materials.
- Any data where outdated information may cause a wrong decision or estimate.

## Process

1. Formulate the research question.
2. Prefer primary sources: manufacturers, suppliers, official documents, maps, catalogs.
3. For every important finding, record link, access date, and what was taken.
4. Separate fact from designer inference.
5. Move results to the relevant file: brief, estimate, references, or decisions.

## Output

- Short summary.
- Source list.
- What applies to the project.
- What requires client or contractor verification.
