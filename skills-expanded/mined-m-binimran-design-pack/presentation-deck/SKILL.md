# presentation-deck

A deck that looks designed, not defaulted: one system, applied consistently.

## Process
1. **Outline first:** narrative arc, one idea per slide, the key takeaway per slide.
2. **Layout system:** a small set of slide templates (title, section, content, big-number, image-full,
   quote) with a consistent grid, type scale, and colour from the brand kit.
3. **Build** via the Canva connector (branded template) or as structured slides; place real content, not lorem.
4. **Visuals:** charts as part of the system; imagery via stock/generation (licensed); minimal text per slide.
5. **Polish:** alignment, consistent spacing, contrast (the `contrast-guard` hook), readable from the back row.

## Output
- The deck (confirmed from the connector) following the layout system, with speaker-note suggestions and
  licensing recorded.

## Guardrails
- Consistent system across slides; on-brand; legible; not a wall of bullet text.
- Confirm the connector built it; record asset licenses.
