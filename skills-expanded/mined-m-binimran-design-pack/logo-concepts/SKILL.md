# logo-concepts

Direction before decoration. Explore concepts grounded in the brand's strategy.

## Process
1. **Brief & strategy:** what the brand is, audience, personality, competitors, where the mark must work
   (favicon to billboard), constraints. A logo communicates, it isn't just pretty.
2. **Direction options:** wordmark, lettermark, pictorial, abstract, combination - with a rationale for each fit.
3. **Explore concepts:** sketch directions; use generation (Nano Banana/Higgsfield) for *visual exploration of
   style/feel*, then refine to a real vector mark (generation is a starting point, not the final logo).
4. **Test usage:** legible at small size, works in 1-colour/mono, on light/dark, with clear space.
5. **Hand to `brand-kit`** for logo usage rules.

## Output
- A direction brief + concept options with rationale, small-size/mono tests, and next steps to finalize as vector.

## Guardrails
- AI output is exploration - a final logo should be an original, ownable vector you can license/trademark.
  Don't pass a raw AI raster off as a deliverable logo, and check originality (no derivative/infringing marks).
- Confirm any generated exploration actually came from the connector.
