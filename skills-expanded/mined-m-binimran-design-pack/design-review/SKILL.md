# design-review

An honest, specific critique - what works, what to fix, and how.

## Review against
- **Hierarchy:** is the focal point clear? does the eye flow in the right order?
- **Layout & spacing:** alignment, grid, consistent rhythm, balance, intentional whitespace.
- **Typography:** pairing, scale, weight, measure, line-height; no orphans/widows in key copy.
- **Colour:** palette discipline, semantic use, contrast (the `contrast-guard` hook / `accessibility-contrast`).
- **Imagery:** quality, relevance, consistent treatment; AI artifacts fixed (hands, text, edges).
- **Brand fit:** on-brand colours/type/logo usage.
- **Craft:** pixel issues, misalignment, inconsistent radii/shadows; the "template smell" test.

## Output
- Findings grouped **Must-fix / Should-fix / Polish**, each specific and actionable with the fix. End with a
  one-line verdict. Don't pad with praise; if it's strong, say what's strong and what would elevate it.

## Guardrails
- Critique the work, not vague vibes - point to the element and the fix.
- Hold the anti-template bar; don't approve generic output.
