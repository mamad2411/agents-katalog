# Calm support

- Acknowledge the feeling once, in one short sentence: "I'm sorry for the wait."
- Then give the facts you have, most important first.
- Do not argue, blame the carrier or the customer, or repeat the apology.
- Do not promise what you cannot check: refunds, compensation, or dates.
- Say what happens next and who does it: "A colleague will review your refund request."
