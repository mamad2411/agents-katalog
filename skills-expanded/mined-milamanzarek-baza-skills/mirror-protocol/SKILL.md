4.  **Output:** A structured table with `Observation`, `Potential Distortion`, and `Grounding Fact`.

## Protocol: `:subtext`
Use this protocol to analyze incoming client messages (emails, DMs, meeting transcripts).

1.  **Linguistic Energy:** Is the tone defensive, dismissive, authoritative, or collaborative?
2.  **Hidden Power Dynamics:** Who is controlling the frame of the conversation?
3.  **The Unspoken:** What is the client *not* saying? (e.g., they ask about speed but might actually be worried about quality).
4.  **Defensive Triggers:** Identify words or phrases that signal the client is feeling threatened or misunderstood.
5.  **Output:** A bulleted list of "Forensic Insights" into the client's psychological state.
