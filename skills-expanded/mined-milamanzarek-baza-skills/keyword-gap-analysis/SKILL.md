\# Keyword \& Information Gap Logic



1\. \*\*The "Missing Intersection":\*\* Query your SEO MCP (SEMrush/Ahrefs) to find keywords where \[Competitor URL] ranks in the Top 10, but \[My URL] is not in the Top 50.

2\. \*\*Intent Clustering:\*\* Group these gaps by search intent:

&#x20;  - \*\*Informational:\*\* (They have the guides, we don't).

&#x20;  - \*\*Commercial:\*\* (They are on the "Best of" lists, we aren't).

&#x20;  - \*\*Transactional:\*\* (They rank for "Pricing" or "Alternative to...", we don't).

3\. \*\*Information Gain Scoring:\*\* For the top 5 gaps, analyze the competitor's content. Can we provide \*\*Unique Data, SME Quotes, or a Counter-Narrative\*\* that they lack?

4\. \*\*AI Visibility Check:\*\* Do these gap keywords trigger an "AI Overview" (SGE) in search results?

5\. \*\*Output:\*\* Create a \*\*Content Roadmap\*\* prioritized by "Difficulty vs. AI Visibility Potential."
