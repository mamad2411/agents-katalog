# Prepare for rushes with mise discipline

> Forecast-based prep lists, station maps, and recovery roles turning surges into choreography.

**Track:** 🍽️ Food & Beverage Ops · **Domain:** Service Flow · **Level:** foundation · **~20 min**

**Who this is for:** Chefs, F&B Managers, Restaurant Owners, Catering Leads

## When to Use This Skill

Forecast-based prep lists, station maps, and recovery roles turning surges into choreography.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Prep from reservation/forecast data + same-day-last-year, written as station lists
2. Par-check backups for the three fastest-moving items before doors
3. Assign a float role per shift whose only job is bottlenecks
4. 86-list communicated verbally AND posted; update instantly
5. Debrief after every rush: what ran out, what died on the pass
6. Track prep waste vs stockouts; both are planning failures

## Common Pitfalls

- Prepping to storage capacity instead of forecast
- Float role evaporating into regular tasks at first pressure
