# Write surveys without garbage-in

> Question construction, ordering effects, and response-scale discipline producing analyzable data.

**Track:** 🧠 UX Research & Design · **Domain:** Research Methods · **Level:** intermediate · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** UX Researchers, Product Designers, Design Leads, Service Designers

## When to Use This Skill

Question construction, ordering effects, and response-scale discipline producing analyzable data.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Draft research questions first; every item maps to one
2. One idea per question; kill double-barrels ruthlessly
3. Balanced scales with labeled midpoints; avoid agree-only sets
4. Randomize option order where position bias lurks
5. Add one attention-check and one open-ended for voice
6. Pilot n=10, inspect distributions and timing before full send

## Common Pitfalls

- Leading questions collecting confirmation, not data
- Ten-point scales nobody can meaningfully differentiate
