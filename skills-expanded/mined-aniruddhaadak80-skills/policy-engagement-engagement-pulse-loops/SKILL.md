# Run engagement pulse loops that change things

> Short surveys, visible actions, closed feedback loops — trust grows from evidence of listening.

**Track:** 👥 People & HR · **Domain:** Policy & Engagement · **Level:** intermediate · **~25 min**

**Who this is for:** HR Managers, Recruiters, People Ops, Team Leads

## When to Use This Skill

Short surveys, visible actions, closed feedback loops — trust grows from evidence of listening.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Pulse 5-8 questions max, anonymous with cohort minimums (n≥5)
2. Ask about energy, clarity, growth, blockers — not pizza-party satisfaction
3. Share raw themes within one week, including uncomfortable ones
4. Pick ONE action per cycle with named owner and date
5. Report progress next cycle before collecting new data
6. Retire questions answered by shipped actions

## Common Pitfalls

- Annual surveys whose results vanish into decks
- Anonymous channels used for blame archaeology
