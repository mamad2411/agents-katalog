# Map journeys across service touchpoints

> Current-state journey maps exposing emotional lows and operational handoff failures.

**Track:** 🧠 UX Research & Design · **Domain:** Design Practice · **Level:** intermediate · **~40 min**

**Who this is for:** UX Researchers, Product Designers, Design Leads, Service Designers

## When to Use This Skill

Current-state journey maps exposing emotional lows and operational handoff failures.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Pick ONE persona + scenario with real data behind it
2. Lay stages by user language, not internal process names
3. Per stage: actions, thoughts, emotions curve, backend actors
4. Mark handoffs between teams — pain concentrates there
5. Quantify lows with support tickets/interviews evidence
6. Prioritize fixes at emotion valleys × business impact

## Common Pitfalls

- Idealized future-state maps pretending current pain away
- Maps as wall art never revisited
