# Communicate through claims without radio silence

> Proactive status rhythms, plain-language updates, and expectation resets during long investigations.

**Track:** ☂️ Insurance Operations · **Domain:** Claims Experience · **Level:** foundation · **~20 min**

**Who this is for:** Claims Adjusters, Underwriters, Insurance Ops, Brokers

## When to Use This Skill

Proactive status rhythms, plain-language updates, and expectation resets during long investigations.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Set contact cadence at claim opening and keep it even with no news
2. Plain-language status: what's done, what's pending, who owns next step
3. Reset expectations BEFORE deadlines slip, never after
4. One named owner per claim; handoffs announced personally
5. Log every contact attempt; patterns feed process fixes
6. Close with summary of what was paid, why, and how to dispute

## Common Pitfalls

- Silence interpreted as denial driving complaint escalation
- Ownership lost in shift handoffs mid-investigation
