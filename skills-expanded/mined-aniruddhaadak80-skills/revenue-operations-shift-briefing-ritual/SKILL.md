# Run shift briefings that actually prepare teams

> Five-minute structured briefings covering arrivals, VIPs, issues, and focus metric.

**Track:** 🏨 Hospitality & Travel · **Domain:** Revenue Operations · **Level:** foundation · **~15 min**

**Who this is for:** Hotel & Restaurant Managers, Travel Designers, Guest Experience Teams

## When to Use This Skill

Five-minute structured briefings covering arrivals, VIPs, issues, and focus metric.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Same format daily: arrivals/VIPs, groups, known issues, yesterday's lesson, today's focus
2. Name specific guests needing extra care with their context
3. Surface maintenance/aesthetic issues visible to guests today
4. One service focus theme per shift, not twelve
5. Close with questions invited explicitly
6. Rotate who leads so knowledge spreads beyond managers

## Common Pitfalls

- Briefings becoming schedule-readings
- VIP context living only in the GM's head
