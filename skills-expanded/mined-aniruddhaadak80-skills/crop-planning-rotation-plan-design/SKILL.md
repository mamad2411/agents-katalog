# Design crop rotations that build, not mine, soil

> Diversity windows, pest-cycle breaks, and nutrient balancing across multi-year plans.

**Track:** 🌾 Agriculture & AgTech · **Domain:** Crop Planning · **Level:** intermediate · **~35 min**

**Who this is for:** Farm Managers, Agronomists, AgTech Builders, Supply Sustainability Teams

## When to Use This Skill

Diversity windows, pest-cycle breaks, and nutrient balancing across multi-year plans.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Map fields with soil test history; group by capability not habit
2. Sequence to break pest/disease cycles (host-free windows) per crop family
3. Balance nutrient export: legumes precede high-N demand crops
4. Include cover crops where windows allow; quantify biomass goals
5. Plan machinery/labor feasibility across the rotation honestly
6. Review yields and soil organic trends annually against plan intent

## Common Pitfalls

- Economics of one season quietly collapsing rotation diversity
- Cover crops seeded without termination logistics planned
