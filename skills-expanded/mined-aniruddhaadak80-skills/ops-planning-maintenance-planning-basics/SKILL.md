# Plan maintenance without grounding surprises

> Forecast-driven scheduling, parts lead-time reality, and deferred-item tracking keeping fleets available.

**Track:** ✈️ Aviation & Aerospace Ops · **Domain:** Ops Planning · **Level:** intermediate · **~35 min**

**Who this is for:** Pilots, Flight Ops, Safety Managers, Maintenance Engineers, Aviation PMs

## When to Use This Skill

Forecast-driven scheduling, parts lead-time reality, and deferred-item tracking keeping fleets available.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Forecast maintenance events from utilization projections, not calendar guesses
2. Track parts lead times as distributions; order long-leads at contract signing
3. Manage deferred defects with hard limits and review board sign-off
4. Bundle tasks opportunistically when aircraft access anyway
5. Negotiate heavy-check windows with realistic discovery allowances
6. Review plan-vs-actual monthly; feed variance into next cycle's buffers

## Common Pitfalls

- Non-routine discoveries treated as schedule shocks every single time
- Deferred items aging silently past limits
