# Triage vendor MSA redlines fast

> Deal-breaker checklist separating acceptable edits from escalations in vendor paper.

**Track:** ⚖️ Legal & Compliance · **Domain:** Contracts & IP · **Level:** intermediate · **~40 min**

**Who this is for:** Legal Ops, Privacy Officers, Compliance Teams, Founders, Engineers

## When to Use This Skill

Deal-breaker checklist separating acceptable edits from escalations in vendor paper.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Fix your walk-away list first: liability cap, indemnity scope, data rights
2. Compare against playbook positions; mark green/yellow/red per clause
3. Escalate only reds with proposed alternative language ready
4. Check auto-renewal and price-escalation clauses explicitly
5. Verify assignment/change-of-control terms against acquisition plans
6. Store executed versions searchable by clause type

## Common Pitfalls

- Unlimited liability accepted via boilerplate oversight
- Vendor-owned feedback clauses covering YOUR improvements
