# Prevent stockouts on heroes with a calendar rhythm

> Velocity-based reorder points plus promo calendar awareness keeping best-sellers in stock.

**Track:** 🛍️ E-commerce & Retail · **Domain:** Inventory & Returns · **Level:** intermediate · **~25 min**

**Who this is for:** E-commerce Managers, Merchandisers, Retail Operators, DTC Founders

## When to Use This Skill

Velocity-based reorder points plus promo calendar awareness keeping best-sellers in stock.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Compute reorder points from actual sales velocity + supplier lead times
2. Overlay promo/email calendar onto stock projections BEFORE campaigns launch
3. Flag heroes dipping below 3 weeks cover automatically weekly
4. Negotiate safety consignment or faster lane for top-5 SKUs
5. Post-mortem every stockout: revenue lost, what signal was missed
6. Track in-stock rate as the north star over inventory turnover alone

## Common Pitfalls

- Promotions scheduled against inventory nobody checked
- Reorder points set once at launch and forgotten
