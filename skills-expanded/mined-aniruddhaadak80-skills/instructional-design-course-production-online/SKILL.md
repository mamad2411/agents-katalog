# Produce an online course people finish

> Structure, pacing, and accountability mechanics fighting the 90% non-completion norm.

**Track:** 🎓 Education & Teaching · **Domain:** Instructional Design · **Level:** advanced · **~45 min**

**Who this is for:** Teachers, Instructional Designers, Course Creators, Trainers

## When to Use This Skill

Structure, pacing, and accountability mechanics fighting the 90% non-completion norm.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Promise one transformation in the title; module = milestone toward it
2. Videos ≤7 minutes each, one idea per video, show don't only tell
3. Deliverable after every module learners can share publicly
4. Weekly rhythm (cohort) or streak mechanics (self-paced) — pick one
5. First-module win within 30 minutes of signup
6. Interview finishers AND quitters; fix what quitters cite first

## Common Pitfalls

- Twelve hours of lecture nobody asked to binge
- Quizzes testing video recall over applied skill
