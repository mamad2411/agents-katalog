# Offboard gracefully and securely

> Knowledge transfer, access revocation, and humane exits protecting everyone involved.

**Track:** 👥 People & HR · **Domain:** Onboarding & Offboarding · **Level:** foundation · **~20 min**

**Who this is for:** HR Managers, Recruiters, People Ops, Team Leads

## When to Use This Skill

Knowledge transfer, access revocation, and humane exits protecting everyone involved.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Run exit knowledge-transfer checklist per owned system
2. Revoke access at separation hour: SSO, repos, VPN, cards
3. Transfer ownership of docs, cron jobs, vendor relationships
4. Exit interview focused on systemic learning, not venting capture
5. Alumni path: references, referral bonus, rehire eligibility stated
6. Audit orphaned accesses 30 days later

## Common Pitfalls

- Departures discovering single-person knowledge lock-ins
- Access lingering for months post-exit
