# Handle cost estimates and consent with clarity

> Range-based estimates, decision tiers, and documented consent protecting animals and practices.

**Track:** 🐾 Veterinary Medicine · **Domain:** Clinical Workflow · **Level:** intermediate · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** Veterinarians, Vet Techs, Practice Managers, Animal Welfare Teams

## When to Use This Skill

Range-based estimates, decision tiers, and documented consent protecting animals and practices.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Give ranges with what moves a case between low and high end
2. Present tiered options (best / acceptable / palliative) without judgment
3. Document declined diagnostics explicitly with risks explained
4. Never let finances stay implicit — revisit if the case drifts from estimate
5. Offer written take-home summaries of decisions and next steps
6. Debrief emotionally hard conversations as a team

## Common Pitfalls

- Estimates quoted as fixed prices then 'surprised'
- Judgment-laden delivery pushing clients away from care entirely
