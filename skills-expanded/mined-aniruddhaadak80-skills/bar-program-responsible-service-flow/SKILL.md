# Run responsible service without killing hospitality

> Pace monitoring, water/food pacing tactics, and refusal scripts that preserve dignity.

**Track:** 🍸 Beverage & Bar Program · **Domain:** Bar Program · **Level:** advanced · **~20 min**

**Who this is for:** Bar Managers, Bartenders, Beverage Directors, Sommeliers

## When to Use This Skill

Pace monitoring, water/food pacing tactics, and refusal scripts that preserve dignity.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Monitor pace per table/guest as a team responsibility, not one server's burden
2. Slow service proactively: water refills, food suggestions, course spacing
3. Refusal script ready: private, firm, offer alternatives, involve manager early
4. Document incidents same-night factually for license protection
5. Know your jurisdiction's cutoffs and transport-duty obligations cold
6. Debrief near-misses to sharpen the team's shared radar

## Common Pitfalls

- Nobody wanting to be the one who says stop
- Refusals delivered publicly turning safety into confrontation
