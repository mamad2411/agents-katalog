# Playbook: Execute a coordinated launch week

> Cross-functional release with support readiness, analytics verified, and rollback armed.

**Track:** 🗺️ Product Management · **Domain:** Journey Playbooks · **Level:** journey · **~65 min**

**Who this is for:** Product Managers, Product Owners, Founders, Product Analysts

Cross-functional release with support readiness, analytics verified, and rollback armed.

## Journey Steps

1. Step 1 — Run launches with a go/no-go checklist: start with "Freeze scope two weeks out; bug-fix-only policy"
2. Step 2 — Build outcome-based roadmaps: start with "Structure by quarter → outcome metric → problem statements, not feature lists"
3. How it fits together: Freeze early. Analytics verification is the most-skipped step and the most-regretted.
