# Operate a just-culture safety reporting flow

> Confidential reporting, non-punitive classification, and visible fixes keeping the pipeline alive.

**Track:** ✈️ Aviation & Aerospace Ops · **Domain:** SMS & Maintenance · **Level:** advanced · **~35 min**

**Who this is for:** Pilots, Flight Ops, Safety Managers, Maintenance Engineers, Aviation PMs

## When to Use This Skill

Confidential reporting, non-punitive classification, and visible fixes keeping the pipeline alive.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Multiple reporting channels including truly anonymous option
2. Classify events: human error (coach) / at-risk behavior (coach+remove temptation) / reckless (discipline)
3. Acknowledge every report within 48h even if investigation pending
4. Publish sanitized lessons learned widely — visibility drives future reports
5. Track corrective actions to closure with named owners
6. Measure leading indicators (reports filed) not just accident counts

## Common Pitfalls

- Reports disappearing into voids, killing future candor
- Punishing honest error reports behind 'accountability' language
