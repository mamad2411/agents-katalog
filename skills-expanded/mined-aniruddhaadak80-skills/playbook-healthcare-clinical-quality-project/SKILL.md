> A measurable process change sustained beyond the pilot window.

## Journey Steps

1. Step 1 — Lead a PDSA quality improvement cycle: start with "Define problem with baseline data over sufficient weeks, not anecdotes"
2. Step 2 — Tune clinical decision support alerts responsibly: start with "Pull override rates and documented reasons per alert type"
3. How it fits together: Baseline data before change; run-chart after. Sustainment is the real finish line.
