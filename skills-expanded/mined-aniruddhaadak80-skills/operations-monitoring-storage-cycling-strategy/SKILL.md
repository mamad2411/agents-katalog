# Operate storage within warranty and market reality

> Cycle management balancing degradation costs against arbitrage and resilience value.

**Track:** 🔋 Energy & Renewables · **Domain:** Operations & Monitoring · **Level:** advanced · **~35 min**

**Who this is for:** Solar Engineers, Energy Analysts, Project Developers, O&M Teams

## When to Use This Skill

Cycle management balancing degradation costs against arbitrage and resilience value.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Model degradation cost per cycle at CURRENT replacement prices
2. Dispatch only when expected spread clears cycle cost plus margin
3. Reserve capacity commitments (backup contracts) take precedence over arbitrage
4. Monitor cell imbalance trends; schedule balancing maintenance proactively
5. Log every cycle with SOC windows feeding warranty compliance records
6. Re-optimize strategy quarterly as tariffs and degradation curves evolve

## Common Pitfalls

- Free-cycling for pennies spreads burning warranty cycles
- Backup reserve silently drained by morning arbitrage
