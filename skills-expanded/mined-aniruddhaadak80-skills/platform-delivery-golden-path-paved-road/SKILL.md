Template repositories, self-service scaffolding, and paved-road adoption measured by pull not push.

## Steps

1. Extract patterns from your BEST team's setup, not committee ideals
2. Scaffold new services with security/observability/CI included by default
3. Make the golden path faster than doing it manually — adoption follows friction
4. Keep templates owned and versioned; breaking changes get changelogs + migration notes
5. Measure voluntary adoption % as THE platform metric
6. Sunset parallel snowflake infrastructure loudly once parity reached
