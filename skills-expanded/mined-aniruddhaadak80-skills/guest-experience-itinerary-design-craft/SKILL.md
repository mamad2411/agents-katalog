# Design itineraries that feel effortless

> Energy pacing, transfer buffers, and one anchor per day building trips travelers rave about.

**Track:** 🏨 Hospitality & Travel · **Domain:** Guest Experience · **Level:** intermediate · **~35 min**

**Who this is for:** Hotel & Restaurant Managers, Travel Designers, Guest Experience Teams

## When to Use This Skill

Energy pacing, transfer buffers, and one anchor per day building trips travelers rave about.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. One anchor experience per day; everything else supports it
2. Respect jet lag and energy curves for first 48 hours especially
3. Buffer transfers by measured local reality, not map estimates
4. Pre-book the unmissable, leave afternoons flexible for weather/mood
5. Include food logic near activities, not across town
6. Deliver as scannable day cards with offline maps and confirmation numbers

## Common Pitfalls

- Five 'highlights' per day exhausting everyone
- Tight connections built on best-case traffic
