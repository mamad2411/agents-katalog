# Deliver demos buyers remember

> Discovery-linked narratives, their-data rehearsals, and objection-proof sequencing.

**Track:** 🤝 Sales & Customer Success · **Domain:** Demo Craft · **Level:** intermediate · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** Account Executives, Customer Success Managers, Sales Engineers, Founders Selling

## When to Use This Skill

Discovery-linked narratives, their-data rehearsals, and objection-proof sequencing.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Open restating THEIR discovery priorities; demo answers those only
2. Rehearse with sample data shaped like their business, never generic placeholder data
3. Sequence: their pain first, wow moment second, feature tour last (or never)
4. Pre-handle top objections live ('you might ask about exports…')
5. Leave-behind references the demo story for internal champions
6. Log which moments generated questions; that's your buying-signal map

## Common Pitfalls

- Feature tours answering questions nobody asked
- Demo environments breaking exactly during flagship moments
