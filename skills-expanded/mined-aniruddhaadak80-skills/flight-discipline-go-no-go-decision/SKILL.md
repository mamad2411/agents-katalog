# Decide go/no-go with structured risk gates

> Personal minimums, IMSAFE self-check, and pre-defined abort criteria beating get-there-itis.

**Track:** ✈️ Aviation & Aerospace Ops · **Domain:** Flight Discipline · **Level:** intermediate · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** Pilots, Flight Ops, Safety Managers, Maintenance Engineers, Aviation PMs

## When to Use This Skill

Personal minimums, IMSAFE self-check, and pre-defined abort criteria beating get-there-itis.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Complete IMSAFE: illness, medication, stress, alcohol, fatigue, eating
2. Score mission against written personal minimums BEFORE looking at desire
3. Identify the two strongest pressure sources pushing toward go; neutralize verbally
4. Define abort/diversion criteria and alternate options in advance
5. Brief the decision out loud — spoken reasoning catches flaws
6. Post-flight: log decision quality, not just outcome

## Common Pitfalls

- Minimums flexing downward with experience inflation
- Outcome bias teaching wrong lessons from lucky flights
