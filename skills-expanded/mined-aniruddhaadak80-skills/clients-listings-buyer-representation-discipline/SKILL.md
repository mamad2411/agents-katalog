# Represent buyers with fiduciary discipline

> Needs discovery, honest property assessments, and negotiation advocacy documented properly.

**Track:** 🏘️ Real Estate · **Domain:** Clients & Listings · **Level:** intermediate · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** Agents, Investors, Property Managers, Analysts

## When to Use This Skill

Needs discovery, honest property assessments, and negotiation advocacy documented properly.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Discovery beyond budget: timeline, must-haves, deal-breakers, decision process
2. Preview properties against THEIR criteria; reject mismatches proactively
3. Point out cons as readily as pros — reputation compounds over decades
4. Prepare offers from comps YOU verified, not asking-price anchoring
5. Negotiate repairs with contractor quotes attached, not vibes
6. Document advice given; agency means records

## Common Pitfalls

- Steering toward own listings over fit
- Waiving inspections without explaining specific risks
