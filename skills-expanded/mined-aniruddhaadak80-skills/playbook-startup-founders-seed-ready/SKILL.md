## Journey Steps

1. Step 1 — Build a founder narrative deck: start with "Slide order: problem, insight, solution, demo, market entry, business model, traction, team, ask"
2. Step 2 — Find your first repeatable growth loop: start with "List candidate loops: SEO/content, communities, partnerships, referrals, PLU product hooks"
3. How it fits together: One compounding loop beats five flat channels. Honest numbers survive diligence; inflated ones die there.
