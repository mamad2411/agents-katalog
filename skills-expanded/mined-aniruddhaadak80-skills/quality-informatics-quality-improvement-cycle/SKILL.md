# Lead a PDSA quality improvement cycle

> Small tested changes over big launches: plan-do-study-act cycles with measurable process metrics.

**Track:** 🏥 Healthcare & Clinical · **Domain:** Quality & Informatics · **Level:** intermediate · **~40 min**

**Who this is for:** Nurses, Physicians, Clinical Managers, Health Informaticists

## When to Use This Skill

Small tested changes over big launches: plan-do-study-act cycles with measurable process metrics.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Define problem with baseline data over sufficient weeks, not anecdotes
2. Pick ONE process change with a clear mechanism story
3. Plan: who measures what, when, predicted effect written beforehand
4. Do: pilot on one unit or one week; protect the experiment
5. Study: compare run-chart against baseline; look for special-cause variation
6. Act: adopt, adapt, or abandon honestly; document decision

## Common Pitfalls

- Changing three things simultaneously, unlearnable results
- Sustainability ignored after the pilot enthusiasm fades
