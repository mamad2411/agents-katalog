# Prequalify subcontractors beyond lowest price

> Safety records, financial health, capacity truth, and reference depth preventing downstream failures.

**Track:** 🏗️ Construction & Built Environment · **Domain:** Subcontractor Management · **Level:** intermediate · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** Project Engineers, Site Managers, Cost Estimators, Contractors

## When to Use This Skill

Safety records, financial health, capacity truth, and reference depth preventing downstream failures.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Score safety history (EMR/TRIR trends) with minimum thresholds firm
2. Verify financial capacity for the package size; bonding capacity checked
3. Confirm actual crew availability for your dates, not theoretical rosters
4. Call three recent references about SIMILAR scope specifically
5. Site walk included before bid for anything existing-conditions dependent
6. Requalify annually; last year's good performer may be this year's risk

## Common Pitfalls

- Lowest-bid winners vanishing mid-package financially
- References curated by the subcontractor accepted unverified
