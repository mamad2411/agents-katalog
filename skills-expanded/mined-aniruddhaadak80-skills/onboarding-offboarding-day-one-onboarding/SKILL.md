# Deliver a first week that converts

> Pre-provisioned access, buddy assignment, and a shipped-by-Friday first task.

**Track:** 👥 People & HR · **Domain:** Onboarding & Offboarding · **Level:** intermediate · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** HR Managers, Recruiters, People Ops, Team Leads

## When to Use This Skill

Pre-provisioned access, buddy assignment, and a shipped-by-Friday first task.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Provision accounts/hardware BEFORE day one; verify login day zero
2. Assign an onboarding buddy separate from the manager
3. Schedule meet-and-greets with key collaborators week one
4. Ship something tiny to production by Friday
5. Day-5 pulse survey: clarity, belonging, blockers
6. Manager check-ins at 7/30/90 days against role expectations

## Common Pitfalls

- Week one lost to access tickets
- New hires reading docs alone for five days
