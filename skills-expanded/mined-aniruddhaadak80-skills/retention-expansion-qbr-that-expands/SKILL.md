# Run business reviews that expand accounts

> Value-delivery narratives with customer metrics turning renewals into growth conversations.

**Track:** 🤝 Sales & Customer Success · **Domain:** Retention & Expansion · **Level:** intermediate · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** Account Executives, Customer Success Managers, Sales Engineers, Founders Selling

## When to Use This Skill

Value-delivery narratives with customer metrics turning renewals into growth conversations.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Open with THEIR goals restated; measure everything against them
2. Show adoption vs success benchmarks with concrete usage stories
3. Quantify delivered value in customer's own KPIs, co-owned numbers
4. Surface untapped use cases mapped to unmet goals
5. Propose next-quarter success plan requiring specific commitments
6. Multi-thread: new stakeholders present every review

## Common Pitfalls

- Feature-update decks nobody reads
- Single-threaded relationships evaporating on champion exit
