# Prepare a STAR story bank, not memorized scripts

> 8-10 reusable stories mapped to competencies, told fresh under pressure.

**Track:** 🎯 Career & Job Search · **Domain:** Interviews & Offers · **Level:** intermediate · **~40 min**

**Who this is for:** Job Seekers, Career Changers, New Grads, Anyone Levelling Up

## When to Use This Skill

8-10 reusable stories mapped to competencies, told fresh under pressure.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Inventory career moments: conflict, failure, leadership, ambiguity, impact
2. Draft each in Situation-Task-Action-Result with YOUR actions explicit
3. Tag stories by competency they prove; overlaps fine, gaps are prep targets
4. Rehearse OUT LOUD to 90-second versions; timing beats content polish
5. For each story prepare one follow-up-depth detail you can expand into
6. Post-interview log which stories landed; refine weak ones while fresh

## Common Pitfalls

- Word-perfect scripts shattering on unexpected follow-ups
- Stories where the team succeeded but 'I' disappears
