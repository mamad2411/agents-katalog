# Operate as a staff engineer without becoming a bottleneck

> Scope selection, influence-through-artifacts, and force-multiplication rhythms for individual contributors.

**Track:** 🧭 Engineering Leadership · **Domain:** Staff+ Operating Models · **Level:** advanced · **~45 min**

**Who this is for:** Engineering Managers, Tech Leads, Staff Engineers, CTOs

## When to Use This Skill

Scope selection, influence-through-artifacts, and force-multiplication rhythms for individual contributors.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Pick one org-level problem per quarter; decline everything else explicitly
2. Write decision documents others execute — code is your worst multiplier
3. Maintain apprenticeship: two engineers growing toward your current scope
4. Schedule recurring alignment with peer staff across teams
5. Measure success by decisions improved, not lines or tickets touched
6. Escalate systemic blockers once, then build the mechanism preventing recurrence

## Common Pitfalls

- Hero-debugging everything while org-level debt compounds
- Strategy docs nobody reads because teams weren't involved
