# Find your first repeatable growth loop

> One channel executed deeply beats five dabbling channels; instrument the loop end-to-end.

**Track:** 🚀 Startup & Founders · **Domain:** Growth & Fundraising · **Level:** advanced · **~35 min**

**Who this is for:** Founders, Indie Hackers, Early Employees, Solopreneurs

## When to Use This Skill

One channel executed deeply beats five dabbling channels; instrument the loop end-to-end.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. List candidate loops: SEO/content, communities, partnerships, referrals, PLU product hooks
2. Pick one matching founder strengths + audience habitat
3. Instrument full loop: touch → visit → signup → activation → share
4. Run 4-week sprint with weekly output targets, review honestly
5. Double down only when loop math shows compounding signs
6. Kill or iterate; dabbling across channels is the failure mode

## Common Pitfalls

- Channel-hopping weekly resetting all learning to zero
- Growth tactics before retention exists
