# Close the loop from tickets to knowledge base

> Every resolved pattern becomes a KB article; every article deflects future tickets measurably.

**Track:** 💬 Customer Support · **Domain:** Ticket Excellence · **Level:** intermediate · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** Support Engineers, CX Leads, Helpdesk Teams, Technical Account Managers

## When to Use This Skill

Every resolved pattern becomes a KB article; every article deflects future tickets measurably.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Tag tickets that needed a nonexistent article — queue them for writing
2. Write from the customer's search phrase, not internal terminology
3. Structure: symptom → quick fix → why it happens → when to escalate
4. Link new articles in macro responses; measure deflection per view
5. Prune/update top-20 articles quarterly against current product UI
6. Celebrate deflection numbers alongside CSAT — both matter

## Common Pitfalls

- KB as dusty archive nobody updates after releases
- Articles written to satisfy audits rather than searches
