# Respond to reviews strategically

> Templates + genuine specifics turning public reviews into trust assets.

**Track:** 🏨 Hospitality & Travel · **Domain:** Guest Experience · **Level:** foundation · **~20 min**

**Who this is for:** Hotel & Restaurant Managers, Travel Designers, Guest Experience Teams

## When to Use This Skill

Templates + genuine specifics turning public reviews into trust assets.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Respond to every review within 48h — positive ones too
2. Negative: acknowledge specific issue, no excuses, state concrete fix done
3. Positive: name the staff moment they mentioned; reinforce specifically
4. Never argue facts publicly; take disputes offline with contact route
5. Mine monthly themes into operations fixes; mention improvements later ('you asked, we changed')
6. Track rating trend against response latency

## Common Pitfalls

- Copy-paste replies visible from space
- Defensiveness on legitimate complaints going screenshot-viral
