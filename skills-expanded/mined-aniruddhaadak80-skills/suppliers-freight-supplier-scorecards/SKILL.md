# Manage suppliers with balanced scorecards

> OTIF, quality, responsiveness, and cost trends driving development or exit decisions.

**Track:** 🚚 Supply Chain & Logistics · **Domain:** Suppliers & Freight · **Level:** intermediate · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** Supply Planners, Logistics Managers, Procurement, Operations Analysts

## When to Use This Skill

OTIF, quality, responsiveness, and cost trends driving development or exit decisions.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Measure OTIF (on-time in-full) from need date, not promised date
2. Add quality PPM and corrective-action response time to the card
3. Weight dimensions by category strategy, not one-size formula
4. Share scorecards with suppliers quarterly — surprises are failures
5. Tie score bands to concrete consequences: volume growth or exit path
6. Track trend direction; one bad quarter ≠ chronic underperformer

## Common Pitfalls

- Scorecards measuring only price variance
- Top offenders kept because switching is annoying
