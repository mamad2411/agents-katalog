# Design campaign measurement before launching

> Hypothesis, KPI tree, tracking implementation, and readout date committed upfront.

**Track:** 📣 Marketing & Growth · **Domain:** Lifecycle & Campaigns · **Level:** foundation · **~30 min**

**Who this is for:** Growth Marketers, Content Strategists, Lifecycle Marketers, Founders

## When to Use This Skill

Hypothesis, KPI tree, tracking implementation, and readout date committed upfront.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Write hypothesis: audience X + message Y through channel Z yields metric delta
2. Define primary KPI and guardrails (unsubscribe rate, CAC ceiling)
3. Implement UTM taxonomy documented centrally — no freeform tags
4. Verify tracking fires correctly BEFORE spend starts
5. Set minimum sample/duration for any conclusion; note it in calendar
6. Readout includes confidence statement and next-test proposal

## Common Pitfalls

- Post-hoc channel credit claimed from correlation
- Tracking broken discovered at readout time
