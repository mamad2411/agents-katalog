# Run food safety logs inspectors respect

> Temperature logs, cooling records, and corrective actions forming an audit-ready HACCP trail.

**Track:** 🍽️ Food & Beverage Ops · **Domain:** Kitchen Consistency · **Level:** intermediate · **~25 min**

**Who this is for:** Chefs, F&B Managers, Restaurant Owners, Catering Leads

## When to Use This Skill

Temperature logs, cooling records, and corrective actions forming an audit-ready HACCP trail.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Map critical control points per menu; log temps at defined frequencies
2. Cooling records show time/temp curves meeting standards, with probe placement noted
3. Corrective actions documented WHEN taken, signed by who took them
4. Calibrate thermometers weekly with ice-point method, logged
5. Rehearse the inspector walkthrough quarterly using your own logs
6. Digital or paper — one system of record, no shadow clipboards

## Common Pitfalls

- Backfilled logs that contradict delivery timestamps
- Same thermometer reading 10°F off for months
