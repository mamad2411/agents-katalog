# Playbook: Convert interviews into offers

> STAR story bank plus principled negotiation landing numbers you can defend.

**Track:** 🗺️ Career & Job Search · **Domain:** Journey Playbooks · **Level:** journey · **~70 min**

**Who this is for:** Job Seekers, Career Changers, New Grads, Anyone Levelling Up

## When to Use This Skill

STAR story bank plus principled negotiation landing numbers you can defend.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Journey Steps

1. Step 1 — Prepare a STAR story bank, not memorized scripts: start with "Inventory career moments: conflict, failure, leadership, ambiguity, impact"
2. Step 2 — Negotiate offers without fear or fantasy: start with "Anchor on market bands from multiple current sources for THIS role/city"
3. How it fits together: Rehearse out loud; negotiate once, clearly, with market truth.
