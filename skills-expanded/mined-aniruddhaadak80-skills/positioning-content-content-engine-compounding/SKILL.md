# Run a content engine that compounds

> Topic clusters, refresh cycles, and distribution loops turning articles into an asset base.

**Track:** 📣 Marketing & Growth · **Domain:** Positioning & Content · **Level:** intermediate · **~40 min**

**Who this is for:** Growth Marketers, Content Strategists, Lifecycle Marketers, Founders

## When to Use This Skill

Topic clusters, refresh cycles, and distribution loops turning articles into an asset base.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Map pillar topics to buyer journey stages; own 2-3 pillars deeply
2. Interlink cluster content to pillar hubs deliberately
3. Ship on cadence you can sustain for a year, not a sprint month
4. Distribute each piece 5 ways: newsletter, socials, communities, repurposed clips, syndication
5. Refresh top decile performers quarterly instead of always creating new
6. Track assisted conversions, not last-click vanity

## Common Pitfalls

- Publishing into the void without distribution loops
- Chasing volume until topical authority dilutes
