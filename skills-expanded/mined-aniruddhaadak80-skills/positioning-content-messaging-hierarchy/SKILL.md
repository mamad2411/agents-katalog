# Build a messaging hierarchy from customer language

> One-liner → proof pillars → objection handling, mined verbatim from real buyer words.

**Track:** 📣 Marketing & Growth · **Domain:** Positioning & Content · **Level:** intermediate · **~35 min**

**Who this is for:** Growth Marketers, Content Strategists, Lifecycle Marketers, Founders

## When to Use This Skill

One-liner → proof pillars → objection handling, mined verbatim from real buyer words.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Harvest verbatims: sales calls, reviews, support tickets, churn surveys
2. Cluster by desired outcome, not feature vocabulary
3. Draft one-liner: for WHO who struggle with WHAT, we deliver OUTCOME unlike ALTERNATIVE
4. Support with three proof pillars, each backed by evidence artifact
5. List top objections with honest counters — including when NOT to buy
6. Test on 5 cold visitors: can they repeat what you do in their words?

## Common Pitfalls

- Internal jargon customers never use
- Claim soup: nine benefits competing for the headline
