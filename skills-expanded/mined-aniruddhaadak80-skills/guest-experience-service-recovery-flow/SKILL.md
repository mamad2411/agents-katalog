# Turn service failures into loyalty

> Fast acknowledgment, empowered staff, proportionate recovery, and follow-up converting complaints.

**Track:** 🏨 Hospitality & Travel · **Domain:** Guest Experience · **Level:** intermediate · **~25 min**

**Who this is for:** Hotel & Restaurant Managers, Travel Designers, Guest Experience Teams

## When to Use This Skill

Fast acknowledgment, empowered staff, proportionate recovery, and follow-up converting complaints.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Acknowledge within minutes; speed matters more than perfection
2. Empower frontline staff with recovery budget up to defined limits, no approvals needed
3. Recover proportionately AND personally — handwritten beats generic upgrade
4. Fix root cause before the guest departs when possible
5. Follow up next day with named person, not survey blast
6. Log failure taxonomy weekly; recurring classes get process fixes

## Common Pitfalls

- Recovery slower than the original failure
- Empowerment rhetoric with approval queues in practice
