# QA support quality without demoralizing agents

> Calibrated rubric sampling, coaching-first reviews, and trend tracking over gotcha auditing.

**Track:** 💬 Customer Support · **Domain:** Support Operations · **Level:** advanced · **~35 min**

**Who this is for:** Support Engineers, CX Leads, Helpdesk Teams, Technical Account Managers

## When to Use This Skill

Calibrated rubric sampling, coaching-first reviews, and trend tracking over gotcha auditing.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Rubric: accuracy, completeness, tone, process adherence — weighted by issue class
2. Sample randomly PLUS all low-CSAT tickets; calibrate reviewers monthly
3. Review as coaching conversation with self-assessment first
4. Trend agent scores over time; never rank publicly on single weeks
5. Feed systemic findings into product/KB, not just individual coaching
6. Track inter-rater agreement; drift means rubric needs revision

## Common Pitfalls

- QA as surveillance producing defensive writing styles
- Perfect scores for polite wrong answers
