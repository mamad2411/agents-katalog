## Steps

1. Inventory current backlog candidates with owners attached
2. Early stage: rank by founder judgment + one metric; speed beats ceremony
3. Scale stage: RICE with calibrated effort from eng estimates
4. Platform investments: cost-of-delay thinking over point scores
5. Cap WIP explicitly; priorities without capacity are fiction
6. Recalibrate scores quarterly against realized outcomes
