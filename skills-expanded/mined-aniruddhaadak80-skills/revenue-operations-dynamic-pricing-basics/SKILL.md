# Apply revenue management without alienating guests

> Demand-based pricing with fences, monitoring, and fairness optics protecting brand.

**Track:** 🏨 Hospitality & Travel · **Domain:** Revenue Operations · **Level:** advanced · **~40 min**

**Who this is for:** Hotel & Restaurant Managers, Travel Designers, Guest Experience Teams

## When to Use This Skill

Demand-based pricing with fences, monitoring, and fairness optics protecting brand.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Segment demand: business vs leisure vs event windows behave differently
2. Set rate tiers with honest fence differences (flexibility, view, timing)
3. Review pickup pace weekly against same-time-last-year and comp sets
4. Adjust on leading indicators (search volume, booking pace), not panic
5. Cap surge multiples; price gouging headlines outlast the revenue
6. Post-mortem every high-demand window: captured vs left-on-table

## Common Pitfalls

- Static pricing leaving peak revenue unfunded
- Loyalty members seeing worse rates than strangers
