# Handle objections without groveling or dodging

> Acknowledge → clarify → reframe to the underlying concern → evidence → confirm resolution.

**Track:** 🤝 Sales & Customer Success · **Domain:** Discovery & Deals · **Level:** intermediate · **~20 min**

**Who this is for:** Account Executives, Customer Success Managers, Sales Engineers, Founders Selling

## When to Use This Skill

Acknowledge → clarify → reframe to the underlying concern → evidence → confirm resolution.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Acknowledge genuinely; defensiveness kills trust instantly
2. Clarify what's UNDER the stated objection (price often means unproven value)
3. Reframe against the cost of the status quo they already quantified
4. Offer specific evidence matched to objection type (case study, trial, SLA)
5. Confirm the objection is resolved explicitly before moving on
6. Log objections verbatim weekly — they're roadmap gold

## Common Pitfalls

- Instant discounting as reflex response to price pushback
- Arguing the objection away instead of exploring it
