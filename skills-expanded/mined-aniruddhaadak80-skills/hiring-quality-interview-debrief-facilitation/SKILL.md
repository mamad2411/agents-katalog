# Facilitate hiring debriefs that decide well

> Independent scoring first, evidence-based discussion, and bias callouts structured into the meeting.

**Track:** 👥 People & HR · **Domain:** Hiring Quality · **Level:** intermediate · **~25 min**

**Who this is for:** HR Managers, Recruiters, People Ops, Team Leads

## When to Use This Skill

Independent scoring first, evidence-based discussion, and bias callouts structured into the meeting.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Collect written independent scores BEFORE anyone speaks aloud
2. Start with strongest disagreement cases, not easiest consensus
3. Require behavioral evidence citations for every claim made
4. Name decision rules upfront: unanimous vs majority vs veto
5. Assign devil's advocate explicitly for borderline candidates
6. Record outcome rationale verbatim for future calibration audits

## Common Pitfalls

- Loudest interviewer anchoring the whole room instantly
- 'Cultural fit' used as uncritiquable rejection reason
