# Build a founder narrative deck

> 12 slides answering investor questions in order: why now, why you, why this market.

**Track:** 🚀 Startup & Founders · **Domain:** Growth & Fundraising · **Level:** intermediate · **~40 min**

**Who this is for:** Founders, Indie Hackers, Early Employees, Solopreneurs

## When to Use This Skill

12 slides answering investor questions in order: why now, why you, why this market.
Use it whenever a matching task appears in conversation — the agent loads these instructions on demand.

## Steps

1. Slide order: problem, insight, solution, demo, market entry, business model, traction, team, ask
2. Lead every slide with the headline takeaway sentence
3. Traction slide uses honest numbers trending up, annotated spikes
4. 'Why now' needs a concrete shift: tech cost curve, regulation, behavior
5. Ask slide: amount → milestones → runway math visible
6. Appendix carries depth; main deck stays 12-15 slides

## Common Pitfalls

- Feature tours instead of narrative arcs
- Vanity metrics (downloads) where revenue belongs
