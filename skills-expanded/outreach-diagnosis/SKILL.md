# Outreach diagnosis (why you're getting zero)

Sending more of what isn't working just burns your list and your morale. This
skill finds the **real bottleneck** in a fundraising outreach funnel and tells you
honestly whether to keep going, change the message, change the channel, or stop
and fix something upstream first.

Read `shared/references/outreach-ethics.md` first.

## First, the honest baseline (so you don't panic at the wrong thing)
- **Cold outreach is a low-yield channel.** Even well-done cold email to investors
  converts on the order of ~1% to a meeting. **Zero from a small cold batch
  (say <50) is statistically normal — not a verdict on you or your company.**
- **Warm intros are a different game.** If *warm* intros are also converting to
  nothing, that's a real signal — targeting, pitch, or readiness, not the channel.
So separate the two before diagnosing. Same number "0 meetings" means very
different things cold vs warm.

## Gather the real funnel (by channel and tier)
Ask for the actual numbers, split cold vs warm and by target tier:
- Contacts reached, **opens** (email), **replies**, **meetings**, **advances**.
- Which were warm vs cold; which tier (A/B/C from `investor-targeting`).
- The exact message(s) sent and the target list.

## Localize the drop-off → the culprit

| Where it dies | Likely cause | Fix (skill) |
|---|---|---|
| **No opens** (email) | subject line, deliverability, wrong/guessed address | `cold-email` (subject); verify contacts |
| **Opens, no replies** | weak/generic hook, no real personalization, targeting off, or you're not yet compelling (traction/readiness) | `cold-email` hook · `investor-targeting` · readiness check below |
| **Replies, no meetings** | vague ask, or polite passes = a fit/traction signal | `cold-email` ask · read the passes |
| **Meetings, no advance** | the pitch, the traction, or genuine stage/fit mismatch | `pitch-narrative` · `cold-call` objections |
| **Warm intros also dying** | targeting or readiness, NOT the channel | targeting + readiness below |

## The four root causes (and how to tell which)
1. **Targeting** — are these actually investors who fund your stage + sector,
   right now? A list of the wrong people converts at zero no matter how good the
   email. Check against `investor-targeting` (stage/check/sector/recent activity).
2. **Hook / message** — does line 1 prove you researched *them* and give a
   specific reason? Generic = ignored. (`cold-email`.)
3. **Readiness / traction** — the honest one: sometimes the message is fine and
   the answer is "not yet fundable at this ask." If even warm, well-targeted
   intros pass, the raise may need more proof or a sharper narrative first.
   (`pitch-narrative`; and see the readiness check.)
4. **Channel** — cold when your profile needs warm. Unknown founder + cold +
   crowded space = near-zero; the fix is warm paths and network-bringing channels
   (`warm-path-finder`, `fundraising-agents`), not more cold.

## Readiness check (the honest gate)
Before sending more, sanity-check that you're actually investor-ready for this
ask: is there a real "why now", a credible traction/insight, a believable use of
funds, and a check-size-appropriate valuation? If several are soft, **stop
outreach and fix the story/traction first** — more emails won't move a raise the
market isn't ready to fund. This is not a knock on you; it's cheaper to learn now
than after burning your best intros.

## Output
- The funnel with the numbers, cold vs warm.
- **The single bottleneck** (targeting / hook / readiness / channel) with the
  evidence.
- The specific next move (which skill), and honestly whether to **keep sending,
  change the message, switch to warm/agents, or pause and fix upstream**.
- A "stop doing this" note if a channel or list is clearly dead.

## Refuse / push back when
- Asked to just "write more/better cold emails" when the real problem is targeting
  or readiness → diagnose first; a better email to the wrong list still converts
  at zero.
- Asked to keep blasting a dead channel → say stop, and point to the upstream fix.
