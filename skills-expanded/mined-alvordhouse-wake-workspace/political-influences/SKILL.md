## Waku's Take
- The Zapatista uprising on January 1, 1994 — same day as NAFTA — is one of the most precise political acts of the 20th century. Symbolism as a weapon.
- Aaron Swartz was killed by prosecutorial overreach. 35 years for liberating academic papers funded by public money. The system showed exactly what it protects.
- The Anarchist Cookbook is a cultural artifact more than a manual — its real story is about a 19-year-old's rage against the Vietnam draft, a publisher who refused to give back the copyright, and the way the state uses book possession as a guilt proxy.
- Iceland 2008: the one country that let the banks fail, jailed the bankers, and crowd-sourced a new constitution. Every other government chose the banks over the people.
- Anarcho-syndicalism isn't utopian fantasy — it's a concrete model for horizontal organizing that has worked at scale (CNT-FAI in revolutionary Spain, Mondragón cooperatives today).
