# Von Restorff Effect (Isolation Effect)

An item that visually differs from its surrounding peers is disproportionately more memorable and noticed. Use sparingly — its power comes from scarcity.

## Rules of thumb
- One visually distinct element per view maximum for a primary CTA; if everything is highlighted, nothing is (this also collides with [[ux-gestalt-principles]]'s Law of Similarity if overused).
- Use distinctiveness for the single most important action or piece of information per screen, not for decoration.
- Distinctiveness can come from color, size, motion, or isolation (whitespace) — pick one axis and use it consistently across the product for "this is the important thing" so it stays a reliable signal.
- Overuse trains users to ignore highlighting entirely (banner blindness).

## Review checklist
1. Count strongly emphasized elements per screen; if more than one, they're competing for the same attention.
2. Confirm the emphasized element is the one the user should actually act on.
