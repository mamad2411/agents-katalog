# Parkinson's Law + Pareto Principle

Prioritization and pacing group.

## Parkinson's Law
Work expands to fill the time given. Applies to both the user's perceived task duration and the team's own delivery: give a task an artificial time constraint (a visible deadline, a bounded step count, a countdown) and it's perceived as more efficient/urgent than an open-ended one. Use bounded steps/time estimates in flows ("this takes about 2 minutes") to set expectations and prevent drop-off from perceived unboundedness.

## Pareto Principle (80/20)
Most usage concentrates on a small fraction of features/paths. Identify the 20% of flows that drive 80% of usage or pain (via analytics, support tickets, or heuristic judgment) and prioritize fixing/polishing those before edge-case paths. Don't spend equal polish on a rarely-used settings screen and the core daily flow.

## Review checklist
1. Does a long/open-ended flow give the user a time or step estimate?
2. Is fix/design effort concentrated on the highest-traffic or highest-pain paths first?
