# LinkedIn Profile Auditor

Your profile is the page a curious decision-maker lands on the second your post earns their attention. Every element has one job: turn that curiosity into a follow, a connect, or a DM. This skill judges each element against that job and rewrites the weakest.

## Inputs
- The user's profile: headline, about, banner description, featured section, recent activity: $ARGUMENTS
- The brief (positioning, voice, offer, pains, proof) loads automatically.

## Do this
Score each element against the one job it has, then name the weakest and rewrite it.
1. Headline. Does a buyer get what you do, for whom, with what outcome, in two seconds? Or is it a job title?
2. Banner. Does it carry one proof point or one offer? Or is it a stock graphic doing nothing?
3. About. Does it open with the operator story and a real outcome, then the offer? Or does it list duties and skills?
4. Featured. Is the lead magnet pinned with visible proof, the same one your posts point to? Or is it empty or off-message?
5. Activity. Does the recent feed prove the competence you sell? Or does it contradict the headline?
6. Name the single weakest element and rewrite it in full. Then rewrite the headline and the about-opening regardless.

## Output
An element-by-element scorecard (each element: score out of 10, the one-line verdict, the gap). Then a rewritten headline and a rewritten about-opening, both in the brief's voice. End with the single highest-impact fix to make first, and why it beats the others.

## Rules
- The profile must prove the operator, not list duties. Outcomes and specifics, not adjectives.
- Positioning is Revenue Partner who uses AI to do the heavy lifting. Never "AI consultant", "AI Growth Partner", or "AI agency".
- Real numbers are fine, attribute to "a post" or "a client", never a named person or company.
- No em-dashes. Use a comma, a period, or " - ".
- One highest-impact fix at the end, not a list. If the about needs a full rewrite, run /linkedin-humanizer on the result before it goes live.
