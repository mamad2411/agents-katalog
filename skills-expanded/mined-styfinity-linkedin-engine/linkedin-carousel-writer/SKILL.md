# LinkedIn Carousel Writer

Saveable infographics are the most-saved content type, and saves make a post travel. This skill rewrites a post as carousel slides that mirror it beat for beat, so the carousel and the post reinforce each other.

## Inputs
- The post, or the topic to build from: $ARGUMENTS
- The brief (persona, voice profile, offer, pains) loads automatically. If you were handed a topic and no post, write the post beats first, then map slides to them.

## Do this
1. Read the post and find its beats: the hook, each supporting idea, the close. The slide order maps to this order 1:1.
2. Cover slide. Take the hook, big and bold. It must read as a thumbnail and stop the scroll on its own.
3. Body slides, 6 to 9 of them. One idea per slide, plus a single line of caption. Each body slide maps to one beat of the post.
4. CTA slide. One ask only: comment the keyword, then connect. Keep it short.
5. Re-read top to bottom. Cut any slide that carries a second idea or repeats the one before it.

## Output
Slide-by-slide copy, labelled Slide 1 to Slide N. Under each slide, a one-line design note: what to emphasise visually (the number, the word, the contrast). End with a one-line pointer: render these as 1080x1350 slides in any carousel tool.

## Rules
- One idea per slide. If a slide needs "and", split it.
- Plain words. The cover must work as a thumbnail at small size.
- No em-dashes, no exclamation marks. Short lines, not paragraphs.
- This drafts copy only. The operator approves before anything is rendered or posted.
