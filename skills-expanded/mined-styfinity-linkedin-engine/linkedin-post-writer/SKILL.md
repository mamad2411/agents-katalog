# LinkedIn Post Writer

The hero skill. Turns a topic and a shred of proof into a post that earns attention and proves the competence the buyer is buying. Drafts only, never posts.

## Inputs
- The topic or angle, plus any proof you have (a number, a before/after, a build): $ARGUMENTS
- The brief (persona, voice profile, offer, pains) loads automatically.

## Do this
1. Pick the best-fit hook formula from THE TEN, given the topic and proof:
   Pattern-Break, Counter-Intuitive Insight, Experience-Led Observation, Direct Consequence, Confession+Result, Numbered Promise, Enemy-Hero-Gasoline-Teaser, BREAKING/News framing, Stat-Anchored (X% vs Y%), Mechanism-Reveal.
2. Write the hook so the first 210 to 235 characters (everything visible before "see more") carry all four value levers at once: dream outcome up, perceived likelihood up, time-delay down, effort down.
3. Body gives a SYSTEM, not tips: name the parts and show the arrows between them. Include one copy-paste-able artefact line (a prompt, a check, a template line). Quantify one before/after in money and hours. Land one contrarian quotable line.
4. Close with a comment-keyword CTA plus a connect line (e.g. "comment WORD and I'll send it").
5. Total length 900 to 1,300 characters. Count it.

## Output
- The post, ready to read top to bottom.
- The hook formula used (one of THE TEN).
- The visible-before-cut preview (first 210 to 235 chars, exactly what shows before "see more").
- The character count.
- A suggested comment keyword.
- A note: run /linkedin-humanizer next before posting.

## Rules
- Sell the operator, not the artefact: the topic must PROVE the competence the buyer is buying, even when it is not literally the product.
- Outcome + mechanism: name the outcome and reveal the mechanism. AI does the volume, operator judgment picks the targets. Position the operator as a Revenue Partner who uses AI to do the heavy lifting.
- Proof attributes to "a post", "a client", or "an operator", never to a named person or company.
- No em-dashes. No banned hype words.
- Draft only. Pass the draft through /linkedin-humanizer before posting. Nothing auto-posts; the operator approves and publishes.
