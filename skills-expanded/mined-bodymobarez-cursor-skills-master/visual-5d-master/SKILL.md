# Visual 5D — Master Hub (ultra-premium creative)

Produce **agency/Awwwards-tier** visuals: cinematic film looks, photoreal/3D images, and premium
logos. "5D" here is a quality bar — **photoreal depth + 3D form + cinematic lighting + motion** —
grounded in real craft (lensing, color science, 3D, compositing), not a literal video format.

## How to use this hub

1. Pick the medium: video / image / logo.
2. Read the skill; combine with `video-ai-master` (AI generation/render) and `color-design-master`
   (palette/contrast) and `ui-master` (brand) as needed.

## Bundled skills

- **cinematic-5d-video-production** ⭐ — Ultra-premium cinematic video: shot & lens language, 3D camera
  moves, depth/parallax, virtual production, VFX/compositing, color grade, AI generation + Remotion 3D,
  and mastering/delivery. Cross-links `video-ai-master`.  
  → `skills/cinematic-5d-video-production/GUIDE.md`
- **image-5d-pro** ⭐ — Premium image creation: photoreal & 3D-render looks, lighting/composition,
  AI prompt craft, upscaling, retouch, wide-gamut export.  
  → `skills/image-5d-pro/GUIDE.md`
- **logo-5d-pro** ⭐ — Premium logos: concept → vector mastery (SVG) → 3D/extruded/glass/metallic
  treatments → mockups → full brand export pack.  
  → `skills/logo-5d-pro/GUIDE.md`
- **3d-render-motion-graphics** — The "5D" engine: Blender / Spline / Three.js (R3F), materials,
  lighting, render pipeline, and motion graphics.  
  → `skills/3d-render-motion-graphics/GUIDE.md`
- **color-grading-cinematic** — Cinematic grading: log/HDR, LUTs, scopes, grading pipeline (DaVinci /
  ffmpeg), and matching shots for a filmic look.  
  → `skills/color-grading-cinematic/GUIDE.md`
