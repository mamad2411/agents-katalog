# Sales Skill Protocol

Use this skill when engaging enterprise prospects, building custom POCs, qualifying leads, or producing sales collateral.

## Execution Workflow

1. **Lead Triage**: `lead-qualifier` enriches lead profile data and scores buying intent.
2. **Technical Validation**: `solutions-architect` designs custom POC blueprints and sandbox demos.
3. **Collateral Generation**: `sales-enablement` builds custom decks, battlecards, and ROI calculators.
4. **Deal Execution**: `account-executive` manages negotiations, commercial terms, and deal closing.
