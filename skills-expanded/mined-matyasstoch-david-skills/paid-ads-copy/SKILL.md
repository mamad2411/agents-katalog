# Paid Ads Copy

Help David write hooks, scripts, and copy for paid ad creatives.

## Where things live

- `work/ads/` — all ad copy, scripts, hooks, ideas
- `work/ads/ideas-for-creatives.md` — running list of hook ideas (organized by campaign)
- `work/ads/<role-or-campaign>-script.md` — one file per ad script (e.g., `personal-assistant-script.md`)
- `work/ads/local-campaigns.md` — strategic campaign-level notes

## Script structure

Every ad script follows this order:

1. **Hooks** — opening lines that stop the scroll
2. **Requirements** — what David is looking for (in the candidate / customer)
3. **Credibility** — who David is, why they should want this
4. **Objection handling** — what the audience might fear or worry about
5. **Quick-fire list** — concrete examples (tasks, benefits, etc.)

## Writing principles

### Hooks (the first 3 seconds)

- Three hook mechanics that work: **shock/surprise**, **provocative question**, **bold promise**.
- Always write **3-5 hook variants** per ad. One hook change can triple performance.
- Match the hook to audience awareness — cold traffic is problem-aware. Open with the pain or the unexpected, not the product.
- For 9:16 video, follow this arc: **Hook → Pain → Reframe → Emotional contrast → CTA.**

### Damaging admission

- Open with — or include — a small admission of a flaw, limitation, or honest disqualifier. Builds instant credibility and disarms skepticism.
- Examples: "I can't pay you Google money…", "This isn't for everyone…", "I'm not the most experienced founder…", "The job is hard — most people quit in the first month."
- Counter-intuitively, naming the downside upfront makes the upside more believable.

### Requirements section — crystal clear

- The "Requirements" section must be **specific and unambiguous** about who David is looking for.
- **No vague adjectives** ("smart", "hard-working", "passionate"). Use observable criteria — what they do, what they've done, what they can commit to.
- **State who the role is NOT for.** Filtering early is a favor to both sides.

## Constraints

- **⚠️ Meta employment ads: NEVER mention age or gender in the ad copy.** Targeting can be set in the ad manager — the creative itself must be neutral.
- Format default: Meta Ads (FB + IG), 9:16 or 1:1.
- Keep files concise, minimal, well organized.
