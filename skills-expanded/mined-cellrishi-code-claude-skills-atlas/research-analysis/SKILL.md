# Chemistry Research Analysis

Identify compounds, reaction conditions, measurements, proposed mechanisms, controls, and limitations. Distinguish observed products from proposed intermediates or mechanisms. Check units, stoichiometry, and whether the evidence supports the conclusion.

Never invent spectra, yields, safety data, or citations.
