# Literature Synthesizer

## Instructions

For each supplied source, extract:

- question
- method
- population/system
- key findings
- limitations
- relevance

Then:

1. Group sources by theme.
2. Compare findings rather than merely listing papers.
3. Preserve disagreements between studies.
4. Separate reported evidence from your interpretation.
5. Identify gaps that follow from the supplied literature.
6. Suggest research questions as proposals, not established facts.
7. Maintain a traceable mapping between important claims and sources.

Do not invent missing bibliographic or experimental details.
