## The Content System

### Daily Workflow (30 minutes)

1. **Curate (10 min):** AI scans 50+ sources (RSS, GitHub trends, arXiv, HackerNews)
2. **Draft (10 min):** AI generates newsletter from curated highlights using template
3. **Review (5 min):** Human adds commentary, context, personal voice
4. **Schedule (5 min):** Queue for optimal send time

### The S.H.A.R.E. Content Framework (adapted for AI newsletters)

- **Surprising:** One counter-intuitive insight per issue
- **Helpful:** Actionable tool, tactic, or resource
- **Authentic:** Human commentary, not just AI summary
- **Relevant:** Tied to subscriber's current challenges
- **Experiences:** Story or case study with emotional hook

## Four Revenue Streams

### 1. Sponsorships ($2,000-$5,000/month)
- Open-source tools, dev tools, AI platforms
- Privacy-preserving companies align with A-Tech values
- Rate: $50-$100 CPM for niche tech audiences

### 2. Paid Subscriptions ($1,000-$3,000/month)
- Premium tier: Deep dives, source code, early access
- Community access: Private Discord/forum
- Pricing psychology: $10/month anchors value; annual saves 20%

### 3. Affiliate Revenue ($500-$1,500/month)
- Tools you genuinely use and recommend
- Open-source hosting (Hetzner referral), courses, books
- Disclose all affiliations (trust = retention)

### 4. Product/Service Sales ($1,000-$5,000/month)
- Templates, playbooks, workshops
- Consulting or implementation services
- Natural extension of newsletter authority

## Ethical Boundaries

- Always disclose AI assistance in content
- Never recommend tools you haven't tested
- Respect subscriber privacy (no data selling, no tracking pixels)
- Cancel-friendly (easy unsubscribe, no dark patterns)
- Quality over quantity (one great issue beats three mediocre)
