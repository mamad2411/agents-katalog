# The AI Digital-Product Playbook: Platform Economics for Solo Creators

## Overview
The geographic-arbitrage companion to the library's service-side playbooks (`ai-copilot-saas-solo-build`, `passive-income-ranked-2026`): the **asset-based** model where you build once and sell indefinitely. The 2026 economics: a disciplined solo operator can build **$3K–$8K/month** in digital-product income in 60–90 days on a **$45–$77/month** tool stack, with zero inventory and full location portability (FEIE excludes up to ~$126.5K for qualifying expats — the tax layer that turns a $60K product business into a 0%-federal one).

## The five product types, ranked by revenue-per-hour
| Product | Monthly range | Build cost | The workflow |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Notion templates** | $1K–$3.5K | ~4 hrs (Claude generates schema → build in Notion → Loom walkthrough) | Business OS, finance trackers, client portals at $15–$49 |
| **Canva bundles** | $1.5K–$5K | ~1 weekend | 30–50 templates for a hyper-specific niche at $27–$67 |
| **AI prompt packs** | $300–$2K | ~1 day | 25–50 prompts, themed, $19–$49 on Gumroad; output screenshots are the marketing |
| **Mini-courses** | $2K–$8K | 3 weekends | 5–10 short videos at $47–$197; case: 5-module Etsy-SEO course, 400 buyers × $67 = $26.8K |
| **eBooks/swipe files** | $800–$3K | ~20 min draft + editing | $17–$37 singles; bundle 3 at $67–$97 |

## The platform ladder (the sequencing is the strategy)
1. **Etsy first** — 95M+ buyers searching "Notion template" daily; 6.5% + $0.20/listing; algorithmic boosts for new listings. Zero-audience distribution.
2. **Gumroad at 25+ reviews** — route social traffic; keep more per sale (10% + $0.50).
3. **Payhip above $97** — 0% fee at $99/mo beats percentage fees at volume.

Support layer: Mercury for fee-free international banking; a virtual US mailbox ($15–25/mo) for platforms requiring a US address.

## The 60-day sequence
- **Days 1–7:** keyword research before building — newest listings with 50+ sales are your product brief.
- **Days 8–14:** build three related products + a bundle priced 20–30% above the sum of parts (bundles are the top converter).
- **Days 15–21:** launch on Etsy with exact-keyword titles, all 13 tags, 10 mockups; price 10–15% below target for velocity.
- **Days 22–45:** 2 new listings/week minimum; parallel Gumroad build; Pinterest for long-shelf-life traffic.
- **Days 46–60:** bundle best-sellers, email buyers for reviews/repeat purchases.

## The honest trajectory (Medellín cost base, $1,400–1,600/mo)
Month 1: $400 gross → $321 net. Month 3: $2,400 → $2,141. Month 6: $5,500 → $4,983 (3.3× cost of living). Month 12: $9,200+ → $8,387+. **The first 90 days are inventory-building, not passive collection.**

## Three failure patterns (the guide's honest list)
1. **Building for yourself, not the buyer** — fix with keyword research first.
2. **Underpricing to compete** — a $5 bundle needs 400 sales/mo to hit $2K; at $37 it needs 54, and higher prices often convert *better* (quality signal).
3. **One listing and waiting** — shops earning $3K+/month average 30–80 listings; passive income is the aggregate.

## What no longer works (cross-checked with the 2026 side-hustle canon)
Generic prompt packs as a product category are saturating; raw AI output is a race to zero because buyers can generate it too; the premium goes to **AI + domain pairing** (Upwork's Future Workforce Index: +34%/hour premium is for AI+domain pairing, not prompting). The durable version of every method here is taste, niche specificity, and accountability — the model supplies scaffolding.
