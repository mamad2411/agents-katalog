# OSS Endowments & Co-ops: The Structural Funding Legs

*(Core skill established in prior cycles. This cycle adds a watch-item update.)*

## Cycle-20 addendum (2026-09-06): the AI-copyright stress test arrives

Sony and Warner Music have sued Anthropic over training on copyrighted material (per the AIToolsRecap K2 Horizon analysis, Sept 5, 2026; a federal court has already held that acquiring training material through piracy is unlawful). The open-source monetization consequence: **publishing training data is simultaneously the openness act and the legal-exposure act** — which is precisely why IFM's K2 Horizon full-lifecycle release (data + recipes where licenses permit) is expensive *and* verifiable, and why most labs stop at open weights. For the funding family, the connection: endowment and co-op structures fund *maintenance and governance*, but the **liability surface of open artifacts is shifting** — training-data disclosure duties (EU AI Act Art. 53(1)(a), from Aug 2027) and copyright litigation risk now price differently for projects that ship data/weights versus code-only. Endowment agreements for AI-asset projects should henceforth include a **liability-surface clause** (what the endowment covers if the shipped artifact becomes litigation exposure). **Watch:** whether any major endowment (Sovereign Tech Fund successors, Digital Commons EDIC grantees) publishes a copyright/liability policy for funded AI-model projects — first mover sets the template.

## Standing core (retained)

- **Endowment leg:** community-governed perpetual income (~5% draw) — fits public-good projects with broad beneficiary bases; governance board controls disbursement; slow to establish, durable once funded.
- **Cooperative leg:** member-owned platform economics — fits tools whose users are also their operators; aligns pricing power with the community; slower capital growth.
- **In-kind compute leg (CodeRabbit addendum, cycle 18):** >$10M direct-cost agentic support for all public GitHub repos — transparent cost tracker, multi-vendor redundancy, and agent-triage-as-pre-gate as the tie-to-roadmap mitigations.
- **The funding-quintet map:** endowment (perpetual, governed) / co-op (member control) / employment (Rust MiR — salaried, funded-but-project-selected) / patron (Omacom — founder-controlled, key-person risk) / standards-participation (Sovereign Tech Standards — funds governance presence, anti-capture by design). Each leg targets a distinct failure mode; portfolio, noteither/or.
