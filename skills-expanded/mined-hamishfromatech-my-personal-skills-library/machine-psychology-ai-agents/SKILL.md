# Skill: Machine Psychology & Behavioral AI Agent Design

## Overview
An emerging interdisciplinary field that applies behavioral science methods to analyze, design, and improve how AI agents behave and interact with humans. Focuses on systematic observation of behavior, intervention design, and theory-guided interpretation.

## Source Research
- "Machine Psychology" emerging as a formal field (2025) applying behavioral science to AI
- AI Agent Behavioral Science emphasizes external behavior observation over internal mechanism analysis
- Integrating behavioral science into AI agents: nudging, habit formation, personalization, cognitive load management, trust-building
- Human-centered AI requires warmth, social presence, transparency, and consistency
- Foot-in-the-door technique and anthropomorphic design increase user compliance and satisfaction
- Empathetic language and responsive tone-adjustment build trust in customer service bots

## Core Principles
1. **Behavioral Observation**: Study what AI agents do externally, not just their internal parameters
2. **Systematic Intervention**: Design changes to agent behavior, test hypotheses, measure outcomes
3. **Theory-Guided Design**: Apply established psychology (cognitive load, social presence, persuasion)
4. **Warmth & Social Presence**: Agents with names, first-person language, empathy, and small talk outperform task-only agents
5. **Transparency Builds Trust**: Explain reasoning, admit uncertainty, offer human handoff when needed
6. **Consistency**: Stable personality and reliable functionality are essential for trust
7. **Ethical Boundaries**: Nudges must serve user goals; avoid manipulation or addiction design

## Implementation Checklist

- [ ] Build human handoff triggers for uncertain situations
