# Multimodal Behavioral Engagement Inference Framework

## Overview

Digital marketing analytics capture behavioral outcomes (clicks, dwell time, conversions) but offer limited insight into how engagement unfolds during interaction. This framework reconceptualizes engagement as a dynamic, temporally evolving process marked by continuous fluctuations in attention, cognitive effort, affective activation, and behavioral adaptation — measured through multimodal behavioral signals rather than aggregated outcome metrics.

## Key Research Findings

**Source:** De Sainte Maresville, Petr, Haggui & Restrepo (IAE Bretagne Sud / Odaptos, Journal of Marketing Analytics, July 2026)

### Core Innovation: Process-Oriented Engagement Measurement

The framework transforms heterogeneous behavioral signals (gaze, facial expressions, vocal characteristics, linguistic cues, micro-gestures) into theory-driven indicators of engagement, with explicit attention to:
- **Temporal structure** — examining whether associations hold at segment-level temporal resolution, not just session aggregates
- **Measurement overlap** — testing whether indicators built from overlapping signals generate spurious associations
- **Construct validity** — treating engagement indicators as behavioral inferences, not direct measures of psychological states

### Three Engagement Dimensions

1. **Attention-related indicators** — allocation and stability of cognitive resources
2. **Stress-related activation** — arousal and strain generated by task demands
3. **Regulatory activity** — behavioral adjustment toward restoring goal-directed processing

### Two Distinct Engagement Outcomes

- **Attentional stabilization** — sustained cognitive allocation facilitating systematic processing
- **Intentional engagement** — approach-oriented, goal-directed engagement

### Central Finding: Asymmetric Robustness

The most significant finding is the **asymmetric robustness** of stress-related activation's associations:

- **Stress → Attentional stabilization**: Robustly NEGATIVE across collinearity diagnostics, bootstrap resampling, leave-one-out estimation, alternative model specifications, gender/duration controls, AND feature-subset reconstruction. This association survives every robustness check.

- **Stress → Intentional engagement**: Positive in original specification but **substantially weaker** once shared gaze and video features are removed. Collapses to near-zero (r=0.015 to 0.179, all non-significant) in 4 of 5 feature-subset reconstructions. This is a **measurement-dependent finding**, not an established behavioral pattern.

### Temporal Ordering Limitation

Neither association holds at genuine temporal lag (segment t → t+1 or t+2). All findings are **contemporaneous**, not sequential. The present data cannot establish whether stress precedes changes in attentional stabilization or intentional engagement.

## Practical Framework

### The Six Multimodal Engagement Tags

| Tag | Modalities | What It Captures | Classification Criteria |
|-----|-----------|------------------|------------------------|
| ATTENTION_HIGH | Gaze, posture, gesture | Sustained attentional allocation | ≥3 of: fixation duration in attentive range, low gaze entropy, limited scanpath transitions, stable postural activity |
| STRESS | Voice, face, gaze, text | Cognitive strain under task demands | ≥2 of: elevated arousal/vocal intensity, negative valence/facial tension, gaze instability, stress-related linguistic markers |
| FIXATION | Gaze, affective signals | Concentrated attentional capture | Prolonged fixation + elevated affective co-activation |
| INTENTION_POS | Text, voice, face, gaze | Approach-oriented engagement | ≥2 modalities converging toward approach (≥1 semantic/affective indicator required) |
| INTENTION_NEG | Text, voice, face, gaze | Avoidance-oriented engagement | Multiple modalities indicating withdrawal/avoidance |
| REGULATION_ACTIVE | Voice, text, gaze, face | Behavioral self-regulation/recovery | Reorganization following prior activation: reduced arousal, gaze restabilization, verbal self-correction |

### Key Design Principles

1. **Multimodal convergence**: No tag assigned based on a single cue; requires convergent evidence from ≥2 modalities
2. **Theory-driven interpretation**: Indicators identified from prior literature, thresholds calibrated ex ante
3. **Conservative assignment**: Segments with weak/contradictory signals remain untagged
4. **Temporal context**: REGULATION_ACTIVE requires temporal context (occurring after activation)

### Robustness Diagnostic Procedure

This is the framework's methodological contribution — a replicable procedure for evaluating whether behaviorally inferred indicators reflect genuine dynamics or measurement artifacts:

1. **Collinearity diagnostics**: VIF, condition indices
2. **Bootstrap resampling**: 5000-sample confidence intervals
3. **Leave-one-out estimation**: No sign reversal, no single session driving results
4. **Alternative model specifications**: Fractional logit with robust SE for bounded proportional outcomes
5. **Feature-overlap analysis**: Reconstruct indicators from disjoint feature subsets
6. **Segment-level temporal testing**: Participant-clustered logistic regression, permutation tests

### Managerial Implications

- **Attentional stabilization signal is more dependable** — it withstood every robustness check
- **Intentional engagement link is provisional** — treat as pending external validation, not basis for operational decisions
- **Users may remain involved even as attentional continuity decreases** — caution against attention-only engagement monitoring
- **Indicators should function as decision-support inputs** that complement, not replace, established performance metrics

## A-Tech Alignment

- **Open-source**: Framework is fully transparent and reproducible; complete gesture-to-category mapping published
- **Data privacy**: Behavioral inference from multimodal signals can be processed on-device, avoiding centralized biometric data collection
- **Financial freedom**: Better engagement measurement enables more effective open-source product marketing and community building
- **Practical implementation**: Provides concrete tag definitions, classification criteria, and robustness diagnostic procedures

## Limitations

- Controlled, interview-based context; generalization to e-commerce/social media requires direct testing
- Indicators remain behavioral inferences, not validated psychological measures
- Partial convergent validity check (blind human coding) showed modest correspondence (r=0.367 for STRESS, r=0.307 for INTENTION_POS)
- No self-report, physiological, or independent marketing-outcome criterion available
- Study 1 and Study 2 draw on substantially overlapping samples (82.5-95% overlap)
- External validation against independent engagement criteria is the necessary next step
