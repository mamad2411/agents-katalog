core_concept: |
  The most powerful and effortless LinkedIn content strategy is not templated hooks, 
  viral frameworks, or AI-generated posts. It is "unsolicited advice" — writing as if 
  you are sitting across the table from your dream client, telling them what you wish 
  every prospect understood, the mistakes they keep making, and the advice you find 
  yourself repeating every week. This approach produces authentic, conviction-driven 
  content that converts silent lurkers into paying clients without sounding like everyone else.

problem_statement: |
  Founder content fails because it sounds like content:
  - Hooks engineered for the algorithm
  - Frameworks borrowed from courses
  - Templated posts that fit the format and forget the message
  - The audience scrolls past because the post sounds like everyone else's post
  
  The result: generic posts that get some likes but convert almost nobody into clients.

strategy:
  name: "The Unsolicited Advice Strategy"
  
  core_prompt: |
    Imagine sitting across the table from your dream client. They ask what they should do. 
    You speak with conviction. You tell them:
    - What you wish every prospect understood before they spoke to you
    - The mistakes they keep making
    - The patterns you keep seeing
    - The advice you repeat every week
    - The misconceptions they show up with
    - The habits they fall into
    - What they are missing
    - The industry-specific fears
    - How they achieve their goals
    - What you didn't know at their stage
    - What you learned from others like you
    - Tough love
    - The fastest way to overcome the challenge
    
    That is your content. That is the strategy.

  execution_steps:
    - id: 1
      title: "Define Your Dream Client"
      description: |
        Picture the buyer who would gladly pay you tomorrow. The one who needs exactly 
        what you provide. The one whose business would change if they followed your advice.
    
    - id: 2
      title: "Capture Your Unsolicited Advice"
      description: |
        Make a running list of the things you wish every prospect knew before they booked a call. 
        Speak the answer out loud. Record yourself if it helps. The way you talk to them — with 
        conviction, stories, and specifics — is exactly the voice that should be in your content.
        If you stop talking like that the moment you open the laptop, your content sounds different 
        from the way you actually think. Bring the table conversation to the keyboard. Stop hiding 
        the version of your voice you use with clients.
        The list usually runs to fifty or a hundred items once you start writing. Each one becomes 
        a LinkedIn post, an article, a podcast episode, a YouTube short. You will never run out 
        of material because the list grows faster than you can publish it.
    
    - id: 3
      title: "Mine Your Existing Conversations"
      description: |
        The content that works hardest is the topics that come up in every client conversation. 
        Sources to pull from:
        - "Ask me anything" sessions with clients
        - Your YouTube channel sorted by views
        - Google Analytics showing top blog traffic
        - Emails you receive
        - WhatsApp messages people send
        
        What they want to know is what your LinkedIn audience wants to know too. You cannot 
        ignore frequency. When you're known for a specific topic you have to exploit it. 
        This makes easy content because you're already answering these questions.

writing_principles:
  - id: 1
    name: "Write for the Lurkers, Never the Likers"
    description: |
      Most founders judge their content by likes and comments. The likes and comments come from 
      a small percentage of the audience. The bigger audience is silent. They watch every post. 
      They make decisions based on what they read. They never engage publicly.
      The lurkers become your clients. They follow you for months without commenting. They read 
      every article without replying. They reach out one day, ready to buy, and you have no idea 
      who they are because you never saw them in your notifications.
      Write for them. The content that sounds like a real person giving real advice converts the 
      silent majority. The content engineered for likes converts almost nobody.
  
  - id: 2
    name: "Trust the Reader to Keep Up"
    description: |
      Founder content fails when it over-explains. The reader is smart. The reader has been in 
      business for years. The reader does not need every concept defined and every term unpacked. 
      Over-explaining makes the post feel slow and the reader scrolls.
      Write the way you would talk to a peer. Drop the buyer into the middle of the idea. Trust 
      them to follow. Cut the setups. Cut the throat clearing. Cut the obvious points. The post 
      gets shorter, sharper, and more memorable. The reader feels respected and the content lands harder.
  
  - id: 3
    name: "Stop Researching the Perfect Formula and Start Writing the Way You Actually Speak"
    description: |
      The voice that comes out is the voice that builds a real personal brand because it sounds 
      like you and nobody else.
