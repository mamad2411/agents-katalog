# Company Strategy

Research a company's strategy using the most recent public materials.

## What to use

- Latest 10-K or annual report
- Earnings call transcripts or investor letters
- Investor day or analyst day materials when available
- LinkedIn company overview
- Recent press or investor relations updates

## Output

- 1-2 sentences describing what the company does and its market position
- 1-2 short paragraphs on strategy, priorities, and product focus
- `Source hints:` including the 10-K fiscal year when cited

## Rules

- Use the most recent fiscal year and latest company materials.
- Keep the tone factual and neutral.
- If the user provides a product or role focus, use it to prioritize which signals to emphasize.
