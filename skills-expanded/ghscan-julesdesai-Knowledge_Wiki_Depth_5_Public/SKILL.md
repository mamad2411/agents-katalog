# Virtue Epistemology with Virtue as Intellectual Skill

**Node Type:** synthesis
**Node ID:** 1eb03568-927f-4f34-9b70-76c794e3a52f
**Depth:** 5
**Terminal:** No

**Parent:** [Is intellectual virtue necessary for knowledge in all cases?](is-intellectual-virtue-necessary-for-knowledge-in-all-cases-2.md)

## Content

1. **For a person to know a proposition, it is necessary that they believe the proposition**
2. **For a person to know a proposition, it is necessary that the proposition is true**
3. **For a person to know a proposition, it is necessary that the belief is the result of the person's intellectual virtue**
4. **An intellectual virtue is an intellectual skill that can be applied effectively in various situations to form true beliefs**
