## Goal

Ask for a mental health day professionally while protecting privacy, work continuity, and trust.

## Preconditions

- You need time away from work for mental health, stress, burnout, recovery, or medical self-care.
- You know your workplace's sick leave, PTO, or absence reporting process.
- You have identified urgent work that needs coverage or can wait.

## Steps

1. **Check the leave channel.** Confirm whether your workplace requires a manager message, HR system entry, calendar block, or sick line. → *Expect:* you know where the request must go.
2. **Decide how much to disclose.** Use a simple line such as "I need to take a sick day today" or "I need a mental health day and will be offline." → *Expect:* your privacy boundary is clear.
3. **Send the request early.** Message your manager before your workday starts if possible. → *Expect:* your absence is visible before others depend on you.
4. **Cover urgent work.** Name what is blocked, what can wait, and who can answer urgent questions. → *Expect:* the team can keep critical work moving.
5. **Set an offline boundary.** [BRANCH: fully offline | limited availability] state you will be offline; if limited availability is required, define one short check-in window. → *Expect:* people know whether they can contact you.
6. **Update systems.** Enter leave in HR, mark calendar as unavailable, and adjust meeting responses. → *Expect:* the absence appears in official and practical calendars.
7. **Use a short return note.** When back, say "I'm back today and catching up; please flag anything urgent." → *Expect:* colleagues know you have returned.
8. **Review recurring causes.** If mental health days are becoming frequent, consider workload discussion, benefits, employee assistance, or medical support. → *Expect:* the absence is not the only intervention.

## Decision points

- If company policy does not require details → do not provide diagnosis, treatment, or personal history.
- If a deadline is at risk → identify the decision or handoff needed before you go offline.
- If you need more than one day → ask about sick leave, medical leave, accommodation, or HR process.
- If you feel unsafe or in crisis → seek immediate local emergency or crisis support rather than treating it as only a work scheduling issue.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Oversharing:** detect a long explanation of symptoms or personal events → shorten to leave type, date, and coverage.
- **F2 Manager pushes for details:** detect questions beyond policy need → say you prefer to keep medical details private and ask what documentation is required.
- **F3 Work boundary fails:** detect repeated pings after you went offline → redirect to the named backup or manager.

## Verification

Your manager or required system has the leave notice, your calendar shows unavailable, urgent work has a coverage note, and your privacy-sensitive details are not broadly shared.

## Variations

- Hourly or shift work: follow call-out timing exactly and record confirmation.
- Client-facing role: coordinate coverage before notifying clients.
- Recurring need: discuss leave, workload, or accommodation options with HR or a healthcare professional.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because health privacy, employment, and workload may be involved. Share the minimum needed for leave approval and seek urgent help if there is risk of self-harm or harm to others.
