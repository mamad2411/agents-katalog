## Goal

Coordinate an interview panel with the right interviewers, times, format, and evaluation coverage.

## Preconditions

- The candidate is approved for a panel interview.
- Interviewers, interview topics, and interview length are known.
- You have access to calendars, scheduling tools, or the applicant tracking system.

## Steps

1. **Confirm interview plan.** Verify panel members, interview focus areas, duration, format, and target date range. → *Expect:* the interview loop has clear coverage and owners.
2. **Check interviewer availability.** Compare calendars or scheduling links for the panel and recruiter. → *Expect:* at least one workable time block is identified.
3. **Check candidate availability.** Use existing availability or request time windows from the candidate. → *Expect:* candidate availability overlaps with panel availability.
4. **Reserve rooms or meeting links.** Book physical rooms, video links, or phone bridges for each segment. → *Expect:* every interview segment has a location or link.
5. **Create calendar holds.** Add events with candidate name, role, interview focus, resume link if appropriate, and feedback deadline. → *Expect:* interviewers receive calendar invitations.
6. **Update the ATS.** Record the interview schedule, panel assignments, and candidate status. → *Expect:* recruiting records match the calendar.
7. **Send internal prep.** Share rubric, interview plan, and candidate materials with interviewers. → *Expect:* interviewers know what they are evaluating.

## Decision points

- If an interviewer has a conflict → find an equivalent trained interviewer or reschedule the panel.
- If candidate availability is limited → prioritize critical interviewers and avoid unnecessary delays.
- If the role requires accommodations → coordinate the schedule and format through the appropriate confidential process.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Calendar mismatch:** detect a calendar invite that differs from the ATS schedule → update both systems to the same time.
- **F2 Missing interviewer:** detect an unaccepted or declined invite → replace the interviewer or reschedule that segment.
- **F3 No room or link:** detect blank location details → add a valid room, video link, or dial-in before confirming.

## Verification

All interviewers and the candidate have matching calendar invitations, the ATS shows the scheduled panel, and each segment has an assigned evaluation area.

## Variations

- US: avoid asking availability questions that reveal protected information beyond scheduling needs.
- Other countries: account for local holidays, works councils, and data-sharing rules.
- Onsite panel: add reception, travel, accessibility, and building entry details.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk, but candidate materials and schedules are confidential. Share resumes and feedback links only with interview participants and keep interview criteria fair and job-related.
