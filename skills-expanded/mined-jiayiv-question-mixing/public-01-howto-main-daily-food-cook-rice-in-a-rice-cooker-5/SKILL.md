## Goal

Rice is rinsed, cooked in a rice cooker, rested, and fluffed without scorching or wet pockets.

## Preconditions

- Rice, rice cooker with inner pot, matching measuring cup if available, water, and rice paddle.
- Stable counter space near an outlet away from sink splashes.

## Steps

1. **Measure rice.** Add rice with the cooker cup or a standard cup, noting the amount. → *Expect:* rice quantity matches a cooker water line or known ratio.
2. **Rinse in the inner pot or bowl.** Swirl with cold water and drain 2-4 times. → *Expect:* water changes from milky to lightly cloudy.
3. **Add water.** Fill to the matching line for that rice type, or use the package ratio. [BRANCH: white rice | brown rice] → *Expect:* rice is level and water sits at the correct mark.
4. **Seat the pot.** Dry the outside of the inner pot and place it flat in the cooker. → *Expect:* pot contacts the heating plate and no water drips into the cooker body.
5. **Start cooking.** Close the lid and press the cook or rice setting. → *Expect:* indicator shows cooking and steam begins later in the cycle.
6. **Let it switch and rest.** When the cooker changes to warm, wait 10 minutes without opening. → *Expect:* steam settles and the top grains look plump.
7. **Fluff gently.** Open away from your face and fold rice with a paddle. → *Expect:* grains separate or cling as intended with no crunchy center.
8. **Hold or store.** Keep warm only for serving time, or refrigerate leftovers in shallow containers within 2 hours. → *Expect:* rice is either hot for service or cooling quickly below 4 °C.

## Decision points

- Cooker has a dedicated brown, sushi, or porridge setting → use that setting and its line.
- Rice is still wet after rest → close lid and keep warm 5-10 minutes, then fluff again.
- Rice will be used for fried rice → cool quickly and refrigerate uncovered until steam stops, then cover.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Crunchy rice:** detect hard centers → add 2-4 tbsp hot water, restart or keep warm 10 minutes.
- **F2 Mushy rice:** detect broken wet grains → use for porridge and reduce water next batch.
- **F3 Scorched bottom:** detect burnt smell or dark crust → unplug, transfer unburnt top without scraping, and clean after soaking.
- **F4 Overflow:** detect starch foam from the vent → unplug if needed, wipe after cooling, rinse rice better next time.

## Verification

Rice is tender through the center, evenly hydrated from top to bottom, not burnt, and leftovers are cooled or held safely.

## Variations

- `sushi-rice`: rinse thoroughly and use the sushi line or slightly less water.
- `brown-rice`: use the brown setting or extra water and time.
- `mixed-grain`: follow the grain package ratio because cooker lines may not apply.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk from steam, hot surfaces, and cooked rice storage. Open lids away from your face and refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours to reduce bacterial growth.
