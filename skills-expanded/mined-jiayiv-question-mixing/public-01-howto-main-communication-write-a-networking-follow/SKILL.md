## Goal

Write a networking follow-up that reminds the person who you are, preserves the relationship, and makes any ask easy to answer.

## Preconditions

- A recent conversation, event, introduction, interview, panel, or professional exchange.
- The person's correct name and contact channel.
- A clear reason to follow up or a genuine thank-you.

## Steps

1. **Follow up promptly.** Send the note within 24-48 hours while the interaction is still memorable. → *Expect:* the recipient can place you.
2. **Use a clear subject.** Write a subject like "Great meeting you at the product analytics event" or "Thank you for the intro." → *Expect:* the message is recognizable.
3. **Remind them of context.** Mention where you met and one specific topic you discussed. → *Expect:* the note does not feel generic.
4. **Add useful value.** Share a promised link, article, contact, or concise reflection from the conversation. → *Expect:* the follow-up gives before it asks.
5. **Make one ask if needed.** [BRANCH: thank-you only | specific ask] close warmly for thank-you only; ask one bounded question, introduction, or 20-minute chat for a specific ask. → *Expect:* the recipient can respond easily.
6. **Keep it short.** Limit the note to a few short paragraphs and remove life story details. → *Expect:* the message is easy to read on mobile.
7. **Offer an easy out.** Use "If now is not a good time, no worries" when asking for time or help. → *Expect:* the relationship stays low-pressure.
8. **Set a reminder.** If you made an ask, note when to follow up once, usually after one week. → *Expect:* you do not accidentally pester them.

## Decision points

- If the person offered something specific → reference that offer directly and make the next step easy.
- If you are asking for a job referral → include role link, resume, and why you fit.
- If the interaction was brief → keep the ask smaller or make it a thank-you only.
- If they do not respond → follow up once, then stop unless there is a new reason.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Message feels generic:** detect no specific shared context → add one detail from the conversation.
- **F2 Ask is too big:** detect requests for broad mentorship or many introductions → reduce to one concrete question or call.
- **F3 Too many follow-ups:** detect more than one unanswered nudge → stop and preserve the relationship.

## Verification

The follow-up includes context, a specific reference, optional useful value, no more than one clear ask, and a respectful close.

## Variations

- After a conference: mention session, booth, or topic and connect within two days.
- After an introduction: thank both people and move scheduling to the introduced contact.
- After an informational interview: send thanks plus one thing you learned and one update you will act on.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Do not share private contact information, confidential job-search details, or another person's name as a referral without permission.
