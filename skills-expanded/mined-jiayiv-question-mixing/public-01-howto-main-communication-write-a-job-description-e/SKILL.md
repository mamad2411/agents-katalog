## Goal

Write a job description that accurately explains the role, attracts qualified candidates, and supports a fair hiring process.

## Preconditions

- Approval to hire or draft a role.
- Clarity on level, manager, team, location, compensation approach, and hiring process.
- Input from the hiring manager and people who understand the work.

## Steps

1. **Define the business need.** Write why the role exists and what problem the hire will solve in the next 6-12 months. → *Expect:* the description is anchored in real work.
2. **List core responsibilities.** Include 5-8 recurring responsibilities and expected outcomes, not every possible task. → *Expect:* candidates can picture the job.
3. **Separate must-haves from nice-to-haves.** Keep must-haves limited to skills truly needed on day one. → *Expect:* requirements do not screen out qualified candidates unnecessarily.
4. **Describe success.** Add first-year outcomes such as "launch monthly reporting cadence" or "reduce ticket backlog by 30%." → *Expect:* performance expectations are concrete.
5. **Use inclusive, plain language.** Remove jargon, inflated credentials, gender-coded wording, and unnecessary years of experience. → *Expect:* the posting is readable and fair.
6. **Add logistics and range.** [BRANCH: remote | on-site] state timezone and travel expectations for remote roles; state location, schedule, and physical requirements for on-site roles. → *Expect:* candidates know constraints before applying.
7. **Review for legal and policy fit.** Check equal opportunity language, pay transparency rules, and internal approval requirements. → *Expect:* the posting can be published responsibly.
8. **Align the interview plan.** Map each requirement to an interview question, exercise, or evidence source. → *Expect:* hiring evaluates what the description promises.

## Decision points

- If a credential is preferred but not required → move it to nice-to-have or remove it.
- If compensation must be public in the role's location → include the approved range and basis.
- If the role is backfill → update stale responsibilities instead of copying the old posting.
- If the role spans multiple jobs → narrow the scope or split the hire.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Laundry-list requirements:** detect more than 10 requirements → cut to day-one essentials and trainable skills.
- **F2 Misleading scope:** detect responsibilities outside the approved level → recalibrate with the hiring manager.
- **F3 Unfair screening:** detect requirements that do not map to job performance → remove or justify them with evidence.

## Verification

The job description includes role purpose, responsibilities, required qualifications, nice-to-haves, success outcomes, logistics, approved range or policy language, and an interview plan mapping.

## Variations

- Internal posting: include reporting line, level, and transfer process.
- Executive role: add mandate, leadership scope, and board or stakeholder exposure.
- Contract role: include duration, deliverables, rate basis, and renewal conditions.

## Safety & privacy

Medium legal and reputational risk. Follow applicable employment, pay transparency, accessibility, and anti-discrimination requirements before publishing.
