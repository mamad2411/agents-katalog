## Goal

Collect structured interview feedback from interviewers before the hiring decision meeting.

## Preconditions

- The interview has occurred or is ending.
- Interviewers have access to the feedback form or applicant tracking system.
- The evaluation rubric is available.

## Steps

1. **Open the interview record.** Locate the candidate, role, interview stage, and assigned interviewers. → *Expect:* the feedback status for each interviewer is visible.
2. **Send feedback reminders.** Ask interviewers to submit feedback by the agreed deadline before group discussion. → *Expect:* interviewers receive a clear reminder.
3. **Check rubric completion.** Review whether ratings, evidence notes, and recommendation fields are filled in. → *Expect:* each submitted form contains structured feedback.
4. **Request missing evidence.** Ask interviewers to replace vague labels with job-related observations from the interview. → *Expect:* notes support the rating with examples.
5. **Lock or mark complete.** When all required forms are submitted, mark feedback collection complete if the system supports it. → *Expect:* the candidate record shows feedback ready for review.
6. **Prepare decision summary.** Summarize submitted ratings, strengths, concerns, and unresolved questions without adding unsupported opinions. → *Expect:* the hiring team has a neutral overview.

## Decision points

- If an interviewer missed the interview → mark the feedback as not applicable and notify recruiting.
- If feedback mentions protected traits → remove or quarantine improper comments according to HR policy and coach the interviewer.
- If feedback conflicts sharply → schedule a debrief focused on evidence and role criteria.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Late feedback:** detect missing forms after the deadline → send a direct reminder and escalate to the hiring manager if needed.
- **F2 Biased feedback:** detect protected-class comments or stereotypes → involve HR and replace with job-related evidence where possible.
- **F3 Groupthink risk:** detect feedback submitted after debrief discussion → note timing and ask for independent evidence.

## Verification

All required interviewers have submitted rubric-based feedback, missing forms are accounted for, and the candidate record is ready for a hiring decision.

## Variations

- US: preserve EEO-sensitive information separately and base decisions on job-related evidence.
- Other countries: follow local retention, works council, and candidate access rules for interview notes.
- Agency recruiting: confirm which feedback can be shared externally before sending summaries.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because interview notes influence employment decisions and may contain sensitive candidate data. Limit access to the hiring team, avoid discriminatory comments, and keep notes factual and job-related.
