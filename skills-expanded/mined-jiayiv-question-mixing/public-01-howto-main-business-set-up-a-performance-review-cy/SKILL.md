## Goal

Launch a performance review cycle with the correct participants, timeline, forms, and calibration steps.

## Preconditions

- Review cycle scope, dates, rating model, and eligible population are approved.
- You have access to the performance management system.
- Managers and HR partners know their responsibilities.

## Steps

1. **Create the cycle.** Enter cycle name, review period, launch date, due dates, and eligible employee population. → *Expect:* the draft cycle appears in the performance system.
2. **Select review forms.** Attach self-review, manager review, peer review, goals, or competency forms as approved. → *Expect:* each participant type has the correct form.
3. **Configure workflow.** Set routing for employee, manager, second-level manager, HR, and calibration if applicable. → *Expect:* the process order is visible.
4. **Check participant list.** Review inclusions, exclusions, recent hires, leaves, transfers, and terminated employees. → *Expect:* the roster matches eligibility rules.
5. **Add communications.** Prepare launch, reminder, and deadline messages with links and support contacts. → *Expect:* scheduled messages are ready.
6. **Test the cycle.** Preview the employee and manager experience using a test or admin view. → *Expect:* forms, deadlines, and routing work as intended.
7. **Launch the cycle.** Start the review cycle after confirming configuration and roster. → *Expect:* participants receive tasks or notifications.

## Decision points

- If eligibility is disputed → resolve with HR before launch.
- If ratings affect pay or promotion → add calibration, documentation, and approval controls.
- If employees are on leave → follow local and company policy before assigning tasks.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Wrong population:** detect missing or extra employees → pause communications and correct the roster.
- **F2 Broken workflow:** detect forms routing to the wrong manager → update reporting lines or workflow configuration.
- **F3 Biased criteria:** detect review prompts unrelated to job performance → replace with role-related criteria.

## Verification

The review cycle is launched with the approved roster, forms, due dates, routing, and participant notifications.

## Variations

- US: ensure reviews do not reference protected traits, protected leave, accommodation use, or retaliation-related factors.
- Other countries: works council consultation, employee file access, and performance documentation rules may differ.
- Lightweight cycle: use goal check-ins and manager summaries instead of ratings.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because reviews affect employment outcomes and may include sensitive personnel data. Use job-related criteria, restrict access, avoid protected-class references, and document decisions consistently.
