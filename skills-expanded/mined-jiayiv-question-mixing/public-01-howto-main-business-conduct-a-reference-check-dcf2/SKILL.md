## Goal

Complete a candidate reference check using job-related questions and document the result.

## Preconditions

- The candidate has provided reference contact information and permission to contact references.
- The role requirements and reference questions are approved.
- You have a secure place to record notes.

## Steps

1. **Confirm authorization.** Verify the candidate approved contacting the reference and any timing restrictions. → *Expect:* permission is documented.
2. **Schedule or place the call.** Contact the reference using the approved phone number or email. → *Expect:* the reference is reached or a message is left.
3. **Verify relationship.** Ask how the reference knows the candidate, dates, role, and reporting relationship. → *Expect:* the reference context is clear.
4. **Ask job-related questions.** Cover responsibilities, strengths, development areas, reliability, collaboration, and eligibility for rehire where lawful. → *Expect:* responses relate to the target role.
5. **Avoid prohibited topics.** Do not ask about protected traits, health, family status, religion, age, national origin, disability, or other non-job-related matters. → *Expect:* the conversation stays within lawful employment criteria.
6. **Record factual notes.** Summarize the reference's statements, date, caller, and any unanswered questions. → *Expect:* the candidate record has concise reference notes.
7. **Update recruiting status.** Mark the reference check complete, pending, or needs review. → *Expect:* the hiring team can see the reference status.

## Decision points

- If the reference gives concerning information → verify relevance and route to the hiring manager or HR before acting.
- If the reference refuses to answer → document refusal and ask the candidate for another reference if needed.
- If the candidate restricts current employer contact → respect the restriction unless explicit permission changes.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 No consent:** detect missing candidate permission → stop and obtain consent before contact.
- **F2 Improper question:** detect a question about protected traits → stop, do not record it as decision evidence, and return to job-related topics.
- **F3 Unverifiable reference:** detect mismatch in identity or relationship → document the issue and request another reference.

## Verification

The candidate record shows reference permission, reference identity, call date, job-related notes, and a complete or pending status.

## Variations

- US: avoid questions that could elicit protected-class information and follow state reference immunity rules.
- Other countries: consent, data transfer, and former employer disclosure rules may be stricter.
- Senior role: include leadership, ethics, and scope questions tied to the job profile.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because references influence hiring and may reveal sensitive information. Ask only job-related questions, document factual answers, protect candidate PII, and do not use protected-class information in the decision.
