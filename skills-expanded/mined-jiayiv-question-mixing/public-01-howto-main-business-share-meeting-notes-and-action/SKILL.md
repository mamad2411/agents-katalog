## Goal

Share meeting notes that preserve decisions, owners, and deadlines after a meeting.

## Preconditions

- The meeting has ended or is ending.
- You have notes, transcript, or memory of decisions and tasks.
- You know the intended audience for the follow-up.

## Steps

1. **Collect raw notes.** Gather the agenda, chat, transcript, whiteboard, and personal notes. → *Expect:* all meeting outputs are in one place.
2. **Separate facts from commentary.** Keep decisions, open questions, and action items; remove side comments and unclear claims. → *Expect:* the draft is concise and factual.
3. **Write action items.** For each task, name the owner, deliverable, and due date. → *Expect:* every task can be assigned and checked.
4. **Record decisions.** State what was decided, who approved it, and any known constraints. → *Expect:* readers can distinguish decisions from discussion.
5. **Flag open questions.** List unresolved items with an owner for getting the answer. → *Expect:* ambiguity is visible instead of buried.
6. **Share with attendees.** Send the notes or link to attendees and relevant stakeholders. → *Expect:* recipients can access the notes.
7. **Ask for corrections.** Give a short window for factual corrections before treating the notes as final. → *Expect:* people know how to fix inaccuracies.

## Decision points

- If the meeting involved confidential topics → restrict sharing to the approved audience.
- If an action item has no owner → assign it before sending or label it as unowned.
- If notes are incomplete → ask attendees for corrections before publishing them broadly.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Missing owner:** detect an action item says "team" or "someone" → name a single owner or escalate for assignment.
- **F2 Access blocked:** detect recipients request permission → adjust document sharing to the intended group.
- **F3 Disputed decision:** detect disagreement after sharing → update the decision record with the final owner and source.

## Verification

Shared notes include dated decisions, action items with owners and due dates, and access works for the intended recipients.

## Variations

- Recurring meeting: append notes to the standing document and carry forward open items.
- Client meeting: send a polished summary and keep internal notes in a separate private document.
- Fast follow-up: send action items first, then a fuller summary later.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk. Remove confidential customer data, private personnel remarks, credentials, and off-record comments before sharing.
