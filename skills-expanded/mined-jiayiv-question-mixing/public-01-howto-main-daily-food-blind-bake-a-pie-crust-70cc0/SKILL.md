## Goal

A pie crust is baked empty so it stays crisp and holds its shape for wet or no-bake fillings.

## Preconditions

- Chilled pie dough in a pie plate, parchment, pie weights or dry beans, fork, baking sheet, and oven.
- Dough has rested cold enough to feel firm.

## Steps

1. **Dock and chill.** Prick the base lightly with a fork and chill the lined pie plate 20 minutes. → *Expect:* dough feels firm and cold.
2. **Preheat the oven.** Heat oven to 190 °C with a baking sheet inside. → *Expect:* oven is fully hot before dough enters.
3. **Line with parchment.** Press parchment into the crust and fill to the rim with weights. → *Expect:* sides are supported and parchment touches the corners.
4. **Bake weighted.** Put pie plate on the hot sheet and bake 15-20 minutes. → *Expect:* edge looks set and just starting to color.
5. **Remove weights.** Lift parchment and weights out carefully. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* hot weights burn and spill easily; lift slowly with dry mitts. → *Expect:* crust base looks pale and slightly damp.
6. **Finish baking.** Return crust to oven 8-15 minutes for partial bake or 15-25 minutes for full bake. [BRANCH: custard filling later | no-bake filling] → *Expect:* base is dry for partial bake or golden for full bake.
7. **Cool before filling.** Set crust on a rack until warm or room temperature as the filling requires. → *Expect:* crust firms and releases steam.

## Decision points

- Filling will bake again → stop when crust is dry and lightly colored.
- Filling is no-bake → bake until evenly golden and crisp.
- Edges brown too fast → cover edge with foil or a pie shield.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Slumped sides:** detect sides sliding down → dough was warm or underweighted; patch if possible and chill longer next time.
- **F2 Puffy base:** detect domed bottom after weights come out → press gently with a spoon and dock more holes.
- **F3 Soggy bottom:** detect damp pale center → return to oven until dry before adding filling.
- **F4 Burnt edge:** detect dark bitter rim → trim lightly or shield earlier next time.

## Verification

Crust sides hold their shape, base is dry, color matches the intended partial or full bake, and no raw dough patches remain.

## Variations

- `single-crust-quiche`: partially blind bake before adding custard.
- `cream-pie`: fully blind bake and cool completely.
- `crumb-crust`: skip weights and bake until fragrant and set.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk from hot pie weights, glass or ceramic dishes, and oven burns. Use dry mitts, keep weights stable, and let hot beans or weights cool before storing.
