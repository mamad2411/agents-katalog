## Goal

Facilitate a retrospective that turns recent work into specific improvements without blaming individuals.

## Preconditions

- A completed project, sprint, launch, incident, or milestone.
- Participants with direct experience of the work.
- Psychological safety sufficient for honest discussion or an anonymous collection method.

## Steps

1. **Set the scope.** Define the time period, event, or project the retrospective covers. → *Expect:* participants evaluate the same work.
2. **Choose prompts.** Use prompts like "What worked?", "What did not?", "What surprised us?", and "What should change next time?" → *Expect:* input is structured.
3. **Collect input privately.** Give people quiet writing time before discussion. → *Expect:* candid points are captured before group pressure shapes them.
4. **Discuss patterns.** Group repeated themes and ask for facts, examples, and impact. → *Expect:* the team focuses on system patterns.
5. **Separate facts from judgments.** Convert "handoff was careless" into "handoff lacked owner, date, and acceptance criteria." → *Expect:* issues become fixable.
6. **Pick improvement actions.** [BRANCH: many issues | few issues] prioritize the top one to three issues when there are many; assign action directly when there are few. → *Expect:* the retro does not create an impossible list.
7. **Assign owners.** Give each action one owner, one deliverable, and one due date. → *Expect:* accountability is clear.
8. **Share the retro summary.** Send themes, actions, owners, and follow-up date to participants. → *Expect:* the record is transparent and usable.

## Decision points

- If the event involved harm or compliance issues → use the formal incident or HR process alongside the retro.
- If blame starts → redirect to process, decision, handoff, or missing information.
- If too many actions emerge → pick the top one to three with owners.
- If leadership action is required → include a direct escalation ask in the summary.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Conversation becomes personal:** detect accusations about character → restate observable behavior and system condition.
- **F2 Quiet team:** detect few contributions → switch to silent writing or anonymous collection.
- **F3 Action list is unrealistic:** detect more actions than owners → cut to the smallest useful commitments.

## Verification

The retro notes contain themes, decisions, 1-3 improvement actions, owners, deadlines, and a follow-up date to check whether actions happened.

## Variations

- Incident retro: include timeline, detection, response, and prevention.
- Sprint retro: keep actions small enough for the next sprint.
- Leadership retro: include decision quality and resourcing constraints.

## Safety & privacy

Medium risk because sensitive performance, conflict, or incident details may surface. Do not publish raw comments broadly without consent.
