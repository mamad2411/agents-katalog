## Goal

Cake layers are filled, crumb-coated, chilled, and frosted into a stable, even layer cake.

## Preconditions

- Fully cooled cake layers, frosting, offset spatula, turntable or plate, serrated knife, and cake board or serving plate.
- Filling and frosting are food-safe for the planned room-temperature holding time.

## Steps

1. **Level layers.** Trim domed tops with a serrated knife if needed. → *Expect:* layers sit flat without rocking.
2. **Anchor the first layer.** Smear a dab of frosting on the board and place the first layer on it. → *Expect:* cake grips the board and does not slide.
3. **Add filling.** Spread an even layer of frosting or filling, keeping it inside the edge. → *Expect:* filling thickness is even and not spilling down the sides.
4. **Stack layers.** Place the next layer centered over the first. → *Expect:* sides line up and stack looks vertical.
5. **Apply crumb coat.** Spread a thin coat over top and sides to trap crumbs. → *Expect:* cake is lightly covered and crumbs are sealed in the thin layer.
6. **Chill to set.** Refrigerate 20-30 minutes until the crumb coat firms. → *Expect:* surface feels cool and no longer sticky.
7. **Apply final coat.** Add frosting to top and sides, then smooth with spatula or scraper. → *Expect:* cake has even coverage and clean edges.
8. **Finish decorations.** Pipe, texture, or garnish while frosting is workable. ⚠️ *Irreversible:* cut fruit and perishable garnishes shorten safe holding time; add them near service. → *Expect:* decorations adhere and cake remains stable.

## Decision points

- Cake layers are warm → wait; frosting will melt and slide.
- Filling is loose → pipe a frosting dam before adding it.
- Cake leans → chill, then nudge layers while frosting is firm but not hard.

## Failure modes & recovery

- **F1 Crumbs in final coat:** detect speckled frosting → scrape lightly, chill, and apply a thin fresh layer.
- **F2 Sliding layers:** detect shifting stack → insert supports if tall, chill, and use less filling next time.
- **F3 Torn cake surface:** detect chunks lifting into frosting → chill cake and use a lighter touch.
- **F4 Bulging filling:** detect ridge between layers → chill, scrape the bulge, and use a stiffer dam.

## Verification

Cake stands level, layers are aligned, crumbs are sealed, final frosting covers top and sides evenly, and perishable fillings are held safely.

## Variations

- `naked-cake`: scrape final coat thin so cake shows through.
- `ganache-coat`: pour slightly cooled ganache over a chilled crumb coat.
- `sheet-cake`: skip stacking and frost the top in the pan.

## Safety & privacy

Low risk from knives and perishable dairy fillings. Refrigerate cakes with cream cheese, custard, whipped cream, fresh fruit, or egg-based fillings unless serving soon.
