# Short-form platform copy

Write 1 paste-ready version per requested platform from the actual video, transcript and brief. Keep the video's context and timeframe. Ground public metrics in `identity/proof.md` or facts the author supplied; never invent attribution, conversion rates, tools, links or a call to action. Keep leads, email opt-ins and product signups distinct.

**Story shorts:** when the short documents the author's journey (an episode on the road to a public goal, a vlog, a podcast or live clip), the copy carries the story, not a tip list. Open on the moment and what is at stake, and keep the product as the setting, never the pitch. Add nothing the video does not show.

Use the relevant parts of `linkedin-copywriter`, `linkedin-hook-writer` and `x-copywriter` and the author's voice guides:

- **LinkedIn:** a useful standalone post with enough concrete steps to reproduce the playbook.
- **X:** a concise, independently readable version.
- **Instagram:** emotion first with a bit of rational, the reverse of LinkedIn. The first line answers "what's in it for them" as a feeling or as the lesson and does not repeat the spoken hook word for word. For a "read the caption" reel the caption is the piece: the moment first, the lesson last. A comment keyword only when the resource exists.
- **YouTube Shorts:** a concise title and description.

Match each platform's real field limits; do not force an identical caption everywhere. Check exact text and character counts. Run every version through `voice/slop-filter.md`.

## Schedule as 1 post

When the author asks for a draft in the publishing tool, the short is 1 post with 1 channel per platform (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, YouTube), each with its own copy and, where needed, its own media file (for example a version without music for YouTube). Reuse the uploaded video. Verify accounts, media, text and unapproved status. Drafting is not permission to approve, schedule or publish.

## Covers on every channel

Every short goes out with its cover on LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube (X has no cover field). Make a 1080x1920 JPG under 2 MB from a calm frame, attach it per channel and check all 3 before reporting the short as scheduled.
