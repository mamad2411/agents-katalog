# Make the thumbnail promise visible

Read the video's actual content, target viewer, title and `context/brand/BRAND.md`. Identify one concrete idea, contrast or outcome the video can fulfill. Use authentic supplied faces/screenshots where the brief calls for them.

Sketch a few distinct compositions, then choose the one that reads clearly at small size. Text should add information to the title, not repeat all of it. Do not invent result screenshots, endorsements or emotionally misleading scenes.

For a big title over a face frame (the tool or topic name across the chest), the silver chrome text in [references/silver-text-treatment.md](references/silver-text-treatment.md) and `assets/silver-text.css` is a tested starting point.

Use the available design/image tool and the actual brand rules. Verify current platform dimensions, file size and format requirements before claiming upload readiness. Keep editable source or the generation/edit brief with the export.

Inspect the thumbnail at full and small sizes for readability, cropping, contrast and factual accuracy. Use `brand-review` for a requested independent visual review, without spawning agents unless authorized by the user/environment.

Return the export and editable source, actual dimensions and any unverified upload constraint. Thumbnail creation alone does not upload or publish.

## Newest method (2026-11 refresh)

- **Pull references first** from the swipe file (`swipe-file`): the card's inspo rows and every thumbnail marked Love or Steal this. Name the rows each concept borrows from. For fresh inspo, `youtube-thumbnail-outlier-research` scouts thumbnails that pop on small channels outside the niche.
- **Format first, content second.** Pick the format from what wins now (re-measure it; recently: a saturated or dark photo background, a big lit face looking at something, 1 glowing hero object, 2 to 3 words in heavy condensed caps, high contrast), then fill it with 1 fact and 2 to 3 words. Clean white-grid cards with small type tend to lose.
- **Concept brief before rendering:** save an editable brief with the evidence (source URL, capture date, measured outlier numbers), what each concept borrows and what changes for this video. Mark 1 pick with a reason; build only what the author picks. State the generation count and cost before paid work.
- **Faces:** start from real source frames of the author. More references do not authorise generating the author's face or an AI relight of it; a light photo polish (clean matte, a little exposure, gentle contrast, light sharpen) on their real pixels is fine.
- Keep the channel's signature while changing the video's visual story. A different hook over an identical scene is not a new concept.
