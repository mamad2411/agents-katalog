# Write for X

Write X posts the way the author writes: their own X posts and their LinkedIn posts. What carries over from LinkedIn is the writing (the hooks, the story, the texture), not a pasted post. Read the source, audience, cleared proof (`identity/proof.md`) and the author's newest posts in `context/`. Never invent a number, tool, price or result.

## Write it like their best posts

1. **Line 1: the author and a number, under about 55 characters, ending in a full stop.** "I grew my LinkedIn to 20k followers."
2. **Line 2 proves it or raises the stakes.**
3. **The story in short lines,** 1 thought per line, a blank line between: the backstory, the struggle, what they do now.
4. **A list where there are steps or items:** 1 line that says what follows, then `1)` sections with `→` items, or a plain `→` list. Steps name the tool doing the work.
5. **Their texture stays.** Keep their casual words and non-native phrasing; do not polish it.
6. **End on the last step, an honest line or a plain connect line.** No closing question unless the author wrote it.
7. **Length is what the story needs.** Always attach media: a photo, a screenshot, the graphic or the video.

## When the author wrote the post

It goes out exactly as written. Fix only a clear spelling mistake and say which. Never rewrite the hook, reorder lines, swap their list markers or add lines.

## What differs on X

- Lists of people tag them: `@handle → what you learn from them`, 1 per line. Tag every creator and company the post names by its X handle (often different from its LinkedIn tag). Check each handle with a user lookup and keep it only when the name and bio match.
- No link in the post body: the link goes in the first reply, with UTM parameters. A lead magnet uses a comment gate (follow, then comment the keyword) and the DM carries the link.
- Get to the examples fast. A LinkedIn repurpose keeps the hook and line 2, then goes straight to the list.
- No role verbs (orchestrates, leverages), no "Label: description" bullets, no "Bonus:" or "Pro tip:", no em dashes, no hashtags.

## Threads

Threads that beat their author's median open with 2 short sentences, the proof then the promise, and end on 🧵. Tweet 2 keeps only a fraction of tweet 1's views, so tweet 1 carries the proof and the image and stands alone. Then 1 tweet of context, 1 step per tweet under 280 characters with its picture, 1 honest line, the call to action last. Count characters the way X does: `→` and emoji weigh 2.

## Deliver

- Run the post through `voice/slop-filter.md` first and name what it cut.
- 1 paste-ready post in a `text` block with its character count; thread posts numbered.
- When saving to Notion is requested, put the copy on the content card (Platform X, Status Creating).
- Writing a draft never authorises posting or scheduling. An X version scheduled next to its LinkedIn post joins the same post as a channel.
