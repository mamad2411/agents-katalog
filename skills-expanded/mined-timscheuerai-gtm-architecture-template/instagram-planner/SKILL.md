# Instagram planner

Turn an idea into a piece in a format that fits it, before anything is recorded or edited. Instagram rewards emotion first: personal storytelling about the author's journey beats value content that reads as "too businessy".

Read first: the author's storytelling strategy, the short-form playbook (hook types, wider angles, the b-roll bank, cut rules), a measured format library if there is one, and earlier verdicts. Numbers come only from the cleared table in `identity/proof.md`; nobody else goes on camera without their yes; every draft passes `voice/slop-filter.md`.

## The 3 families

All 3 go out every week; each covers a different growth lever.

| Family | Sub-formats | What it needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Talking | Yapping, Actionable, Voiceover, Vlog | A take or a day of phone footage |
| Non-talking | Story with timestamps, Read the caption, Before and after, Single b-roll plus text | Clips from the b-roll bank and 1 line of text |
| Carousel | List, Split screen, Timeline, Step-by-step guide, Transformation, Day in the life | Photos of their life and the slide text |

A sensible default for 7 reels a week plus carousels: 4 talking, 3 non-talking, 2 carousels. Non-talking reels and carousels are the cheap volume: they come out of the b-roll bank and the week's long videos.

| The idea is | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Something that happened this week | Vlog, or Yapping told as a story |
| A lesson they can prove with their own result | Actionable, or a Step-by-step carousel |
| A feeling without footage of the moment | Voiceover over bank clips |
| A change over time | Story with timestamps, Timeline or Transformation carousel |
| 2 contrasting states | Before and after, or Split screen |
| A longer text worth reading | Read the caption |
| 1 strong line | Single b-roll plus text |
| A set of things | List carousel |

Episodes are events, not days: a day-counter series tends to underperform its author's median.

## The session

1. **Window:** default the next 7 days. Read every card in the window on the content board, count the Instagram cards per format, and name the family that is missing or thin before proposing anything.
2. **Sources:** the week's long videos, what happened toward the author's public goal this week (ask for 3 events if the board does not show them), and saved story outlines.
3. **Slate:** propose pieces that fill the gaps; the author picks and cuts.
4. **Brief per pick:** the format, the hook (type, wider angle, visual hook), the beats in pain, journey, payoff order (the author's own words when a story outline exists), the b-roll shot list, the caption plan and the close. Put it on the card.

Editing is `shorts-edit` or the video skills; final copy and upload are `shortform-platform-copy`; carousels are rendered with `graphics-designer`.
