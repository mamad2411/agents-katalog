# Brand identity

Use for developing or refining a brand system, assessing a brand guideline, selecting a name, or adapting approved brand assets. Determine which task is requested, the audience, intended applications, supplied references, constraints and decision authority. Distinguish known business facts from proposed strategy; do not invent claims, affiliations, provenance or an owner's approval.

For iterative work, present meaningfully distinct directions when useful, review each at its intended size and context, and record exactly what the owner chooses before assuming a direction is approved. Preserve approved source assets and unrelated work. Do not publish, send, replace a canonical asset or update a live surface without authority for that exact action.

- For naming, including candidate screening, read [naming](references/naming.md).
- For adaptations, exports and source-to-distribution integrity, read [asset production](references/asset-production.md).
- For a guideline review or authorized publication, read [review and publication](references/review-publication.md).

Use the actual project/runtime interfaces for conversion, review and output; do not assume a specific agent, converter, visual style, deployment target or artifact format. Report the checks actually performed and any limitations.
