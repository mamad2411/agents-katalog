# Edit for natural prose

Use when asked to humanize, polish or remove mechanical writing patterns. Preserve the author's meaning, evidence, quotations, uncertainty and intended audience. If the user provides a voice sample, use its rhythm and vocabulary without copying unrelated claims; otherwise match the requested tone.

Look for unsupported significance or promotional claims, vague attribution, empty transitions, repeated sentence shapes, synonym cycling, unnecessary disclaimers and forced contrast or rhetorical questions. Rewrite only where they impede the text; the presence of a particular word, punctuation mark or list is not proof of AI authorship. Keep useful structure and legitimate qualifications.

Prefer concrete observations and natural phrasing. Do not insert personal feelings, stronger claims, fabricated examples, new sources or stylistic quirks to simulate a human voice. Show the rewritten text or the relevant file changes. The original upstream license is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
