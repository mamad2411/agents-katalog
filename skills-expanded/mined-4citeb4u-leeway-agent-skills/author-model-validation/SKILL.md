# LeeWay Author Model Validation

Validate beyond lexical similarity. Test held-out topics, different audiences/emotional states and medium shifts. Ask the author to rate voice fit and identify what feels wrong. Use negative controls to detect generic-model prose or overfitting to memorized phrases.

Evaluate dimensions separately: content selection, attention, reasoning/rhetoric, diction, syntax/rhythm, emotional modulation, audience adaptation and revision behavior. AUTHOR_APPROVED is stronger evidence than model self-scoring.
