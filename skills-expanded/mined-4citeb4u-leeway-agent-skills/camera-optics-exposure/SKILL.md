# LeeWay Camera Optics & Exposure
Define capture/display context: sensor/gate/aspect, focal length/FOV, subject distance, aperture, focus, CoC convention, shutter angle/time, frame rate, ISO/EI, ND/filters and camera transform. Focal length does not alone determine perspective; camera position does. 'Normal lens' depends on format/viewing. CoC values such as 0.03 mm are conventions tied to assumptions, not universal constants.
