# LeeWay View Transition & Spatial Navigation
Use native View Transition capabilities where supported, preserve focus/reading/navigation semantics and provide fallback when APIs/features are unavailable. Element/cross-document capabilities may have narrower support than document-scoped same-document transitions; capability-detect rather than assume.
