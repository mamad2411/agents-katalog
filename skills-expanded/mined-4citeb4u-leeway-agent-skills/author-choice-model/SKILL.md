# LeeWay Author Choice Model

Surface imitation asks 'what words look similar?' Choice modeling asks 'given several plausible ways to express this intent, which would the author choose and why?'

Collect pairwise/ranked preferences over content selection, ordering, specificity, tone, metaphors, sentence forms and endings. Bind reasons when the author provides them. Use preferences to improve generation and active-learning questions; preserve contradictions rather than forcing false consistency.
