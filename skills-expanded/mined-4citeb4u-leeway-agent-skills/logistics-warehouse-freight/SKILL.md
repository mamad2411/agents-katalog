# LeeWay Logistics Warehouse & Freight
Use demand, lead time, variability, service level, storage/handling, ordering and transport constraints. EOQ/ROP are baseline models when assumptions fit; perishability, capacity, MOQ, stochastic demand, multi-echelon networks and dynamic freight may require richer optimization. Hours-of-service and transport regulations are hard constraints requiring current jurisdiction-specific evidence.
