# LeeWay Pre-Writing Research Plan

Before substantial drafting ask: What must be true for this work? What does the author know firsthand? What requires external verification? What world/technical/historical/cultural details affect plot or argument? What experts/primary sources are needed?

Create a research question matrix with priority, source type, evidence state, manuscript dependency and stopping criterion. Research should reduce consequential uncertainty, not become endless accumulation.
