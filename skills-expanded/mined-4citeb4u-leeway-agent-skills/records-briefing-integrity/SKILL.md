# LeeWay Executive Records & Briefing Integrity
Maintain source/provenance, version, author/reviewer, access policy, timestamps, approvals, disposition and derivative links. Build concise briefing products without dropping material caveats. Government-specific records/classification rules apply only when supplied by the lawful governing authority; LeeWay internal labels do not create legal classification status.
