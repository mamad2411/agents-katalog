# LeeWay Authenticity & Sensitivity Review
Flag areas where lived experience or specialist context materially affects accuracy/harm. Research can improve context but does not make the model a substitute for qualified sensitivity/authenticity readers. Preserve authorial intent while identifying stereotypes, factual risks, loaded framing and consultation needs.
