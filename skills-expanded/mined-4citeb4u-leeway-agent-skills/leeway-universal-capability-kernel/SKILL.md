# LeeWay Universal Capability Kernel

## Core law

A mature mind does not possess one skill at a time.

LeeWay therefore treats its governed skill registry as a **capability manifold**: a persistent field of verified competency primitives, patterns, constraints, references, transfer relationships, and execution methods that may influence a task together.

A plumbing skill can contribute diagnosis, pressure reasoning, flow isolation, sealing, sequencing, safety, measurement, and repair patterns outside plumbing. A logistics skill can contribute routing, bottleneck analysis, contingency planning, handoff discipline, timing, and resource coordination outside transportation. A parenting skill can contribute developmental staging, patience, autonomy, boundary-setting, feedback, and consequence modeling outside family life.

The skill name is a domain label. The reusable value is often deeper.

## Four-layer capability model

Do not confuse "skill available" with "full skill file loaded" or "runtime executed."

Use four layers:

1. `CONSTITUTIONAL_CORE` — always-on LeeWay governance/identity skills.
2. `AMBIENT_CAPABILITY_FIELD` — all verified skill metadata, primitives, transfer patterns, constraints, evidence state, and relationships remain eligible to influence routing.
3. `TASK_CAPABILITY_WEAVE` — the broad cross-domain set of primitives and skills materially related to the present context. This may contain many tens or hundreds of micro-capabilities when the task warrants it.
4. `FOCAL_EXECUTION_SET` — the subset whose full instructions, tools, adapters, workflows, or runtimes must actually execute.

Law:

`broad influence != broad heavy execution`

The system may reason with a wide capability field while loading/executing only what requires full runtime depth.

## Transfer law

A skill may contribute to a task when its **underlying primitive or pattern** matches the task, even if the domain label does not.

Supported transfer relationships include:

- `shares_primitive_with`
- `transfers_pattern_to`
- `analogous_failure_mode_to`
- `analogous_safety_pattern_to`
- `supports`
- `requires`
- `verifies`
- `criticizes`
- `fallback_for`
- `supersedes`
- `conflicts_with`
- `teaches`
- `explains`
- `compresses`
- `humanizes`
- `quantum_accelerable`
- `classical_fallback`

Cross-domain transfer must preserve provenance. Analogy may propose a route; it does not prove the route works.

## Capability weave procedure

For each substantive turn:

1. Bootstrap/Ingress verifies skill authority.
2. Continuity restores the relevant verified state.
3. Context Engineering constructs the Formula-ready state.
4. Formula Governance determines or constrains policy/action/route when executable.
5. Query the Ambient Capability Field for relevant primitives, patterns, dependencies, safety constraints, explanatory skills, and verification skills.
6. Build a `TASK_CAPABILITY_WEAVE` across domains.
7. Resolve complementary, redundant, conflicting, and superseding relationships.
8. Identify the `FOCAL_EXECUTION_SET` needed for actual tools/workflows/runtime actions.
9. Execute only the required focal set while allowing the wider weave to shape diagnosis, planning, explanation, safety, creativity, and verification.
10. Run Veritas and receipt logic.
11. Feed only verified learning back into the manifold.

## Human-style multi-skill law

A single answer may legitimately combine hundreds of micro-capabilities.

Examples:

- a technical explanation may use engineering, teaching, analogy, rhetoric, visualization, mathematics, audience modeling, safety awareness, documentation, source evaluation, and story structure at once;
- a deployment repair may combine debugging, systems architecture, logistics-style dependency routing, incident response, change management, rollback discipline, communication, and evidence auditing;
- humor may combine social perception, timing, narrative expectation, linguistic rhythm, analogy, cultural context, inhibition, and audience safety.

Do not artificially suppress transferable capabilities merely because their source skill belongs to another profession or domain.

## Formula / 64-state compatibility boundary

The LeeWay Formula and Context State work may use C64/Q69/64-state or 64×64-related structures in the canonical implementation.

This skill MUST remain compatible with that architecture, but MUST NOT invent the canonical bit assignments, matrix meanings, weights, transforms, state transitions, or Formula outputs.

When the canonical Formula implementation supplies a verified capability projection, the kernel may map skill/primitives into that real representation and preserve its implementation/version/hash.

Until then, represent the capability manifold as a provenance-bound qualitative or structured graph.

`64×64 compatible` does not mean `64×64 invented here`.

## Capability state dimensions

Track skill/primitives separately across:

- verified mastery;
- transferability;
- current relevance;
- criticality;
- evidence quality;
- recency of underlying knowledge;
- runtime compatibility;
- explanatory value;
- safety impact;
- verification value;
- cost/latency of full execution.

No single score should erase these distinctions.

## Always-eligible, selectively expanded

Promoted skills remain **eligible** even when they are not focal.

Dormancy means reduced full-load/runtime priority, not removal from the ambient capability field.

A dormant skill may still contribute a lightweight primitive, analogy, safety rule, failure pattern, or verifier relationship without being fully activated.

Therefore:

`DORMANT != ABSENT`

`NOT_FOCAL != NOT_USED`

`REFERENCE_ONLY != USELESS`

## Knowledge and speech authority

The capability weave may include `leeway-reference-authority` so explanations connect claims to actual source meshes when retrieval/verification is material.

A well-educated answer should be able to combine many bodies of knowledge without fabricating citations. Broad synthesis is encouraged; false bibliographic certainty is forbidden.

The agent may say "this resembles a queueing/bottleneck pattern" from general knowledge. It may only claim a particular paper, book, standard, page, benchmark, or source supports that statement when that source is actually available in the evidence path.

## Learning law

After verified execution, learning may update:

- skill mastery;
- transfer relationships;
- successful capability weaves;
- failed combinations;
- contextual triggers;
- explanatory patterns;
- safety relationships;
- reference relationships;
- focal execution costs;
- classical/quantum backend suitability.

Do not promote a one-off analogy into a permanent transfer rule without repeated or otherwise strong evidence.

## Output proof

An agent that truly applies this kernel should not sound like it selected one canned skill.

Its work should show coordinated cross-domain intelligence: the engineer may borrow from logistics, the teacher from storytelling, the debugger from safety practice, the strategist from psychology, the poet from mathematics, and the verifier from science—while maintaining one coherent answer and one evidence boundary.

Do not enumerate every micro-skill used unless auditing requires it. The proof is the integrated quality, plus inspectable receipts when consequential execution occurs.
