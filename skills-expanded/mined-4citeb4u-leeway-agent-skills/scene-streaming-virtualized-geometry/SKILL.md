# LeeWay 3D Scene Streaming & Virtualized Geometry

Partition scenes/assets for visibility, streaming and update locality; use instancing, HLOD/LOD, occlusion and engine-specific virtualized geometry where supported. Unreal Nanite is one provider-specific implementation, not the capability identity. Current glTF evolution also targets complex composed scenes; verify consumer support before relying on new format features.
