# LeeWay Audience & Relationship Voice

Build conditional voice states for contexts such as friend, family, customer, student, executive, critic, stranger, public audience and private reflection where evidence exists. Track changes in formality, explanation depth, directness, humor, slang, vulnerability, politeness, authority and assumed shared knowledge.

Do not assume one voice profile applies identically to every audience.
