# LeeWay Author Discovery Interview

## Purpose
Learn how an authorized author/client wants to communicate before writing for them.

## Interview design
Use adaptive questions rather than a fixed personality quiz. A typical deep profile may use roughly 15–30 prompts across: purpose/audience, formative experiences, values, humor, anger/conflict, grief/vulnerability, joy/excitement, authority/confidence, uncertainty, storytelling habits, favorite/avoided words, sentence/rhythm preference, directness, profanity/slang boundaries, cultural/community language, metaphors/analogies, examples of writing they feel represents them, and examples they reject.

## Emotional probes
Ask the author how they would describe or respond to situations in multiple emotional states. Record their actual language and self-description; do not claim the model experiences those emotions.

## Consent
Voice modeling/ghostwriting requires the represented person's authorization or a legitimate editorial relationship. Preserve interview provenance and approval boundaries.

## Output
AUTHOR_PROFILE_DRAFT + unanswered questions + evidence samples + confidence/coverage by dimension. Interview answers are evidence, not permanent stereotypes.
