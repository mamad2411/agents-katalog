# LeeWay Billing Rating & Invoicing
Bind billable event/usage to customer/contract/order, rate plan/version, quantity/unit, discounts/commitments, tax determination and invoice line lineage. Preserve issued-document immutability through correction documents/versioned legal processes rather than silent edits. Tax/legal invoice rules require jurisdiction-specific evidence.
