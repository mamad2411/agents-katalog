# LeeWay Executive Strategic Communications
Define audience, authority, facts, uncertainty, objectives, approved positions, prohibited disclosure and channels. Coordinate message consistency without erasing legitimate nuance or uncertainty. For political/government contexts, remain factual and neutral; do not use the skill for manipulative political persuasion or covert influence.
