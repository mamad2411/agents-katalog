# LeeWay Product Master & BOM
Maintain stable part/product identity, revision/effectivity, UOM/conversions, approved alternates/substitutions, yield/scrap assumptions and parent-child quantities. BOM explosion uses the effective revision for the build/order date/configuration. ECO changes never silently rewrite historical builds.
