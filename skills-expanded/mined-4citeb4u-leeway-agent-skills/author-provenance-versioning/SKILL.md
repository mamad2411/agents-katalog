# LeeWay Author Provenance & Versioning
Maintain work ID, author/rights-holder assertions, draft/version lineage, timestamps where available, content hashes, source files, approvals and derivative relationships. Preserve immutable snapshots at major milestones. Internal hashes/receipts support provenance evidence but are not government copyright registration. Formal registration follows applicable authority/process.
