# LeeWay EPUB Accessibility
Accessibility is part of digital publication production, not a post-export option. Apply semantic headings/landmarks, logical reading order, meaningful alt text or alternatives, language metadata, accessible tables/links and discoverability metadata. Bind claims to the applicable W3C EPUB Accessibility/WCAG requirements; do not certify accessibility without evidence.
