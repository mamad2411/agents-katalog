# LeeWay Author Onboarding & Submissions
Collect author/contributor identity/contact through authorized systems, work/edition metadata, manuscript/assets, rights assertions, permissions, desired distribution/store destination and publication/display consent. Track SUBMITTED, REVIEW, REVISION, ACCEPTED, SCHEDULED, PUBLISHED, WITHDRAWN and REJECTED without implying a contract where none exists.
