# LeeWay Publishing Rights & Permissions
Inventory third-party content and asserted rights. Record license/permission/source and unresolved clearance. Route formal copyright/registration/legal questions to authoritative sources or qualified professionals as appropriate. Do not claim clearance from absence of a complaint.
