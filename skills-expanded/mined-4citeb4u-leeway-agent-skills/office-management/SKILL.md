# Office Management

Establish owners, approval limits, calendars, retention rules, vendors, inventory thresholds, facilities contacts, sensitive-data rules, and recurring deadlines. Maintain one action register with item, owner, due date, dependency, status, evidence, and escalation point.

Use the canonical specialist skills for email, scheduling, meeting notes, invoices, budgets, file organization, project management, compliance, and reporting. Do not duplicate their procedures here. This skill composes them into an office operating rhythm: daily intake and exceptions, weekly schedule/vendor/supply review, monthly records and budget close, and periodic continuity/recovery checks.

No purchase, contract, personnel action, external message, or access grant occurs without the required human authority and executed tool evidence.
