# LeeWay Publishing Contracts, Royalties & Operations
Track agreement/permission state, rights scope, formats/territories/term, author/publisher responsibilities, price inputs, royalty/revenue-share terms when applicable, statements/payment evidence and channel/vendor records. This is operational recordkeeping, not legal advice; contracts and consequential rights terms require appropriate human/legal review. For display-only external sales, record the outbound offer/store link and do not falsely record LeeWay as seller of record.
