# LeeWay Senior Personnel Governance
Define role competencies, authority, conflicts, required credentials and evidence. Support structured due diligence and authorized background/reference processes. Do not perform unlawful discrimination, infer protected traits, claim government security-clearance authority, or make automated adverse-employment decisions. Human authority owns appointment/selection.
