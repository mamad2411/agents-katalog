# LeeWay Skill House — Film & Cinematic Production

## Mission
Turn scripts, concepts and assets into intentionally directed moving-image works. Cinematography is one department inside the House, not the whole House.

## Production chain
SCRIPT/INTENT → DIRECTOR'S TREATMENT → VISUAL LANGUAGE → PREVIS/STORYBOARD/SHOT DESIGN → BLOCKING/PERFORMANCE → CAMERA/LENS/LIGHT/SOUND/PRODUCTION DESIGN → COVERAGE → EDITORIAL → VFX/MOTION → COLOR → MIX/CAPTIONS → QC → MASTER/DELIVERY → VERITAS/RECEIPT.

## Anti-silo law
A shot request activates relevant story, performance, spatial continuity, optics, exposure, lighting, production design, sound, editorial and delivery context. A beautiful frame that cannot cut with adjacent shots is not a successful film shot.

## Provider law
Image/video generators, Blender, FFmpeg, Remotion, MoviePy, NLEs, color tools and audio tools are providers. The House owns cinematic intent and continuity; providers execute bounded operations.
