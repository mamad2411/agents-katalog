# LeeWay Genre & Reader Promise
Identify genre/subgenre, target audience, comparable expectations and required/optional conventions. Conventions are promises/probabilities, not formulas. Deliberate subversion should preserve reader orientation and payoff rather than accidentally omitting the genre engine.
