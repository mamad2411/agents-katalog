# LeeWay Spatial Blocking & Continuity
Maintain world coordinates/relative positions, action axis, eyelines, entrances/exits and FG/MG/BG depth. The 180-degree rule is a continuity tool, not an absolute prohibition: intentional axis crossing requires motivated movement, neutral/re-establishing coverage or deliberate disorientation. Left/right symbolism is culturally/contextually contingent; screen-direction continuity is the stronger technical constraint.
