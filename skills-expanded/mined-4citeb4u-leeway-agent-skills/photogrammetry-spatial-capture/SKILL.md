# LeeWay 3D Photogrammetry & Spatial Capture

Plan coverage, overlap, exposure/focus consistency and scale references; align cameras/sensors; reconstruct dense geometry/point data; remove noise/outliers; establish scale; retopologize/UV/bake for production. Preserve source capture and uncertainty. Single-view image reconstruction and multi-view capture are different evidence classes.
