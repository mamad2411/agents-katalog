# LeeWay Pre-Writing Readiness

Check PURPOSE, AUDIENCE, MEDIUM, AUTHORSHIP/VOICE AUTHORITY, RESEARCH QUESTIONS, SOURCE NOTEBOOK, CANON/WORLD STATE, RIGHTS/CONFIDENTIALITY BOUNDARIES, STRUCTURAL INTENT and APPROVAL LOOP. Missing items may be intentionally UNKNOWN, but consequential unknowns must be visible before drafting.
