# LeeWay Admin & Front Office
Model arrivals, service capacity, queue/wait, priority/SLA, routing accuracy and record quality. Little's Law applies under its assumptions and does not mean every healthy system has zero queue. Define acceptable queue/wait/utilization profiles from service goals and variability.
