# LeeWay Developmental Editing
Evaluate the work at macro scale before line polish. Diagnose premise/engine, causality, structure, pacing, stakes, character arcs, POV strategy, theme, setup/payoff and missing/redundant material. Produce prioritized revision notes with evidence/examples; preserve author intent and distinguish REQUIRED_FOR_COHERENCE from OPTIONAL_CRAFT_CHOICE.
