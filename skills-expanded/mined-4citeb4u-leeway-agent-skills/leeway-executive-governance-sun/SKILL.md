# LeeWay Executive Governance Sun
Five rays: SECRETARIAT_RECORDS; DECISION_STATECRAFT; CONTINUITY_READINESS; STRATEGIC_COMMUNICATIONS; SENIOR_PERSONNEL_PROCESS. Use evidence-bound centered deviations from declared governance targets. No ray may encode political preference, official governmental authority, classified status or legal compliance without authoritative evidence. Hard gates include authority, records integrity, material factual error, unlawful action, required approval and continuity of essential functions. A zero vector means target process state, not certainty about geopolitical outcomes or people.
