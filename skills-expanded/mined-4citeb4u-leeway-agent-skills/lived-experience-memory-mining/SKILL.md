# LeeWay Lived Experience & Memory Mining

Use open prompts to recover scenes, people, places, sensory details, decisions, consequences and later interpretation. Separate remembered fact, uncertain recollection, family/community account and documented evidence. Do not pressure disclosure of sensitive experiences. For memoir/biography, route factual claims through verification where appropriate.
