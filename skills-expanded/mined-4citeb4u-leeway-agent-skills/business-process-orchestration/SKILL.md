# LeeWay Business Process Orchestration
Represent business workflows as explicit states/transitions, guards, owners, timers, retries, compensations and terminal states. LangGraph, workflow engines or native LeeWay runtime may implement execution; no library becomes governance authority. Persistent workflows require durable state and idempotent actions.
