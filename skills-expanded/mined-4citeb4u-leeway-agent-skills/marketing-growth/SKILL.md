# LeeWay Marketing & Growth Operations
Measure acquisition, activation, conversion, retention, revenue/margin and attribution uncertainty. LTV:CAC=3 is a common heuristic, not universal equilibrium; acceptable economics depend on gross margin, payback, capital, retention and growth strategy. Never fabricate attribution certainty.
