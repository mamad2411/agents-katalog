# LeeWay Skill House — Enterprise Operations

## Mission
Operate a real organization from measured business state while preserving human authority, legal/compliance boundaries, financial controls, audit evidence and rollback.

## Core departments
ADMIN/FRONT OFFICE; LOGISTICS/WAREHOUSE/FREIGHT; SALES/CRM; MARKETING/GROWTH; PEOPLE/HR; FINANCE/ACCOUNTING; PROCUREMENT/VENDOR; CUSTOMER SUPPORT; COMPLIANCE/RISK.

## Provider law
Frappe/ERP systems, CRM, PocketBase/PostgreSQL, LangGraph/workflow engines and other apps are providers/data sources. LeeWay owns operational semantics, Formula evaluation, authorization, orchestration and evidence.

## Automation law
Read/measure may be automated when authorized. Consequential writes—payments, payroll, hiring/firing, legal/compliance submissions, contracts, price changes, inventory write-offs and other high-impact actions—require the applicable approval/control policy. A failed metric may dispatch diagnosis/remediation planning without automatically authorizing the remediation.

## Formula
Targets are business/project profiles, not universal constants. Hard legal, safety, payroll, accounting, privacy and authorization failures cannot be averaged away by aggregate health.
