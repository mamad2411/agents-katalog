# LeeWay Enterprise Compliance & Risk
Map obligations/policies to controls, owners, evidence, exceptions and remediation. Compliance state is jurisdiction/domain/time dependent. Never infer legal compliance from a generic metric. Hard violations or missing mandatory evidence can independently block an operation/release.
