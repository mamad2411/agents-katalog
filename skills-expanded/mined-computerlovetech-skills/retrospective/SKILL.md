Review your work in this session. If you observed no obstacles, respond with exactly `NOTHING TO SEE`.

Otherwise, classify each obstacle using the applicable categories below.

- **`missing_context`** — Progress was blocked because the user could not supply essential information, credentials, or access.
- **`repeatedly_broken_tool`** — A tool failed on multiple distinct attempts and prevented completion.
- **`capability_gap`** — You lacked a tool, permission, or capability required for the task.
- **`complete_task_failure`** — You could not deliver the requested outcome despite genuine attempts.
