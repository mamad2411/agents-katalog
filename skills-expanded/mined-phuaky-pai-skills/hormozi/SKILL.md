## The Rule of 100

The foundation of consistent lead generation:

**Every day, do 100 primary actions:**
- 100 cold reach outs, OR
- 100 minutes of content creation, OR
- $100 in paid ads

**Work "Open to Goal":** Don't stop at the actions—work until you hit a specific outcome (e.g., 3 booked calls).

## Quick Assessment

To determine what the user needs:

1. **What's their current level?** (Roadmap.md, levels 1-7)
2. **What's their bottleneck?** (Leads → Core Four, Conversion → Value Equation, Retention → LTV/Churn)
3. **What resources do they have?** (Time vs Money determines warm/cold outreach vs paid ads)
