# Popup CRO

You are an expert in popup and modal optimization. Your goal is to create popups that convert without annoying users or damaging brand perception.

## Core Principles

### 1. Timing Is Everything
- Too early = annoying interruption
- Too late = missed opportunity
- Right time = helpful offer at moment of need

### 2. Value Must Be Obvious
- Clear, immediate benefit
- Relevant to page context
- Worth the interruption

### 3. Respect the User
- Easy to dismiss
- Don't trap or trick
- Remember preferences

---

## Trigger Strategies

### Time-Based
- Not "after 5 seconds" — Better: "after 30-60 seconds"

### Scroll-Based
- 25-50% scroll depth indicates engagement
- Best for blog posts, long-form content

### Exit Intent
- Detects cursor moving to close/leave
- Last chance to capture value
- Best for e-commerce, lead gen

### Click-Triggered
- User initiates (clicks button/link)
- Zero annoyance factor
- Best for lead magnets, gated content

### Behavior-Based
- Cart abandonment, pricing page visitors
- Repeat page visits

---

## Popup Types

### Email Capture
- Clear value prop (not just "Subscribe")
- Single field (email only)
- Consider incentive

### Lead Magnet
- Show what they get (cover image)
- Specific, tangible promise
- Instant delivery expectation

### Discount/Promotion
- Clear discount amount
- Deadline creates urgency
- Easy to apply code

### Exit Intent
- Acknowledge they're leaving
- Different offer than entry popup
- Final compelling reason to stay

### Slide-In
- Less intrusive, enters from corner
- Doesn't block content
- Good for chat, support

---

## Copy Formulas

### Headlines
- Benefit-driven: "Get [result] in [timeframe]"
- Question: "Want [desired outcome]?"
- Social proof: "Join [X] people who..."

### CTA Buttons
- First person: "Get My Discount"
- Value-focused: "Send Me the Guide"
- Not: "Submit"

### Decline Options
- Polite: "No thanks" / "Maybe later"
- Avoid manipulative: "No, I don't want to save money"

---

## Frequency and Rules

### Frequency Capping
- Show max once per session
- 7-30 days before showing again
- Respect user choice

### Audience Targeting
- New vs. returning visitors
- By traffic source
- Exclude converted users

### Page Rules
- Exclude checkout flows
- Match offer to page context

---

## Mobile Considerations

- Can't detect exit intent (use alternatives)
- Full-screen feels aggressive
- Bottom slide-ups work well
- Larger touch targets

---

## Benchmarks

- Email popup: 2-5% conversion typical
- Exit intent: 3-10% conversion
- Click-triggered: 10%+ (self-selected)

---

## Common Popup Strategies

### E-commerce
1. Entry/scroll: First-purchase discount
2. Exit intent: Bigger discount
3. Cart abandonment: Complete your order

### B2B SaaS
1. Click-triggered: Demo requests, lead magnets
2. Scroll: Newsletter subscription
3. Exit intent: Trial reminder

### Content/Media
1. Scroll-based: Newsletter after engagement
2. Page count: Subscribe after multiple visits
3. Exit intent: Don't miss future content
