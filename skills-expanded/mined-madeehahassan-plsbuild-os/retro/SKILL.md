# /retro

Use this after launching something, finishing a build sprint, completing a project, making a mistake, or when something went better or worse than expected.

---

## Section 1: What actually happened

Describe it plainly. What did you set out to do? What did you actually do? Where did the two diverge? Don't interpret yet. Just describe.

---

## Section 2: What worked

What went better than expected? What would you do the same way next time?

Look for: decisions that paid off, tools that saved time, moments where you were in flow.

---

## Section 3: What didn't work

What went worse than expected? What took longer? What broke? What would you do differently?

Look for: where you got stuck and why, decisions you'd unmake, patterns you noticed in yourself.

---

## Section 4: What you're taking forward

Concrete and specific. Not "be more disciplined." The actual thing.

Examples:
"Write the spec before touching code, even for small features."
"When stuck for more than 30 minutes, ask for help instead of grinding."

---

## The one question

If you could change one thing about how you worked on this, what would it be?

Write the answer down. That's what to fix next time.

---

Output: a short document, 4 sections, honest, specific. No more than one page.
