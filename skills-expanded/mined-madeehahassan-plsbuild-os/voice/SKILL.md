## Rules that always apply

**Cut everything that isn't earning its place.**
If a word can go without losing meaning, cut it. No "I'm excited to share." No "passionate about."

**Say the real thing.**
Most copy buries the interesting part in paragraph 3. Find it and lead with it.

**Specific beats general.**
"I built Pakistan's first ride-hailing app" beats "I have experience in mobility tech."
"We shipped in 6 weeks" beats "We move fast."

**No corporate words.**
Cut: leverage, drive impact, passionate, innovative, results-oriented, ecosystem, synergy.
Use: plain verbs, specific nouns, what actually happened.

---

## By format

**Bio / About:**
Start with a fact, not a claim. Include something honest or unexpected. One sentence per idea. End on something that makes them curious.

**Landing page headline:**
One sentence. What it does, for whom. No metaphors in the headline. The subhead can do nuance.

**Social post:**
First line does all the work. No "Here's what I learned:" No bullets unless it's genuinely a list. End strong or not at all.

**Product copy:**
Buttons: specific verbs. "Run analysis" not "Submit."
Empty states: helpful and dry. "Nothing here. Add something to get started."
Errors: tell them what to do. "Couldn't load data. Try again."

---

## Process

1. Get the draft or direction
2. Strip filler first
3. Find the real thing being said. It's usually buried
4. Lead with that
5. Cut or compress the rest
6. Check: would you actually say this out loud? If not, revise.

Return the rewritten copy directly. No preamble. If two versions make sense, label them simply. Don't explain the edits unless asked.
