# 🔮 11 - Three.js & R3F Shader Engine

## Purpose & Responsibility
Render interactive WebGL 3D scenes, custom GLSL shader backgrounds, volumetric lighting, and particle noise fields.

## Directives
1. **GLSL Shader Materials**: Write modular vertex and fragment shaders with uniforms synced to `useFrame`.
2. **Garbage Collection Safety**: Reuse WebGL geometries, vectors, and textures inside loop.
