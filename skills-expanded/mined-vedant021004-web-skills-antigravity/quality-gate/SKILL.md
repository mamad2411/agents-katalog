# 🛑 15 - Final Quality Gatekeeper Engine

## Purpose & Responsibility
Act as the final unyielding gatekeeper. Blocks low-quality code, generic templates, derivative clones, and weak typography before user delivery.

## Rejection Directives (AUTOMATIC REJECTION IF FOUND)
- ❌ **Generic AI Layout**: Simple hero + 3-card grid + basic footer with standard Tailwind defaults (`bg-gray-100`, `text-blue-600`).
- ❌ **Bootstrap-Looking Interface**: Tacky drop shadows, rounded-lg colored buttons without spring feedback.
- ❌ **Derivative Clones**: Direct uninspired copies of Linear, Vercel, or Apple landing pages without brand identity adaptation.
- ❌ **Broken Spacing**: Non-8px baseline offsets (`margin-top: 13px`, `padding: 18px`).
- ❌ **Tacky Gradients**: Overused plastic glassmorphism or bright rainbow text gradients.
- ❌ **Unnecessary Motion**: Janky layout reflow animations (`width`, `height` transitions).

## Gatekeeper Approval Condition
No project or component output is allowed to pass until 100% of anti-patterns are eliminated and the Design Review Jury score reaches >= 95 / 100.
