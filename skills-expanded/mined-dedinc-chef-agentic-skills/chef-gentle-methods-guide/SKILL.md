# Chef Gentle Methods Guide

Steamed and boiled food has a sad reputation because people treat gentleness as neglect. Done with precision, these are luxury techniques: Cantonese steamed fish is a delicacy, poached chicken is silk, en papillote is theater. The craft = exact temperatures + aggressive flavoring at the right moments (water washes flavor OUT — so you must engineer it IN before or after).

## The temperature ladder (water is a precision instrument)

| °C | State | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 60–70 | Barely moving | Custards-in-jars, onsen eggs, holding |
| 70–82 | Shivering, first micro-bubbles on pot floor | POACHING: eggs, fish, chicken — the silk zone |
| 85–95 | Trembling surface, lazy bubbles | Braising liquid, dumplings after float, delicate simmers |
| 96–99 | Gentle rolling | Grains, potatoes, blanching finish |
| 100 rolling | Violence | Pasta ONLY (needs agitation), water for tea; almost nothing else benefits |
Most "boiled" food is ruined at 100°C that wanted 85°C. When auditing recipes (`chef-recipe-optimizer`): "boil the chicken 40 min" is a rubber-production instruction — rewrite to the ladder.

## Steaming (the underrated crown)

- **Physics:** 100°C vapor, heat transfer gentler than water contact, NOTHING washes out — nutrients and flavor stay. The dish must arrive already-flavored: marinate before, sauce after, aromatics in the steam (lemongrass/ginger/herbs in the water perfume mildly).
- **Setups:** bamboo basket over wok (parchment/cabbage-leaf liner); metal insert; IMPROVISED: three foil balls + heatproof plate in any lidded pot — works perfectly; microwave-covered-bowl = legitimate steamer (equipment guide §5).
- **Times:** fish fillet 6–10 min (to 55–60°C core); whole fish 15–20; chicken breast sliced 8–10; dumplings 8–12 fresh; vegetables: broccoli 4–5, carrots 6–8, potatoes 15–20 — crisp-tender, then SEASON IMMEDIATELY hot (salt+fat+acid land better on hot surfaces).
- **The Cantonese benchmark dish:** fish + ginger-scallion, steamed; hot oil poured over at service + soy — four ingredients, restaurant luxury, 12 minutes. Teach this one dish to convert steam-skeptics.
- En papillote = self-steaming envelope: fish/veg + aromatics + splash of wine in parchment, 200°C 12–15 min, opens at the table — gentle method AS spectacle (event-worthy, `chef-menu-event-planning`).

## Boiling & poaching craft

- **Salt the water like it matters:** pasta 10 g/L; vegetables 15–20 g/L (blanching brine seasons through); potatoes from COLD water (even cooking), green veg into ROLLING water (speed preserves color), shock in ice = restaurant green.
- **Poached eggs:** freshest eggs (whites hold), 75–82°C, gentle vortex optional, 3–4 min; strainer-drain the watery white first — the one trick that fixes everything. Batch-poach ahead + ice bath + 30 s reheat = brunch for a crowd.
- **Poached chicken (the technique everyone needs):** cold water + aromatics, bring to BARE simmer, 10 min, off-heat lid 15–20 — carryover finishes to 65°C+; silk texture, free broth. This is the base for salads, ramen toppings, kids' food, diet tables — the highest-leverage gentle technique in the set.
- **Fish poaching:** court-bouillon (water+wine+bay+peppercorn+lemon, simmered 10 min first), 70–80°C, 8–12 min; or oil-poach (submerged in 60–70°C oil) for luxury texture.
- **Fruit poaching:** syrup 1:2 sugar:water + spice/wine, 80–90°C until tender-pierced — pears/quince/stone fruit; dessert from the diet-compatible arsenal.
- **Blanch-and-shock as prep infrastructure:** vegetables par-cooked, shocked, held — finished in 2 min at service (sauté/butter-glaze/grill-kiss). This is how restaurants serve 12 perfect vegetables at once (chain `chef-menu-event-planning` timelines).

## Flavor engineering for gentle methods (anti-sad insurance)
1. Season the medium (salted water, court-bouillon, aromatic steam) — or accept blandness.
2. Marinate/brine BEFORE (salt penetration — gastrophysics §3).
3. Sauce AFTER with contrast: gentle protein + assertive sauce (ginger-scallion oil, salsa verde, yogurt-garlic, brown butter) is the master pattern.
4. Finish with texture: crunch garnish rescues soft-on-soft (fried shallots, toasted seeds, croutons — flavor-balance texture law).
5. Temperature-contrast plating: warm poached thing + cool sauce (or reverse) reads as intentional cuisine, not hospital tray.
