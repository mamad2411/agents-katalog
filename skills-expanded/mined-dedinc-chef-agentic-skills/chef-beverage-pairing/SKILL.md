# Chef Beverage & Pairing

Pairing is flavor-balance across the table: the drink is another component of the dish. Same seven levers (salt/fat/acid/sweet/bitter/umami/heat from `chef-flavor-balance`), applied between glass and plate.

Read `references/pairing-cheatsheets.md` for dish→drink tables, wine style map, and non-alcoholic recipes.

## The six pairing laws (these do 90% of the work)

1. **Acid cuts fat.** Rich food wants acidic drink: sparkling with fried anything, crisp white with cream sauces, kombucha with pork belly. The single most reliable law.
2. **Match the weight.** Delicate dish + delicate drink; heavy braise + heavy drink. A powerful wine steamrolls a sole fillet; light lager drowns under lamb. Weight-mismatch is the classic amateur error, not variety choice.
3. **Sweet needs sweeter.** Dessert wine must be sweeter than the dessert, or the wine turns sour-thin. Also: sweetness tames chili heat (off-dry riesling + spicy food is the pro cheat-code).
4. **Tannin loves protein & fat, hates fish & chili.** Big red + steak = chemistry (tannins bind protein, mouth resets). Big red + delicate fish = metallic; tannin + capsaicin = amplified burn.
5. **Bubbles scrub.** Carbonation resets a fatty/salty palate — sparkling wine, beer, sparkling tea, seltzer with acid. When in doubt at a mixed table: bubbles.
6. **Regional pairs are pre-solved.** What grows together goes together: tomato-basil + Chianti-style, oysters + Muscadet-style, plov + green tea, borscht + smetana... and rye bread kvass. Use the cuisine's home pairing as the default answer.

Plus the umami warning: high-umami food (aged cheese, mushrooms, soy) makes tannic/oaky wines bitter — go softer, older, or add salt/acid to the dish.

## Non-alcoholic pairing (a craft, not a consolation prize)

Build NA pairings on the same levers — the missing tools are alcohol's body and burn, replace with:
- **Acid backbone:** shrubs (fruit + vinegar + sugar), verjus, citrus-forward spritzes.
- **Tannin/bitter:** strong-brewed teas (black tea = red-wine slot; green/white tea = white-wine slot; oolong = the food all-rounder), tonic, bitter sodas, coffee with desserts.
- **Umami/depth:** kombucha (fermented complexity), kvass with hearty food, tomato-based drinks with savory brunch.
- **Body:** unfiltered juices, oat-milk elements, egg-white-style foams in mocktails.
Rule: NA pairing must have STRUCTURE (acid or bitterness). Plain sweet juice fatigues by mid-meal — the reason "сок к ужину" feels wrong.

## Cooking with wine/beer (kitchen side)
- Cook only with what you'd drink cheerfully cheap — flaws concentrate; "cooking wine" products are salted garbage.
- Dry white = kitchen workhorse (deglazing, seafood, cream sauces); dry red for red-meat braises; don't dump raw wine late — it needs minutes of simmering to integrate (alcohol harshness cooks off, flavor stays; note: never fully to zero — relevant for halal/recovery contexts, see restrictions skill).
- Beer in batter = CO₂ + flavor; dark beer in stews = malty depth (bitterness concentrates — moderate amounts).

## Event drinks math (chain with chef-menu-event-planning)
Per guest per hour: 1 drink (mixed crowd average). Split default 60% wine (of it ~60/40 white+sparkling vs red), 20% beer, 20% NA — shift by menu and crowd. Bottles: 750 ml = 5 glasses. ALWAYS one structured NA option per course, not an afterthought. Ice: 0.5–1 kg per guest for a long evening. Tea/coffee service after dessert is the most-forgotten line item.
