# Chef Spice Guide

Own spice identity, dose and timing. Let chef-flavor-balance own the complete
salt-acid-fat balance and let the selected cuisine own its named traditions.
Do not load either sibling unless it resolves a real additional decision.

## Build a small deliberate profile

Identify the main ingredient, batch size, cooking method, desired flavor and
heat tolerance. Choose one leading note and a few supporting notes rather than
adding every spice in the cupboard. Distinguish aroma from chilli heat.
Clarify an ambiguous spice name before giving a substitution that changes the dish.

Give a batch-scaled starting dose in grams or level measuring spoons. State whole
versus ground, and whether the mix already contains salt. A 'pinch of everything'
is not a usable recipe for someone who cannot estimate by eye.

## Choose timing, not a universal rule

Select dry toasting, blooming in fat, simmering in liquid or late addition according
to the spice and dish. State the observable endpoint and a short first-check time
for fast-burning steps. Prepare the next ingredient before turning on the heat.
Do not claim every useful flavor compound is fat-soluble or that unbloomed spices
are always wrong; dressings, marinades and finishing seasonings are legitimate.

## Diagnose before increasing the dose

- Weak aroma: inspect age, preparation and the dish's salt/acid balance.
- Acrid burnt seasoning: stop the overheating step; do not hide burnt fat under
  extra spices. Rebuild the small spice component when feasible.
- Excess heat: reduce concentration with a compatible unseasoned base; explain
  that sweetness or dairy changes perception rather than removing chilli.
- An overpowering single spice: test dilution on a spoonful before altering the pot.

## Substitution and blends

Replace the functional note, naming the flavor trade. Do not label a homemade
approximation as the authentic named blend. Keep unsalted blend formulas separate
from the dish's salt dose and preserve allergen checks on commercial mixes.
For an unfamiliar blend or a disputed identity, consult an appropriate primary
source rather than fabricating a tradition.

## Output

Give the chosen small profile, dose for the stated batch, stage of addition,
endpoint and one useful adjustment. Low-focus mode: premix the measured blend
before heat so the cook is not opening five jars over a frying pan.
