# Chef Silk Road Cuisine (Middle East · Turkey · Central Asia)

One connected flavor civilization along the old trade routes: yogurt + herbs + warm spices + fire + rice + dough. For a cook in Central Asia this is HOME cuisine — treat it as the native layer, with the Middle Eastern and Turkish branches as близкие родственники, not exotics. Plov spec lives in `chef-world-classics`; shawarma mechanism too. Spice blends (baharat, za'atar, ras el hanout, advieh) live in `chef-spice-guide`.

## The shared grammar

1. **Yogurt is a mother sauce:** garlic-yogurt (manty, kebab), herb-yogurt, jajik/tzatziki family, marinades (lactic acid tenderizes gently — best chicken marinade base on earth), and cooking yogurt into hot dishes (stabilize with a spoon of starch or temper, or it splits — cliff-edge note).
2. **Herbs by the FISTFUL, not the pinch:** dill, cilantro, parsley, mint are vegetables here (Persian sabzi logic). A Silk Road plate without a green mountain is underdressed.
3. **The acid-allium finishing layer:** sumac onions (sliced onion + sumac + salt, squeezed), pickled everything, lemon, pomegranate molasses (narsharab) — richness is always counterweighted (`chef-flavor-balance`).
4. **Fat as flavor carrier:** lamb tail fat (kurdyuk) in the Central Asian branch, olive oil in the Levantine, butter+chili (the Turkish red-oil drizzle: butter + pul biber poured over yogurt dishes = the signature move to steal).
5. **Fire literacy:** mangal (open coals) and tandyr are the reference heats — home adaptation table below.

## The mezze system (composition superpower)
A table of small plates = the original grazing menu: hummus (tahini+lemon+garlic+chickpeas — silky secret: overcooked chickpeas + ice water in the blender), muhammara (walnut-pepper), baba ghanoush (eggplant FULLY collapsed — doneness table), labneh (strained yogurt — ferment skill), dolma, sumac onions, warm flatbread. Mezze is `chef-menu-event-planning` gold: everything make-ahead, room-temp-stable, dietary-flexible (mostly vegan by default).

## Kebab & mangal craft
- **Ground kebab (lyulya/kofta/adana):** fatty lamb or beef (20–30% fat, hand-chopped ideal) + onion JUICE-SQUEEZED (water = kebab falls off skewer — the classic failure) + salt kneaded to TACKY (myosin bind, same science as sausage) + chill hard before skewering. No mangal: rage-hot cast iron or broiler close to element.
- **Chunk kebab (shashlik):** marinade = onion purée + salt + black pepper + (optional) yogurt or narsharab; NOT vinegar-drowning (tough surface myth — meat-guide marinade science); cubes uniform, fat cube between meat cubes; high heat, turn by crust-release cue.
- **Home fire adaptation:** cast iron + finishing under broiler ≈ 80% mangal; liquid smoke drops or smoked paprika = declared trade; for any actual flame/charcoal setup, use only a location and appliance allowed by its instructions and local rules. Tandyr breads → skillet-plus-broiler (the same mechanism swap as pizza — `chef-equipment-gastrophysics`).

## The stew-and-rice civilization
- **Pilaf logic** (plov is the crown, spec in world-classics): fat → protein → onion → carrot/aromatics → liquid → rice on top, NEVER stirred. Variants: Uzbek plov, Persian polow (with tahdig — the crust as the prize: oil+yogurt-rice layer on the pot bottom, low heat 30–40 min, flip like a cake — cliff-edge but glorious), Turkish bulgur pilavı (budget king).
- **Tagine/khoresh logic:** low braise + fruit-and-spice sweetness against meat (apricots, prunes, quince) + acid finish — the sweet-sour thesis shared with German cuisine, different wardrobe. No tagine pot needed: heavy lidded pot, lowest heat (`chef-constraints-adaptation`: weak burner = advantage again).
- **Lagman:** hand-pulled ideal, honest home path = fresh thick noodles + the vaja (meat-pepper-tomato-celery stir-braise) — the noodle spec routes to world-classics dumpling/noodle doughs.
- **Shakshuka:** pepper-tomato base reduced properly (watery base = poached-egg soup), eggs cratered in, lid, whites set yolks loose — the breakfast-for-dinner ambassador.

## The dough belt
Manty (steamed, juicy filling rules from the dumpling family spec), samsa (flaky fat-layered dough — home oven honest version of tandyr samsa), pide/lahmacun (canoe pizzas — pizza skill mechanics apply), lavash/yufka (flatbread on skillet — no-oven bread of `chef-baking-doughs-guide`), gözleme (filled skillet flatbread = fastest impressive lunch in the whole skillset).

## Beginner ramp
(1) Shakshuka + sumac onions → (2) hummus properly silky → (3) gözleme → (4) lyulya discipline (the tacky-knead lesson) → (5) plov by the world-classics spec → (6) tahdig as the flex graduation. Native-pantry advantage: for a Central Asian kitchen every ingredient here is at the nearest bazaar — this is the cuisine track where sourcing friction is zero (`chef-ingredient-localizer` mostly off-duty).
