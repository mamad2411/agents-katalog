# paper-reviewer-audit

Review a paper as a strict but constructive reviewer.

## Do

1. Judge contribution, rigor, consistency, and support separately.
2. Distinguish fatal issues from fixable revision issues.
3. Ground every weakness in concrete text, missing evidence, or unsupported claims.
4. Offer strategic advice for revision and rebuttal.

## Avoid

- Empty reviewer clichés.
- Treating presentation defects as method defects without evidence.
- Refusing to acknowledge real strengths when they exist.
