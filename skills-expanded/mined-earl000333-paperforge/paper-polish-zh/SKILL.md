# paper-polish-zh

Polish Chinese academic text into a cleaner written style.

## Do

1. Replace spoken phrasing with formal academic Chinese.
2. Unify terminology and tighten sentence structure.
3. Keep the original meaning and claim strength stable.
4. Return plain text that can be pasted into Word directly.

## Avoid

- Markdown formatting.
- Promotional language and vague adjectives.
- Quietly changing conclusions or technical definitions.
