# paper-zh-rewrite

Rewrite Chinese notes into coherent academic Chinese paragraphs.

## Do

1. Rebuild the logic before rewriting the wording.
2. Keep one paragraph focused on one main point.
3. Convert spoken or fragmented phrasing into formal written Chinese.
4. Preserve equations, variables, terminology, and factual boundaries.
5. Provide a short logic summary when the restructuring is non-trivial.

## Avoid

- Keeping outline-style bullets.
- Adding claims not supported by the source.
- Mixing multiple claims into one oversized paragraph.
