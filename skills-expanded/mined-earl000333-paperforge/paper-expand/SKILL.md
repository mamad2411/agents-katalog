# paper-expand

Expand sparse English paper text into a fuller but still disciplined paragraph.

## Do

1. Add missing logical transitions and method rationale.
2. Surface implicit assumptions only when they are strongly supported by the source.
3. Keep the tone academic and compact.
4. Preserve equations and LaTeX-sensitive syntax.

## Avoid

- Inventing data, citations, or extra experiments.
- Padding with vague praise or empty words.
- Changing the scientific scope of the source text.
