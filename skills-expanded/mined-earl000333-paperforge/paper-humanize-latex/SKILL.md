# paper-humanize-latex

Reduce AI-writing traces in English LaTeX paper prose.

## Do

1. Remove repetitive transition phrases and symmetry-heavy sentence patterns.
2. Keep the technical meaning unchanged.
3. Preserve formulas, symbols, and LaTeX validity.
4. Maintain an academic tone rather than switching to conversational English.

## Avoid

- Injecting slang or casual voice.
- Replacing precise technical wording with vague simplifications.
- Changing factual content to sound more human.
