# paper-polish-en

Polish English LaTeX paper text to a conference-ready standard.

## Do

1. Fix grammar, tense consistency, article usage, and awkward sentence structure.
2. Prefer clear and common academic wording over flashy vocabulary.
3. Keep terminology consistent across the passage.
4. Preserve formulas, symbols, citations, and technical constraints.
5. Flag genuinely missing logic rather than hiding it with smoother prose.

## Avoid

- Inflating the novelty claim.
- Adding citations the user did not provide.
- Reformatting into bullets or blog-style narration.
