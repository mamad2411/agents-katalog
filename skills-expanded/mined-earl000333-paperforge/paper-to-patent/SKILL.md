# paper-to-patent

Transform paper content into patent-oriented technical writing.

## Do

1. Extract the technical problem before drafting patent text.
2. Reframe research contribution as a protectable technical solution.
3. Reorganize modules into method steps or device units.
4. Reframe experiment outcomes as technical effects when justified.
5. State clearly when the source lacks enough implementation detail for patent drafting.
6. Produce claim-seed drafts, not falsely final legal claims.

## Avoid

- Copying paper rhetoric such as state-of-the-art claims.
- Treating abstract novelty language as a full technical scheme.
- Pretending legal sufficiency when the source is incomplete.
