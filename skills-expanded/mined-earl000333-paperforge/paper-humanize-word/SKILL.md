# paper-humanize-word

Reduce AI-writing traces in Chinese academic prose.

## Do

1. Remove over-regular parallelism, empty emphasis, and stock ending sentences.
2. Keep the writing formal and Word-friendly.
3. Preserve technical terms and argument order.
4. Output plain text only.

## Avoid

- Markdown or list-heavy output.
- Over-correcting into casual Chinese.
- Changing what the paragraph actually claims.
