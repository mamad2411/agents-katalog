## When to Use

Use for any task involving Notion: capturing team decisions (ADRs), extracting insights from meetings, conducting multi-source research, or decomposing product specifications into engineering tasks.



## Procedure

1. Identify the specific Notion task: Knowledge Capture, Meeting Intelligence, Research, or Spec-to-Implementation.

2. For Knowledge Capture: Extract decisions/FAQs from unstructured text and save to the appropriate database (ADR, FAQ, How-To).

3. For Meeting Intelligence: Prepare pre-reads/agendas from Notion context or analyze transcripts for speaking ratios and dynamics.

4. For Research: Conduct multi-source research, synthesize conflicting data, and create high-fidelity reports/comparison grids.

5. For Spec-to-Implementation: Deconstruct PRDs/RFCs into trackable engineering task lists, milestones, and progress tracking.

6. Ensure all outputs are linked to existing Notion pages or added to the correct database using the appropriate templates.



## Pitfalls

- Do not create duplicate records in the same database; always check for existing entries first.

- Ensure that research citations are correctly formatted and linked to source URLs.

- When decomposing specs, ensure the task breakdown is granular enough for individual tickets but high-level enough for milestone tracking.



## Verification

1. The resulting Notion page/database entry is correctly categorized and tagged.

2. Task lists generated from specs are logically ordered and include clear acceptance criteria.

3. Research reports include synthesis and actionable insights, not just raw data.
