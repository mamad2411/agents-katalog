# Alder Optimization Review

Read the bundled [Optimization Review](references/optimization-review.md) in full as the authority before reviewing. It is a byte-identical copy from the pinned source revision in [provenance](references/provenance.json); do not fetch the latest guidance or require the user to select internal documents. Historical evidence links are background, not required review inputs.

1. Read the target repository's `AGENTS.md`. Find current Business Design at the explicit request path, the project's declared path, or conventional `docs/business-design/`. Ask a focused target question if several designs remain plausible; ask for a readable design if none exists. Record its revision or explicit working-tree state. Read the whole relevant design and its connected Activities and Objects before locating the Problem.
2. Identify the actual target Activity, handoff or Human task before proposing changes. For a vague burden request, help the requester locate a few real touchpoints in the whole design and confirm where the burden occurs; do not require an Activity name up front. If the work remains unidentified, return only current-work confirmation, with no Optimization Candidates or Extreme perspectives, even when High Pain is supplied. Where the workflow is outside the model or undescribed, confirm current work instead of inventing Activities.
3. Confirm who experiences which Problem in the identified work, then resolve Pain from the design or explicit requester context. Ask for a concrete example or missing input only where it changes the review; do not re-ask already supplied facts. Keep prompt-only PoC input distinct from agreed facts; it does not waive target identification or human adoption.
4. Apply the authority's Review behavior, boundary decomposition, output fields and stopping conditions. Ground alternatives in actual work and explain the change from existing responsibilities. Keep observations, burden hypotheses and expected benefits distinct. Software or library development and Alder's own operation can be targets when described. A research-only intermediate artifact is not established operational work.
5. Return at most three useful candidates, including zero where appropriate, and only useful separate Extreme perspectives. Lead each candidate with a category or effect-oriented title and the affected work/burden. Name the current work, evidence, responsibility change, preserved meaning, assumptions and decision-relevant questions using the authority's fields. Do not turn source-settled facts or uniquely derived corrections into new human business approvals. If candidates are returned, explicitly ask the responsible requester to accept, modify, defer or reject them and stop awaiting human adoption. Research evidence or CI output alone does not deliver that decision to the requester. Zero candidates does not require a manufactured adoption choice.
6. Keep this review read-only. Do not edit Business Design, generate a maintained intermediate artifact, change JSON, Checks, code or tests, write GitHub, select or approve a candidate, or treat silence as adoption. Skill-development authorization does not adopt an operational change. A human acceptance starts a separate design revision and re-agreement before downstream updates. Include the installed Alder version from `../../plugin.json`, the bundled authority revision/digest, target design revision and installed plugin commit if known.

Requests to draft/revise Business Design belong to `alder-draft-business-design`; requests to review Business Design's quality or correlations belong to `alder-review-business-design`; completed code/DDL/test review belongs to `alder-review-implementation`. A request for structural questions without a known Problem belongs to `alder-discover-business-questions`, under the authority's optional Structural Discovery boundary; do not invent Optimization input.
