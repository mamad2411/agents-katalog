# wp-divi-5-woocommerce-cart-checkout

Use this skill for custom Divi cart or checkout pages and Theme Builder commerce layouts.

## Required Inputs

- WooCommerce cart and checkout page assignments.
- Enabled guest/account checkout behavior.
- Enabled gateways, shipping methods, tax modes, coupons, and consent fields.
- Required billing, shipping, additional-information, order-detail, and payment fields.
- Third-party checkout, gateway, fraud, address, or field extensions.
- Approved staging payment methods and cleanup plan.

## Core Principles

- One effective cart form.
- One effective checkout form.
- No competing shortcode, block, or duplicate Divi form.
- Required modules remain present and visible.
- Notices and validation are part of the functional flow.
- Test totals and state transitions, not only styling.
- Never conduct unapproved production charges or orders.
