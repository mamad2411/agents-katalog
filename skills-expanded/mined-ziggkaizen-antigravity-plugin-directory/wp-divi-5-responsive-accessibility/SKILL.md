# wp-divi-5-responsive-accessibility

Use this skill for a focused responsive and accessibility review of one page, template, component family, or visitor journey.

## Review Boundary

This skill reviews Divi output and configuration. It does not claim a legal accessibility certification and does not replace testing with disabled users or specialist review for high-risk services.

## Breakpoint Strategy

- Always review Desktop, Tablet, and Phone.
- Review Phone Wide, Tablet Wide, Widescreen, and Ultra Wide when enabled or when layout evidence requires them.
- Test exact widths near wrapping, navigation, grid, and typography transitions.
- Verify on a physical touch device for touch-critical changes.

## Interaction Strategy

- Follow the complete keyboard path.
- Check visible focus and focus order.
- Review hover, focus, active, checked, and sticky states where present.
- Verify that hover-only content has a keyboard and touch alternative.
- Confirm dialogs, off-canvas content, menus, and popups can be entered, operated, and exited predictably.
