# wp-divi-5-woocommerce-product-template

Use this skill when building or reviewing the Divi-facing structure of a WooCommerce product page.

## Required Inputs

- Exact Divi, WooCommerce, WordPress, and PHP versions.
- Active theme/child theme and relevant commerce extensions.
- Product types and data states that must work.
- Existing Theme Builder assignments and exclusions.
- Required product information, purchase actions, trust content, and merchandising.
- Tax, stock, review, catalog-visibility, and account settings that affect output.

## Core Principles

- WooCommerce owns product truth and purchase behavior.
- Divi modules display or style that behavior.
- Choose modules from requirements; do not add every available product module.
- Keep current-product or selected-product context explicit.
- Test real products, not only builder placeholders.
- Prefer one clear purchase path and visible feedback.
- Keep related and upsell output intentional and bounded.
