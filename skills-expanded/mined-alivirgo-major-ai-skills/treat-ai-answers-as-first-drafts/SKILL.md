# Treat AI Answers as First Drafts (The 80/20 Finishing Protocol) (AI Skill)

## Overview
Copying and pasting raw, unedited AI output directly into an email, client deliverable, or blog post creates immediate reputational risk. Readers quickly recognize the synthetic cadence, tell-tale vocabulary (*"delve"*, *"testament"*, *"tapestry"*), and unverified assertions.

The **80/20 Finishing Protocol** recognizes AI's true superpower: **curing the tyranny of the blank page**. AI gets you from **0% to 80% in 30 seconds**; your job as a professional is to apply the **4-Step Human Finishing Pass** to take the work from 80% to 100%.

---

## The 80/20 Division of Labor

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 The 80/20 Production Model                  │
│                                                             │
│  [ 0% ──► 80%: AI ENGINE (30 Seconds) ]                     │
│  • Structure, research scaffolding, syntax, initial draft   │
│                                                             │
│  [ 80% ──► 100%: HUMAN FINISHING PASS (2-3 Minutes) ]       │
│  1. FACT GROUNDING  ──► Verify numbers, dates, links        │
│  2. VOICE INFUSION  ──► Add personal anecdotes & opinion    │
│  3. CLICHÉ EXCISION ──► Strike out synthetic AI buzzwords   │
│  4. FINAL QA SIGN-OFF ──► Align with strategic goals        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## The 4-Step Human Finishing Checklist

Before publishing or sending any AI-assisted deliverable:

| Step | Action | What to Look For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Grounding** | Verify every proper noun, statistic, and code import. | Did the AI invent a library or miscalculate a ratio? |
| **2. Voice Injection** | Add 1 personal detail or specific company reference. | Replace generic examples with real client stories. |
| **3. Cliché Cut** | Delete all fluff words. | Strike *"Moreover"*, *"Crucial"*, *"It is important to note"*. |
| **4. Brevity Trim** | Cut total length by 20-30%. | Delete the intro summary and the closing pleasantry. |

## Master First-Draft Generation Prompt

```markdown
Generate a **Rough Working Draft** for [TOPIC / PROPOSAL].

Instructions:
- Focus on strong structure, comprehensive coverage, and logical flow.
- Highlight any areas where you made assumptions with `[NEEDS_USER_DATA]`.
- I will perform the final voice polish and factual verification.
```

## Real-World Case Study

### Scenario: Writing a Client Project Scope Proposal

#### Raw AI Draft (80% Complete)
> *"In today's fast-paced digital ecosystem, having a robust mobile application is crucial for customer engagement. Our comprehensive agile development approach seamlessly delivers end-to-end user satisfaction across all iOS and Android touchpoints..."*

#### Human-Finished Version (100% Polish)
> *"For our Q4 launch, we are building the iOS and Android checkout flow to reduce mobile cart abandonment. We will deliver the Stripe integration and push notification service in a 4-week sprint, targeting live deployment on November 15th."*

**Outcome**: Transformed from generic corporate filler into a crisp, authoritative, client-ready proposal in 60 seconds.
