# Employment Law Intake Evaluation

Screens individual-side employment claims the way a plaintiff's employment attorney would: administrative deadlines, evidence, and damages potential drive viability.

## Workflow

1. **Check deadlines first, always.** Discrimination/harassment claims typically require an administrative charge (EEOC or state agency) before suit, with a short filing window (often 180-300 days depending on jurisdiction and whether a state agency has a work-sharing agreement — don't assume a specific number, flag that it needs jurisdiction-specific confirmation). Wage claims and wrongful termination have their own separate statutes of limitations. Flag whichever deadline is closest.
2. **Classify claim type**: wrongful termination (at-will exception — public policy, contract, retaliation), discrimination/harassment (protected class basis), wage and hour (unpaid overtime, misclassification, meal/rest break violations).
3. **Assess evidence strength**: documentary evidence (emails, performance reviews, pay records), witness availability, whether a comparator exists (for discrimination — similarly situated employees treated differently), and timing (temporal proximity between protected activity and adverse action, for retaliation claims).
4. **Assess damages potential**: lost wages (back pay/front pay), emotional distress, whether punitive damages are plausible (requires malice/reckless indifference showing), and mitigation efforts (has the client sought new employment).
5. **Flag red flags**: documented performance issues predating the claim, at-will employment with no protected basis identified, missed administrative deadline already.
6. **Score and summarize** (format below).

## Summary format (per submission)

```
**[Name]** — [Claim type] | Last day worked/incident date: [date]

- **Deadline status:** [within window / approaching ⚠️ / administrative charge deadline unclear — confirm jurisdiction]
- **Evidence strength:** [strong/moderate/weak — why: documents, comparators, witnesses]
- **Damages potential:** [estimate range of factors, not a dollar figure — back pay period, emotional distress, punitive plausibility]
- **Red flags:** [documented performance issues, no protected basis identified, etc. — "None" if none]
- **Score: X/10**
- **Take assessment:** [take / pass / needs more info, with reason]
```

## Scoring rubric (10 points)

| Factor | Points | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Deadline status | 2 | Well within window = full; approaching or unclear = 0-1 |
| Evidence strength | 4 | Strong documentary/comparator evidence = full; weak/he-said-she-said only = low |
| Damages potential | 3 | Meaningful back pay/front pay or strong emotional distress showing = full; minimal damages = low |
| Absence of disqualifying red flags | 1 | No documented performance issues undermining the claim, clear protected basis identified |

## Notes

- Never assert a specific administrative filing deadline (180 vs. 300 days) without confirming the jurisdiction and whether a work-sharing agreement applies — flag this explicitly rather than guessing.
- Never estimate a specific settlement or damages dollar figure — describe the factors that drive damages, not a number.
