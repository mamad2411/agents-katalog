# Estate Planning / Probate Intake Evaluation

Screens new estate planning and probate matters for complexity and urgency, since these two factors — not case "size" — determine what kind of engagement it actually is.

## Workflow

1. **Check urgency first.** Flag prominently: terminal diagnosis or advanced age with no existing plan, incapacity already occurring (no valid POA in place), or a probate matter with an imminent deadline (creditor claim periods, will contest filing windows).
2. **Assess asset complexity**: number and type of assets (real property across states, business interests, retirement accounts with beneficiary designation issues, out-of-state property triggering ancillary probate), blended family/multiple marriages, minor or special-needs beneficiaries.
3. **Assess family conflict risk**: estranged relationships, prior disinheritance, blended family tension, any indication of undue influence concerns or likely will contest.
4. **For probate specifically**: testate vs. intestate, whether the will is being contested, and creditor/claim status.
5. **Score and summarize** (format below).

## Summary format (per submission)

```
**[Name]** — [New estate plan / Probate matter]

- **Urgency:** [routine / time-sensitive ⚠️ — why]
- **Asset complexity:** [simple / moderate / complex — key factors]
- **Family conflict risk:** [low / moderate / high — why]
- **Score: X/10**
- **Take assessment:** [take / pass / needs more info, with reason]
```

## Scoring rubric (10 points, oriented toward "good engagement fit" not case value)

| Factor | Points | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Urgency handled appropriately | 2 | Full points if urgency is clear and manageable within firm capacity; flag separately if urgent and firm can't move fast enough |
| Complexity matches firm capability | 4 | Full points if asset/family complexity is within the firm's typical scope; lower if it needs specialists (tax, special-needs trusts, multi-state) |
| Conflict risk manageable | 3 | Low conflict risk = full; high risk = flag need for conflict-of-interest and engagement-letter care |
| Completeness of information | 1 | Enough info to scope the engagement |

## Notes

- Never assess undue influence or capacity as a legal conclusion — flag the factual indicators and note it needs attorney judgment (and possibly medical documentation).
- Urgency (incapacity, terminal illness, contest deadlines) should never be buried under complexity scoring — call it out first.
