# Live Product Lineup

Plan the optimal product reveal order for TikTok Live sessions based on price anchoring, audience warmup, and peak engagement timing. This skill turns a flat product list into a sequenced live show script that maximizes audience retention, conversion per viewer, and average order value throughout a session.

## Quick Reference

| Decision | Strong | Acceptable | Weak |
|----------|--------|------------|------|
| Opening product | High visual appeal, low price (<$30), fast close | Mid-price, strong hook | High-ticket first |
| Price arc | Low → Mid → High → Flash deal | Mostly consistent range | Random / descending |
| Session length | 60–90 min with 3 act structure | 30–60 min with 2 acts | <20 min or >2 hrs unplanned |
| Scarcity mechanic | Real limited stock with live counter | "Limited time" with countdown | No scarcity signals |
| Re-engagement hook | Teaser of next hero product every 15 min | Periodic teaser | No forward hooks |
| Transition pacing | 5–7 min per product | 8–10 min per product | <3 min (rushed) or >15 min (drops) |
| Closing product | High-AOV bundle or exclusive collab | Best-seller at discount | Weakest product last |

## Solves

1. **Viewer drop-off in the first 10 minutes** — Opening with a high-ticket or boring product loses the audience before the hook is set; the fix is a fast, visual, low-cost opener
2. **Low GMV despite high viewership** — Audience watching but not buying; usually caused by misaligned price points (too expensive for the audience warmth stage) or weak scarcity mechanics
3. **Flat session energy** — No arc, no peaks, no surprises; planned emotional beats and "host excitement" cues create natural momentum
4. **Early exits before the best products** — Without forward hooks ("stay for the mystery bundle at the 45-minute mark"), viewers leave before the high-margin products appear
5. **Low average order value** — Products shown in isolation without bundle suggestions miss the cross-sell window that live commerce enables
6. **Creator fatigue and dead air** — Unplanned transitions between products create awkward pauses; a tight script with segue lines eliminates dead air
7. **Inventory mismatch** — Running out of the opening product kills momentum; stock calculations must be built into the lineup plan

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Gather Inputs
Collect from the seller/creator:
- Full product list with prices, available stock quantities, and any existing sales data
- Session target duration (30 min / 60 min / 90 min / 2 hrs)
- Target audience: new viewers (discovery) vs. existing followers (loyalty)
- Platform: TikTok Live, Shopee Live, YouTube Shopping, Instagram Live
- Any confirmed flash deal or hero product the brand wants to feature
- Past session replay data: when viewership peaked and dropped

### Step 2 — Classify Products by Role

| Role | Criteria | Position |
|------|----------|----------|
| **Hook product** | <$30, high visual appeal, satisfying reveal, fast demo | First 5 min |
| **Warm-up products** | $20–$60, easy demo, broad appeal, clear benefit | Min 5–25 |
| **Hero product** | Best-seller or new launch; mid-high price; core GMV driver | Min 25–45 |
| **Price anchor** | Highest-priced item in lineup; makes hero seem reasonable | Before hero |
| **Flash deal** | Time-limited discount on popular product; creates urgency spike | Min 45–60 |
| **Closing bundle** | 2–3 products grouped at AOV-boosting price; "show exclusive" | Final 10 min |

### Step 3 — Build the Price Arc
Map product prices on a timeline:
- **Act 1 (0–20 min):** $10–$40 — audience warmup, easy yeses
- **Act 2 (20–55 min):** $30–$120 — hero products, peak viewer count
- **Act 3 (55–end):** Flash deal ($15–$60 special) → Closing bundle ($80–$200)

The price arc should look like a wave, not a flat line or random scatter.

### Step 4 — Write the Transition Script

For each product, write:
1. **Intro hook** (10 sec) — Creates anticipation before reveal
2. **Demo/show** (3–4 min) — Live demonstration, UGC clip playback, or feature walk-through
3. **Close mechanic** (60 sec) — "We only have 47 left, add to cart now, I'll count down from 10"
4. **Forward hook** (15 sec) — Teases the next product to hold viewers

Template:
```
[HOOK]: "Okay chat, you're not ready for what I'm about to show you..."
[DEMO]: [Product features + live demo + comment reading]
[CLOSE]: "Cart link is pinned — we have [STOCK COUNT] left. First [X] orders get [BONUS]."
[FORWARD HOOK]: "Don't leave yet — in about 10 minutes I'm dropping something I've literally never shown on live before."
```

### Step 5 — Stock Allocation Planning
For each lineup slot, calculate safe stock allocation:
- **Hook product:** 30–50 units (fast close, high conversion rate expected)
- **Warm-up products:** 20–40 units each
- **Hero product:** 50–100 units (main GMV driver; ensure no stockout)
- **Flash deal:** 20–30 units (scarcity is real; counter reinforces it)
- **Reserve:** 10% buffer per product for re-show if energy spikes

### Step 6 — Engagement Mechanics Schedule
Insert engagement beats every 10–12 minutes:
- **Comment prompt:** "Tell me in the comments: have you tried [product category] before?"
- **Poll/quiz:** "Which color do you want me to show next — pink or blue?"
- **Countdown close:** "Pinning the link now — 47 left, adding to cart takes 10 seconds"
- **Giveaway tease:** "The 500th person to share this live gets a free [hook product]"

## Examples

### Example 1 — 60-Minute Beauty Live (TikTok Shop)

**Product list:** Lip gloss ($12), Face mist ($28), Foundation ($45), Skincare set ($89), Eye cream ($38), Mascara ($22)

**Optimized Lineup:**

| Time | Product | Price | Role | Stock |
|------|---------|-------|------|-------|
| 0:00–5:00 | Lip Gloss | $12 | Hook — fast demo, satisfying color reveal | 40 units |
| 5:00–12:00 | Mascara | $22 | Warmup — relatable daily use | 30 units |
| 12:00–22:00 | Eye Cream | $38 | Warmup → transition | 25 units |
| 22:00–25:00 | Skincare Set tease | $89 | Price anchor — show without selling | — |
| 25:00–38:00 | Foundation | $45 | **Hero** — live application demo | 60 units |
| 38:00–46:00 | Face Mist | $28 | Reset + cross-sell to foundation buyers | 35 units |
| 46:00–52:00 | Flash Deal: Mascara + Lip Gloss bundle | $29 (save $5) | Flash deal | 25 bundles |
| 52:00–60:00 | Skincare Set | $89 (exclusive kit add-on) | Closing bundle | 20 units |

### Example 2 — 90-Minute Home Goods Live (Shopee Live)

**Optimized arc principle:** Open with a $15 kitchen gadget (high visual demo appeal), build through $25–$60 storage and organization products (broad appeal), peak at $120 cookware set (hero), close with a bundle of top sellers at session-exclusive pricing.

**Energy cues written into script:**
- Min 15: "Drop a 🔥 if you want to see the next product — we'll reveal it early if we hit 50 comments"
- Min 30: Comment poll on color variant for hero product
- Min 60: Flash deal countdown — "3 minutes, 22 units remaining, link is live"

## Common Mistakes

1. **Opening with the most expensive product** — High-ticket items before trust is established lose cold viewers immediately
2. **No price anchor before hero product** — Without showing a $150 item first, the $75 hero feels expensive; anchor high before presenting the value
3. **Forgetting to tease upcoming products** — Viewers who don't know what's coming have no reason to stay
4. **Real inventory not counted before going live** — Claiming "only 20 left" when there are 200 destroys trust when buyers check later; always verify stock
