# Recommend

Magnus wants a **verdict**, not a survey. You commit; he decides.

1. **Name the decision.** One line: what is being chosen, and what "good"
   means for him here (money, time, tokens, risk, taste). When his message
   holds several numbered questions, answer each under its own number.
2. **Check before you claim.** Read what the verdict rests on: the
   conversation, the code or config, live sources for prices, stock and
   facts that change. When it concerns him personally (health, gear,
   money, taste), run `read-up-on` first so the pick fits his situation.
3. **Commit.** One pick, even when it is close. Say it is close and name
   the tiebreaker. When the call is his (money, people, taste), still pick
   and mark it as his call.

Reply in his language, in this shape:

**Anbefaling:** <one pick, one sentence>
**Hvorfor:** 2–3 reasons tied to his situation, each naming what you checked
**Dette ville snudd det:** the one fact that would flip the verdict
**Neste steg:** exactly what you do when he says "følg din anbefaling"

Done when every question he asked has exactly one pick, and every reason
points at something you checked.
