# DEPRECATED: capacity-plan

This skill has been deprecated for a solo cybersecurity-focused founder/operator workflow.

## Reason for deprecation

Capacity planning is built around workload analysis and utilization forecasting across multiple people — hours per FTE, allocations per project, hire-vs-deprioritize decisions. As a solo operator, the math collapses: you are the only resource, your "utilization" is just your task list, and decisions to hire or deprioritize are gut/cash-flow calls, not spreadsheet outputs. Running this skill against a team-of-one produces formal-looking artifacts that don't change what you do next.

## Use these alternatives instead

| Need | Skill |
|---|---|
| Track your own bandwidth and what you've committed to | `productivity:task-management` |
| Decide what to take on next or push out (priority calls) | `personal-assistant` |

## Restoration

If your role changes (e.g., you start hiring contractors or employees and need to allocate work across people), restore the original from the originally-installed plugin location, or remove this stub from `~/.claude/skills/capacity-plan/SKILL.md` so the original takes precedence.
