# Analyze Monitors

1. Identify the monitor, owning service, signal, tags/groups, and operational purpose.
2. Inspect threshold logic, evaluation window, recovery behavior, no-data handling, and notification routing.
3. Review alert history and correlate alerts with real incidents.
4. Identify duplicate, overly broad, flapping, or low-actionability monitors.
5. Preserve detection coverage while reducing noise.
6. Do not disable production monitors unless explicitly authorized.

## Output
- Monitor Intent
- Alert Behavior
- Noise/Coverage Findings
- Routing Findings
- Recommended Changes
