# Analyze Data Streams

1. Resolve producer, consumer, topic/stream, environment, and time window.
2. Inspect end-to-end flow, throughput, latency, consumer lag, and service dependencies available in Datadog.
3. Correlate stream degradation with application traces/logs and infrastructure events.
4. Determine whether delay originates with producer, broker/stream, consumer, or downstream processing.
5. Route platform-specific diagnosis to `kafka-*` or `eventhub-*` skills when direct platform evidence is required.

## Output
- Stream Topology
- Lag/Latency Evidence
- Producer/Consumer Findings
- Failure Layer
- Recommended Next Skill
