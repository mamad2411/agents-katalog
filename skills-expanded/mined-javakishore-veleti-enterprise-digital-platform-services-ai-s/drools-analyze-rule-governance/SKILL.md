# Analyze Rule Governance

1. Identify rule owners, source repository, artifact/version strategy, and promotion path.
2. Inspect traceability from business requirement to rule, test, artifact, and deployment.
3. Review approval and segregation-of-duties expectations where applicable.
4. Verify decisions can be tied to the ruleset version that produced them.
5. Identify unmanaged runtime changes or environment drift.
6. Keep governance proportional to business and regulatory risk.

## Output
- Ownership/Traceability
- Version/Promotion Controls
- Auditability
- Governance Gaps
- Recommended Controls
