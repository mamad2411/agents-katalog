# Analyze SLO

1. Identify the SLO, SLI definition, target, evaluation window, and owning service.
2. Inspect current attainment, error budget, burn behavior, and contributing signals.
3. Determine which endpoints, errors, regions, versions, or dependencies contribute most to the breach.
4. Correlate with incidents and deployments.
5. Distinguish SLO definition problems from genuine service reliability problems.
6. Do not change targets merely to eliminate an active breach.

## Output
- SLO State
- Error Budget/Burn
- Contributing Signals
- Definition Quality Findings
- Recommended Action
