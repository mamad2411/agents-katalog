# Analyze Traces

1. Resolve service, environment, endpoint/resource, and time window.
2. Identify representative failing and successful traces when available.
3. Follow the request across service boundaries.
4. Compare span duration, status, errors, retries, and dependency timing.
5. Locate the earliest confirmed failing or abnormal span.
6. Correlate trace evidence with logs, deployment version, and infrastructure signals.
7. Do not label a downstream service as root cause solely because its span is slow.

## Output
- Request Path
- Critical Spans
- Latency/Error Evidence
- First Confirmed Failure
- Recommended Next Skill
