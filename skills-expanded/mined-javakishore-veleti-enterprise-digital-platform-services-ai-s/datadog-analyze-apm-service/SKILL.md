# Analyze APM Service

1. Resolve service, environment, version, and comparison window.
2. Inspect request rate, errors, latency distributions, top resources/endpoints, and dependency behavior.
3. Compare versions when a deployment or regression is suspected.
4. Identify whether degradation is broad or isolated to particular resources or dependencies.
5. Drill into representative traces for evidence.
6. Correlate with runtime/container metrics where relevant.

## Output
- Service Health
- Affected Resources
- Version/Dependency Findings
- Trace Evidence
- Recommended Action
