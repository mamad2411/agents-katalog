# Analyze Ingestion Cost

1. Identify teams/services and telemetry products in scope.
2. Inspect high-volume log sources, indexing/retention, custom metrics, tag cardinality, and trace ingestion/sampling.
3. Separate useful high-value telemetry from redundant or low-value volume.
4. Identify cost drivers by service/team/source where evidence permits.
5. Preserve incident, security, compliance, and SLO requirements.
6. Recommend filters, sampling, cardinality reduction, or retention changes with explicit observability tradeoffs.

## Output
- Primary Volume/Cost Drivers
- High-Cardinality Sources
- Optimization Opportunities
- Coverage Tradeoffs
- Recommended Priorities
