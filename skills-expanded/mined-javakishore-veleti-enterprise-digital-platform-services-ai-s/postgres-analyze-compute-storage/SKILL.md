# Analyze Compute and Storage

1. Identify compute tier, size, storage type/capacity, provisioned IOPS/throughput, and growth.
2. Inspect CPU, memory, storage latency, IOPS, throughput, and capacity trends.
3. Correlate saturation with query, vacuum, checkpoint, and workload behavior.
4. Evaluate Burstable, General Purpose, or Memory Optimized fit against workload characteristics.
5. Verify current regional/platform support before recommending a storage option.
6. Do not resize production resources without explicit authorization.

## Output
- Compute/Storage Profile
- Saturation Evidence
- Workload Fit
- Scaling Options
- Recommended Action
