# Analyze Upgrade and Maintenance

1. Identify current/target Kafka version, cluster type, broker type, and client ecosystem.
2. Inspect planned or recent version/configuration/maintenance changes.
3. Evaluate client, connector, Streams, schema, and protocol compatibility.
4. Review partition replication and workload resilience for rolling changes.
5. Correlate incidents with maintenance or upgrade timelines.
6. Do not initiate production upgrades without explicit authorization.

## Output
- Current/Target State
- Compatibility Findings
- Maintenance/Change Evidence
- Availability Risk
- Recommended Plan
