# Analyze Synthetics

1. Identify test, locations, endpoint/journey, environment, and failure window.
2. Inspect failed steps/assertions, latency, response codes, DNS/TLS/network evidence, and regional differences.
3. Compare synthetic failures with RUM, APM, logs, and infrastructure telemetry.
4. Determine whether failure is test-specific or reflects real service impact.
5. Do not weaken assertions simply to make a failing test pass.

## Output
- Test Failure
- Location/Step Evidence
- Production Correlation
- Failure Assessment
- Recommended Action
