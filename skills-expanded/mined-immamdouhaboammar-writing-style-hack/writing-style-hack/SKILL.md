## 1. Foundational Copywriting Principles

Every piece of content must respect these 4 core tenets:

1. **Clarity over Cleverness**: If forced to choose between creative wordplay and crystal-clear understanding, choose clear every single time.
2. **Benefits over Features**: Focus on the tangible transformation or outcome for the reader, never mere capability descriptions.
3. **Specificity over Vagueness**: Concrete numbers, timeframes, and metrics beat abstract assertions. ("Saves 3 hours every week" beats "saves valuable time").
4. **Customer Language over Corporate Jargon**: Write the way smart practitioners speak over coffee. Banish buzzwords.

### Prohibited Vocabulary & Buzzwords
- Never write: *"utilize"*, *"facilitate"*, *"commence"*, *"streamline"*, *"optimize"*, *"leverage"*, *"synergy"*, *"revolutionize"*, *"game-changer"*, *"delve"*, *"tapestry"*, *"testament"*.
- Replace with: *"use"*, *"help"*, *"start"*, *"improve"*, *"cut"*, *"show"*, *"build"*.

### Voice & Cadence Rules
- **Active Voice**: Write *"We engineered this system"* instead of *"This system was engineered"*.
- **Ruthless Trimming**: Strip weak qualifiers (*"very"*, *"really"*, *"just"*, *"actually"*, *"literally"*).
- **Short Paragraphs**: 1–3 sentences per paragraph maximum. Use single-sentence paragraphs to punctuate critical takeaways.
- **CTA Formula**: `[Action Verb] + [What They Get] + [Qualifier]`.
  - ✅ Good: *"Start My Free Trial"*, *"Get the Complete Checklist"*, *"Join 10,000+ Subscribers"*.
  - ❌ Bad: *"Submit"*, *"Click Here"*, *"Learn More"*.

## 3. Step-by-Step Generation Workflow

When generating content under this skill:

1. **Classify Platform**: Determine if the output is Newsletter, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, or General Copy.
2. **Select Archetype & Hook**: Choose one of the verified hook formulas matching the topic.
3. **Draft with Progressive Disclosure**:
   - Deliver strongest value in the opening 20%.
   - Inject specific numbers, names, or real benchmarks.
   - Maintain conversational cadence (1–3 sentences per paragraph).
4. **Enforce Formatting**: Double spacing, scannable subheadings, punchy bullet points.
5. **Quality Review Checklist**:
   - [ ] No corporate buzzwords or AI filler phrases.
   - [ ] Benefits stated before features.
   - [ ] Actionable CTA present.
   - [ ] Tone calibrated to the target platform.
