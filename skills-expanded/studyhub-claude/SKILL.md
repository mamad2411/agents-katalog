# StudyHub

One-stop platform for students navigating higher education decisions.

## What It Solves

Students juggle fragmented info across dozens of sites when planning higher studies — tuition costs, living expenses, job prospects, loan options, scholarships. StudyHub brings it all into one place so they can compare, plan, and decide with confidence.

## Core Domains

- **Tuition & Costs** — Program fees, living costs, hidden expenses by school/city
- **Jobs & Career Outcomes** — Post-graduation employment rates, salary data, career paths
- **Loans & Financial Aid** — Loan options, interest rates, repayment calculators, scholarships
- **Admissions** — Requirements, deadlines, acceptance rates
