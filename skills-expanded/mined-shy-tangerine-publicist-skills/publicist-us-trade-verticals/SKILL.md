# US trade vertical media, US market

Use this guide to identify US industry publications and tailor B2B pitches. Verify each outlet's current beat, contact route, and editorial calendar before outreach. Pair it with `publicist-us`.

---

## Why trade verticals matter

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| **Trade publications in US** | 1,000+ |
| **B2B decision-makers reached** | 80%+ read trade pubs |
| **Conversion rate** | 3-5x general media |
| **Pitch competition** | Lower than national media |

## Pitch template by vertical

```
Subject: [VERTICAL] [Story Type]: [Specific Hook], [Company/Source]

Hi [Reporter Name],

I read your recent piece on [specific topic], especially its focus on [specific detail].

We have [data/announcement/expert] that extends your story on [topic]:

[1 sentence: what changed]
[1 sentence: specific numbers/names/locations]
[1 sentence: why this matters to your readers right now]

Available: [Spokesperson name, title, availability windows]
Materials: [Data, demo access, photos, customer reference. Provide links only.]

Best,
[Your Name]
[Title]
[Phone]
[Email]
```

## Finding the right reporter (per vertical)

| Method | Effort | Success Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Read outlet for 1 week, note bylines** | 30 min | Highest |
| **Muck Rack / Cision search by beat** | 10 min | High |
| **Twitter/X search: "beat reporter" + vertical** | 5 min | Medium |
| **LinkedIn: "reporter" + vertical + outlet** | 10 min | Medium |
| **Muck Rack / Cision database** | 5 min | High (if subscribed) |
