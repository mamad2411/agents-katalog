# WeChat public account (微信公众号), China market

**Scope**. Only WeChat 公众号 commercial/editorial engagement. Not a general China skill, pair with `publicist-china`.

## Platform rules (2024-25 verified)

### Mandatory: 腾讯广告互选平台
All commercial cooperation MUST go through official platform. Four modes:
| Mode | Description | Pricing | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| 原创定制 | Creator writes full custom article per brand brief | 文章一口价 | Brand reviews draft before publish |
| 供稿直发 | Creator edits brand-supplied content lightly | 文章一口价 | Brand reviews draft |
| 种草合集 | One article, multiple brand slots (坑位) | 按坑位定价 | Brand confirms offline |
| 图片消息 | Up to 20 images, each with ad component | 图片消息一口价 | Brand reviews |

**Platform fee**. 5% of ad spend (technical service fee). No exceptions.
**AI content**. Must carry 显性标识 (visible label) + 隐性标识 (metadata) per 2025-09-01 《人工智能生成合成内容标识办法》
**Violations**. Warning → limited forwarding → deletion → account function suspension (search/follow/share disabled)

### Content classification (required per pitch)
Every pitch must declare target classification:
- `editorial`, pure editorial, no commercial exchange
- `企业供稿`, brand-contributed content, labeled
- `付费合作`, paid cooperation, labeled
- `分发`, distribution service (e.g., PR wire)

**Never imply** a public account accepts unsolicited bulk messages.

## Pitch protocol

### Pre-pitch verification
1. **Account identity**. Verify 微信认证主体 (certified entity) matches target outlet
2. **Submission convention**. Check account's historical 投稿约定 (usually in menu/auto-reply)
3. **Recent editorial rhythm**. Read last 5-10 articles for beat, format, length, tone
4. **Commercial signals**. Look for "广告" labels, 互选卡片, 小程序链接, H5/二维码, classify accordingly

### WeChat-first contact (not email)
1. **Add contact**. 微信备注 = `姓名/公司/事由/目标账号`
2. **Pre-warm**. Read their 朋友圈 / 公众号近期文 3-5 days before first message
3. **First message** (single, concise):
   ```
   [编辑/运营你好] 我是[公司]的[姓名]，关注贵号[具体栏目/文章]有一阵了。
   我们有[本地化落地故事/数据/专家]，与贵号[近期选题]高度契合。
   材料已备齐[脱敏数据表/专家中文简历/官方投稿链接]，属于[editorial/企业供稿/付费合作]。
   请问是否适合进一步沟通？如需核对，北京时间[时间段]通过贵方官方渠道回复即可。
   ```
4. **Follow-up**. Once, 3-5 Chinese business days later. No bulk add, no group blast.

### Article format for WeChat
- **Title**. Concrete, news-value-first, avoid English idioms
- **Lead**. Local consequence in first paragraph (not global expansion)
- **Evidence**. Verified Chinese names, methodology, limitations, source links
- **Disclosure**. Explicit `editorial` / `企业供稿` / `付费合作` / `分发` statement
- **CTA**. Clear request (interview / byline / data share / expert quote)
- **Availability**. China Standard Time window

## Compliance checklist (per pitch)
- [ ] Classification declared (`editorial`/`企业供稿`/`付费合作`/`分发`)
- [ ] AI segments → 显性标识 (文首/文末/交互界面) + 隐性元数据
- [ ] Commercial route → 腾讯广告互选平台 (no private deals)
- [ ] 记者/运营身份 → 微信认证主体核验
- [ ] 敏感行业/跨境数据/平台权限 → `NEEDS_REVIEW` + 法务确认
