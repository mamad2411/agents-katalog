# US state government press offices, US market

Use this guide to identify state government press offices and plan media access across the 50 states and DC. Verify current contact details and requirements on official state pages. Pair it with `publicist-us` and `publicist-us-regional-media`.

---

## State press office structure

### Every state has
| Office | Role | Contact Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| **Governor's Press Office** | Chief executive communications | governor.[state].gov/press |
| **Agency PIOs** | Individual agency communications | agency.[state].gov/news or /media |
| **Legislature Press** | House/Senate communications | legislature.[state].gov/media |
| **Attorney General Press** | Legal/consumer protection | ag.[state].gov/media |
| **Supreme Court PIO** | Judicial branch | courts.[state].gov/media |

### PIO networks (professional associations)
| Organization | Scope | Resource |
|---|---|---|
| **NAGPIA** | National Association of Government PIOs | nagpia.org |
| **State PIO Associations** | Each state has own | e.g., CAPIO (CA), TAPIO (TX) |
| **NASAO** | State aviation officials | nasao.org |
| **NCSL** | Legislative staff | ncsl.org |

## FOIA / FOIL (public records requests)

### Federal (FOIA)
| Agency | Portal | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **DOJ** | foia.gov | 20 business days |
| **EPA** | foiaonline.gov | 20 business days |
| **SEC** | sec.gov/foia | 20 business days |

### State FOIL (varies)
| State | Law Name | Portal | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CA** | CPRA | agency.ca.gov/records | 10 days |
| **NY** | FOIL | open.ny.gov | 5 days (ack) / 20 days |
| **TX** | PIA | texasattorneygeneral.gov | 10 days |
| **FL** | Public Records Law | flgov.com/public-records | "Promptly" |
| **IL** | FOIA | foia.illinois.gov | 5 days |
| **PA** | RTKL | openrecords.pa.gov | 5 days |

Use the state-specific portal, cite the applicable statute, and describe the records sought.

## Accreditation for state events

| Event Type | Credentialing Body | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Legislative Session** | Legislature Press Office | 2-4 weeks before |
| **Governor Events** | Governor's Press Office | 1-2 weeks |
| **Agency Hearings** | Agency PIO | 1-2 weeks |
| **Emergency/Disaster** | State EMA | Real-time |
| **Court Proceedings** | Court PIO / Clerk | Varies |

Typical requirements:
- Press credentials / media affiliation proof
- Editorial assignment letter
- Equipment list (camera, audio)
- Background check (some states)

## Press conference protocols (state level)

### Governor press conferences
| Element | Protocol |
|---|---|
| **Notice** | 24-48 hours (emergency: immediate) |
| **Credentialing** | Pre-registration required |
| **Pool Feed** | Often provided (TV/radio) |
| **Q&A** | Moderated by Press Secretary |
| **Embargo** | Rare; usually immediate release |

### Agency press briefings
| Element | Protocol |
|---|---|
| **Notice** | 1-2 weeks (scheduled) |
| **Format** | Statement + Q&A |
| **Materials** | Fact sheets, slides, data posted online |
| **Recording** | Usually allowed (confirm) |

## PIO relationship building

| Action | Frequency | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Attend press briefings** | Weekly during session | Face time |
| **Provide expert sources** | Ongoing | Become go-to |
| **Flag upcoming announcements** | 1 week ahead | Respect their planning |
| **Correct errors fast** | Immediate | Credibility |
| **Respect embargoes** | Always | Trust |
| **Annual PIO conference** | Annual | Network |
