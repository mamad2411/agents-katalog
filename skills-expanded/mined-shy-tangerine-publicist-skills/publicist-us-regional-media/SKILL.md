## Editorial calendar patterns

| Season | Key Hooks | Pitch Timing |
|---|---|---|
| **Jan-Mar** | Legislative sessions (most states), Budget, New laws | Dec-Jan for session preview |
| **Apr-Jun** | Legislative wrap, Graduation, Tourism season | 4-6 weeks ahead |
| **Jul-Sep** | Back-to-school, State fairs, Budget FY | 6-8 weeks for events |
| **Oct-Dec** | Election cycle (even years), Holiday, Year-end | 4-6 weeks |

## Pitch email template (regional)

```
Subject: [STATE/REGION] [Story type]: [Specific hook], [Why it matters locally]

Hi [First name],

[1 paragraph: the local hook + why readers in the state/region care now]
[1 paragraph: the data/source/exclusive available + a local spokesperson]
[1 paragraph: suggested format + availability in the outlet's time zone]

Happy to align by phone or email: [phone] | [email]
Best regards,
[Name], [Organization], [Phone/Email]
```
