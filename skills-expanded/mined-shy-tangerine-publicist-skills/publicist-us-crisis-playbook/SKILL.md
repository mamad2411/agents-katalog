# US crisis communication playbook, US market

Use this playbook for US crisis communications. Pair it with `publicist-us` and `publicist-us-compliance`.

---

## US crisis cycle

### The US crisis timeline
```
T+0 min    : Event breaks on Twitter/X
T+5 min    : First viral clips, hashtags forming
T+15 min   : Cable news (CNN, MSNBC, Fox) picks up
T+30 min   : Wire services (AP, Reuters, Bloomberg) alert
T+1 hr     : Major print (NYT, WSJ, WaPo) assigning reporters
T+2-4 hr   : First wire stories, cable segments
T+6-12 hr  : Morning/evening shows booking guests
T+24 hr    : Print editions, deep-dive pieces
T+48-72 hr : Investigative follow-ups, congressional interest
T+1 week   : Regulatory/legal actions, sustained cycle
```

### Key difference vs other markets
| Market | First Break | Cable Equivalent | Wire Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **US** | Twitter/X | Cable news (24/7) | AP/Reuters/Bloomberg = authority |
| **UK** | Twitter | BBC/Sky | PA/Reuters |
| **EU** | Twitter | National broadcasters | National agencies |
| **China** | Weibo/WeChat | State TV | Xinhua = authority |

The typical US sequence is social media, cable news, wire services, then print. Recheck the actual order and timing for each incident.

## Crisis war room (first 60 minutes)

### Roles & responsibilities
| Role | Person | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| **Crisis Lead** | CCO / Senior VP Comms | Decisions, spokesperson approval, legal sync |
| **Legal Counsel** | GC / Outside counsel | Approve all statements, privilege protection |
| **Monitoring Lead** | Social/media intelligence | Real-time tracking (Twitter, cable, wires) |
| **Stakeholder Lead** | IR / Govt Relations | Investors, regulators, employees, partners |
| **Spokesperson** | CEO / Pre-trained exec | Authorized voice only |
| **Digital Lead** | Social media manager | Owned channels, monitoring, response |
| **Logistics** | EA / Comms coordinator | Scheduling, room, tech, food |

### War room setup (physical/virtual)
- **Dedicated Slack/Teams channel**. "Crisis-[Client]-[Date]"
- **Monitoring dashboard**. Twitter (TweetDeck), Cable (TV Eyes/Critical Mention), Wires (AP/Reuters/Bloomberg alerts)
- **Document repo**. Google Drive/SharePoint, holding statements, FAQs, bios, visuals
- **Approval chain**. Legal approves first, then the Crisis Lead, then the spokesperson. No solo approvals.

## Holding statements (first 15 minutes)

### Template library (pre-approved)

#### Type 1: acknowledgment (no facts yet)
```
We are aware of reports regarding [incident] affecting [location/people].
Our first priority is the safety of [employees/customers/community].
We are gathering facts and will provide an update within [timeframe].
For immediate questions: [dedicated email/phone].
```

#### Type 2: action taken (partial facts)
```
We can confirm [specific fact] occurred at [time] in [location].
We have [activated emergency response / engaged authorities / secured facility].
No injuries reported / [X] injuries confirmed.
We are cooperating fully with [agency]. Updates at [channel] every [interval].
```

#### Type 3: data breach / cyber
```
We detected a potential security incident affecting [systems/data] on [date].
We immediately [contained / engaged forensic firm / notified authorities].
No evidence of [customer data / financial data] compromise at this time.
We will notify affected individuals per legal requirements. Updates at [URL].
```

### Approval chain (non-negotiable)
```
Draft the statement, allow 5 minutes for legal review, 2 minutes for the Crisis Lead, and 1 minute for the spokesperson. Then post it.
```
Target the first post within 15 minutes of event detection.

## Channel strategy (US cascade)

### Phase 1: owned channels (T+0 to T+30 min)
| Channel | Content | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| **Twitter/X** | Holding statement + thread | T+15 min |
| **LinkedIn** | Professional statement | T+20 min |
| **Website** | Dark site / crisis page live | T+30 min |
| **Email** | Stakeholder alert (investors, employees) | T+30 min |

### Phase 2: wire services (T+30 min to T+2 hr)
| Service | Timing | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Business Wire / PR Newswire** | T+45 min | Official record, disclosure compliance |
| **AP / Reuters / Bloomberg** | T+60 min | Journalist pickup, authority |
| **GlobeNewswire** | T+60 min | Financial media |

### Phase 3: proactive outreach (T+1 hr to T+4 hr)
| Target | Method | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Top 10 national reporters** | Direct email + call | Exclusive background briefing |
| **Local markets affected** | Local TV assignment desks | Visual assets, local spokesperson |
| **Trade vertical reporters** | Direct email | Technical FAQ |
| **Key influencers/analysts** | Direct DM/email | Data pack |

### Phase 4: sustained cycle (T+4 hr to T+72 hr)
| Cadence | Channel | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Every 2-4 hrs** | Twitter/X | Updates, corrections, FAQs |
| **Every 6 hrs** | LinkedIn | Stakeholder letter |
| **Daily** | Wire service | Updated release |
| **Daily** | Cable news | Spokesperson availability |
| **As needed** | Print (NYT/WSJ/WaPo) | Deep-dive cooperation |

## Message discipline (US-specific)

### Message guidance
- Lead with what the organization is doing for affected people.
- Give specific facts, such as "50 engineers deployed," instead of broad claims like "working hard."
- Add the date, time, and timezone to updates, for example, "As of 2:15 PM ET, August 15."
- Use photos of the response instead of stock footage.
- Name the affected location and response, for example, "In [City], we have [X] teams on the ground."
- If facts are not confirmed, say, "We're gathering facts and will update at [time]."
- Use plain language. Do not shift blame, make claims without evidence, or attack reporters. If expressing sympathy, also describe the action being taken.

## Post-crisis (T+72 hr to T+30 days)

### After-action review (day 3-5)
| Element | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| **Timeline** | Minute-by-minute with decisions |
| **Media analysis** | Volume, sentiment, key narratives, share of voice |
| **Stakeholder feedback** | Survey + interviews |
| **Legal/Regulatory status** | Filings, inquiries, investigations |
| **Lessons learned** | 3-5 actionable improvements |

### Reputation recovery (30-day plan)
| Week | Focus | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Facts established, accountability shown | Media sentiment > neutral |
| **2** | Concrete remediation announced | Stakeholder trust survey |
| **3** | Progress visible, third-party validation | Third-party audit/partner |
| **4** | New safeguards operational | Employee/customer confidence |

## US-specific legal/regulatory triggers

| Trigger | Agency | Timeline | Comms Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Workplace fatality** | OSHA | 8 hours | Holding statement mandatory |
| **Environmental release** | EPA/State | Immediate | EPA media protocol |
| **Data breach (500+)** | HHS/State AG | 60 days (HIPAA) | Breach notification law |
| **Securities fraud** | SEC | Immediate | Reg FD, no selective disclosure |
| **Product recall** | CPSC/FDA/USDA | Immediate | Joint announcement |
| **Transportation incident** | NTSB/DOT | Immediate | NTSB media protocol |
| **Financial fraud** | SEC/DOJ | Immediate | No selective disclosure (Reg FD) |
