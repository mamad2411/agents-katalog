# Foreign Press Center Japan (FPCJ), Japan market

**Scope**. Official gateway for foreign media/companies to access Japanese press infrastructure. Pair with `publicist-japan` + `publicist-japan-kishaclub`.

## What is FPCJ
- **Operator**. 外国人記者センター (Foreign Press Center Japan)
- **Mission.** Assist foreign correspondents in Japan and help foreign entities access media
- **Legal basis**. 外務省「外国常駐ニュース機関と外国記者采访条例」
- **Services.** Press registration, interview arrangement, press tours, briefing, translation, and press club access

## Eligibility & registration

### Foreign correspondents (駐在外国記者)
- **Requirement**. Foreign news org with Tokyo bureau + 外務省登録
- **Benefit**. 日本記者クラブ (JNPC) 利用、官公庁記者会見出席、取材ビザ支援
- **Process**. Employer applies → 外務省審査 → 外国記者登録証発行

### Foreign companies (non-resident)
- **No direct "membership."** FPCJ serves journalists, not companies.
- **Access path**. Hire registered foreign correspondent OR work through Japanese PR agency with FPCJ ties
- **Press tour participation**. FPCJ organizes tours for foreign media, companies can pitch inclusion

## Services for foreign companies

### 1. Press registration card (外国記者登録証)
- **For**. Your foreign correspondent / hired freelancer
- **Enables**. 官公庁記者会見出席、記者クラブ施設利用、取材ビザ更新
- **Apply via**. FPCJ (employer sponsorship required)

### 2. Interview arrangement (取材アレンジ)
- **FPCJ role**. Match foreign media interest with Japanese sources
- **Company role**. Provide Japanese spokesperson + materials + JST availability
- **Format**. FPCJ briefing room / online / on-site

### 3. Press tours (プレスツアー)
- **FPCJ-organized**. Themed tours (tech, environment, culture, disaster recovery)
- **Company pitch**. Propose site visit / executive interview as tour stop
- **Timing**. Plan 2-3 months ahead; FPCJ selects participants

### 4. Press conference support (記者会見支援)
- **Venue**. FPCJ briefing room (内幸町), foreign media friendly
- **Services**. 同時通訳、資料翻訳、映像収録、配信支援
- **Cost**. Room fee + interpretation (実費)

### 5. Translation / materials
- **Japanese → English**. Press releases, fact sheets, Q&A prep
- **Quality**. FPCJ-registered translators (journalism background)

## Protocols for foreign companies

### Recommended practice
- Hire an FPCJ-registered correspondent for ongoing Japan coverage.
- Use a Japanese PR agency with FPCJ or press-club relationships.
- Prepare a Japanese-speaking spokesperson or budget for an interpreter.
- Keep the embargo exact in JST and record it in ISO format and JST.
- Disclose foreign ownership and the absence of a local entity.
- Use the FPCJ briefing room for events aimed at foreign media.

### Access limits
- FPCJ does not provide press-club access to company staff. Access is for journalists.
- Provide Japanese translations with materials sent to Japanese media.
- A Japanese PR agency may help arrange local press-club access.
- Support claims about local presence, traction, or approval with evidence.
- Obtain the required 取材ビザ for media activities. A tourist visa does not cover them.

| Entry Point | Mechanism | Proof Required |

## Press conference via FPCJ (vs. Kisha Club)

| Dimension | FPCJ Briefing Room | Kisha Club (兜/東商/日銀) |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary audience** | Foreign correspondents (駐在/訪問) | Japanese beat reporters |
| **Language support** | 同時通訳標準装備 | 日本語のみ (通訳自前) |
| **Access control** | FPCJ登録記者のみ | 幹事社承諾 + クラブ加盟社のみ |
| **Venue cost** | 室料 + 通訳実費 | クラブ施設使用料 (安価/無料) |
| **Credibility signal** | "Foreign co. serious about Japan" | "Domestic co. standard practice" |
| **Best for** | Global announcement, foreign investor/partner message | Domestic market penetration, earnings |

## Compliance checklist
- [ ] 外国記者登録証保有者経由で官公庁会見出席
- [ ] 取材ビザ取得 (ジャーナリスト活動=報道ビザ)
- [ ] APPI遵守: 日本側連絡先情報の海外持ち出し経路確認
- [ ] 特定電子メール法: 商業性判定・オプトアウト
- [ ] 翻訳正確性: 日本語原文と齟齬なし (法的責任)
- [ ] 恵与禁止: 取材対価・接待・便宜供与なし
