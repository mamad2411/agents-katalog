# US freelancer/contributor pitch protocol, US market

Use this guide when pitching freelance journalists and contributors who write for multiple outlets. Pair it with `publicist-us` and, for industry coverage, `publicist-us-trade-verticals`.

---

## Why freelancers matter now

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| **Freelance share of bylines** | 30-50% at major outlets |
| **Staff reduction (2019-2024)** | 25-40% at major papers |
| **Freelancer income median** | $30-50k/year (supplemented) |
| **Outlets per freelancer** | 3-8 average |

Freelancers often pitch story ideas to editors, so give them the materials they need to make that pitch.

---

## Freelancer vs staff journalist (pitch differences)

| Dimension | Staff Journalist | Freelancer |
|---|---|---|
| **Pitch target** | Editor assigns | Freelancer pitches editor |
| **Decision power** | Can greenlight | Must sell to editor |
| **Time horizon** | Daily/weekly | Weeks to months |
| **Risk tolerance** | Low (editor backs) | High (own reputation) |
| **Payment** | Salary | Per piece ($200-3000) |
| **Exclusivity** | Outlet owns | Negotiable (often retained) |

When pitching a freelancer, include the evidence and access they need to propose the story to an editor.

## Freelancer pitch protocol

### Initial outreach
```
Subject: Story idea for [Outlet/Beat]: [Specific Hook], [Your Expert/Asset]

Hi [Name],

I read your recent [Outlet] piece on [specific topic], especially [specific detail].

I have a story that fits your [beat] perfectly:

[1 sentence: what's new]
[1 sentence: specific data/access/expert]
[1 sentence: why it fits [Outlet] readers now]

I can send a brief or join a 10-minute call. You would own the byline; I am only connecting you with the source.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Title, Org]
[Phone] | [Email]
[Relevant links only]
```

### Key differences from staff pitch
| Element | Staff Pitch | Freelancer Pitch |
|---|---|---|
| **Subject line** | "Pitch: [Topic]" | "Story idea for [Outlet]: [Hook]" |
| **Value prop** | "Cover this" | "Here's a ready-to-file story" |
| **Assets** | Press kit link | Draft outline, expert access, data, photos |
| **Exclusivity** | Assumed | Explicit: "Exclusive to you/Outlet" |
| **Timeline** | "Let me know" | "Draft by [date] if interested" |

## What freelancers need to say yes

### The ready-to-file package
| Asset | Required? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft outline/angle** | Yes | 3-5 bullet structure |
| **Expert access** | Yes | Named, available, pre-vetted |
| **Exclusive data** | Strongly | CSV, charts, methodology |
| **Visuals** | Preferred | Photos, charts, B-roll links |
| **Second source** | Preferred | Named, contact info |
| **Background doc** | Optional | 1-pager for context |

### Financial transparency
- **Never offer payment** for coverage (ethics violation)
- **Cover expenses** if on-site (travel, meals), disclose upfront
- **Honoraria** for expert time, standard $200-500, disclose
- **No "exposure" promises**, insulting

## Contractual & legal

### Standard freelancer agreement points
| Clause | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Rights** | First North American Serial Rights (FNASR) |
| **Kill fee** | 25-50% if killed after assignment |
| **Revisions** | 2 rounds included |
| **Kill fee timeline** | Within 48h of assignment |
| **Expenses** | Pre-approved only |
| **Copyright** | Freelancer retains, licenses to outlet |

### Your obligations
- **No editorial control**, freelancer owns the story
- **Fact-check support**, provide docs, not edits
- **Timely response**, 24h max for fact checks
- **No pre-pub review**, violates editorial independence

## Approaches to avoid
- Do not ask a freelancer to write about the organization. Offer a story they can pitch to an editor.
- Never offer payment for coverage.
- Do not ask to review a story before publication.
- Do not demand exclusivity before the freelancer has sold the story to an editor.
- Provide access to an expert instead of sending only a press kit.
- Allow more than a week for a feature assignment.
- Offer a second source when available.

Before sending, verify the freelancer's current outlet and beat. Include the story angle, source access, supporting assets, and any relevant exclusivity terms.
