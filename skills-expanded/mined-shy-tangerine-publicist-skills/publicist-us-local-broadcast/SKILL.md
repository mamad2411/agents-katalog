# US local broadcast (TV/Radio), US market

Use this guide to target local TV and radio stations across US Designated Market Areas (DMAs). Pair it with `publicist-us`.

---

## DMA system overview

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| **Total DMAs** | 210 (Nielsen) |
| **Top 10 DMAs** | NYC, LA, Chicago, Philadelphia, Dallas-Ft. Worth, SF-Oakland, Atlanta, Houston, Boston, Phoenix |
| **Coverage** | 100% US households |
| **Local TV Stations** | 1,700+ (commercial + public) |
| **Local Radio Stations** | 15,000+ (AM/FM) |

### DMA tiers (pitch strategy)
| Tier | DMAs | Population | Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Top 10** | 1-10 | 40%+ US pop | Dedicated producers, competitive, need strong visual/hook |
| **11-25** | 11-25 | 25% US pop | Professional desks, more accessible |
| **26-50** | 26-50 | 20% US pop | Smaller staff, more flexible |
| **51-100** | 51-100 | 10% US pop | General assignment, eager for local stories |
| **101-210** | 101-210 | 5% US pop | One-person desks, very receptive |

## TV pitch protocol

### Station structure
| Role | Responsibility | Pitch Target? |
|---|---|---|
| **News Director** | Overall editorial | No (too senior) |
| **Assignment Editor** | Daily story assignment | **YES**, primary gatekeeper |
| **Executive Producer** | Show-level editorial | **YES** for specific shows |
| **Booking Producer** | Guest booking | **YES** for interviews |
| **Segment Producer** | Specific segment content | **YES** for niche topics |
| **Reporter/Anchor** | On-air talent | No (pitch through producers) |

### Show types & pitch angles
| Show Type | Timing | Pitch Window | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Morning News** | 4:30-9:00 AM | 1-2 weeks | Live interviews, consumer tips, local events |
| **Midday** | 11:00 AM-1:00 PM | 1-2 weeks | Expert segments, lifestyle, health |
| **Evening News** | 4:00-6:30 PM | Same day - 1 week | Breaking, investigative, human interest |
| **Late News** | 10:00-11:30 PM | Same day | Breaking, lighter features |
| **Weekend Morning** | 6:00-9:00 AM | 2-3 weeks | In-depth, lifestyle, community |
| **Public Affairs** | Weekend 5-7 AM | 3-4 weeks | Long-form, policy, non-profit |

### Pitch protocol (TV)
1. **Email Assignment Desk** (primary) + **Booking Producer** (if interview)
2. **Subject**. `STORY IDEA: [Topic], [Visual Hook], [Local Angle]`
3. **Body**. 3 sentences max, Hook, Visuals available, Local relevance, Spokesperson
4. **Attach**. None. Link to press kit + B-roll/photos on Google Drive/WeTransfer
5. **Follow-up**. Call the assignment desk 2-3 hours after emailing. TV desks often work by phone.

A TV pitch is ready when it reaches the right desk, states the local angle, links the visuals, and names the available spokesperson.

### Visual requirements (non-negotiable)
| Asset | Specs | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| **B-roll** | 1080p/4K, .mov/.mp4, 30-60 sec clips | WeTransfer/Google Drive link |
| **Photos** | 1920x1080 min, .jpg, horizontal | Google Drive link |
| **Lower-third info** | Name, title, org, pronunciation | Text in email |
| **Live shot info** | Location, parking, contact, best time | Text in email |

### Booking an interview
```
Subject: GUEST PITCH: [Name/Title], [Topic], [Visuals Available]

Hi [Producer Name],

[Spokesperson] is in [City] on [Date] and available for a [live/remote] interview on [Topic].

Why now: [One-sentence news hook]
Visuals: [B-roll type / demo / location shoot available]
Time: [Time window, e.g., "6:00-9:00 AM live window"]

Press kit: [Link]
B-roll: [Link]
Contact: [Your name, cell, email]

Best,
[Name]
```

## Radio pitch protocol

### Station types
| Format | Audience | Pitch Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **News/Talk** | 25-54, engaged | Expert interviews, call-in segments |
| **Public (NPR/PRI)** | 35-64, educated | In-depth, policy, human interest |
| **Sports Talk** | Male 25-54 | Athlete interviews, analysis |
| **Music (CHR/AC/Country)** | Varies | Artist interviews, concerts, contests |
| **Spanish-Language** | Hispanic 18-49 | Bilingual spokespeople, community issues |

### Radio pitch protocol
1. **Email Program Director / Producer** (not host directly)
2. **Subject**. `RADIO GUEST: [Name/Topic], [Time Available], [Phone/Studio Ready]`
3. **Key**. Mention "phone-ready" or "in-studio available". Radio loves phone interviews.
4. **Follow-up**. Call producer (radio = phone culture)

### Radio guest requirements
- **Landline/VOIP quality** (cell = last resort)
- **Quiet environment** (no background noise)
- **Available for 10-30 min segments**
- **Can do multiple stations via phone patch**

## Regional nuances

### Major markets (top 10 DMAs)
- **NYC**. WABC, WNBC, WCBS, WNYW, WPIX, NY1, Spectrum News NY1
- **LA**. KABC, KNBC, KCBS, KTLA, KCOP, Spectrum News 1
- **Chicago**. WLS, WMAQ, WGN, WFLD, CLTV
- **Philadelphia**. WPVI, KYW, WCAU, WTXF, PHL17
- **Dallas-Ft. Worth**. WFAA, KXAS, KDFW, KTVT, Spectrum News 1

### Secondary markets (11-50)
- **Strong local loyalty.** The "local angle" must be genuine.
- **Fewer national feeds.** More appetite for local content.
- **Weather/Traffic anchors** often double as feature reporters

### Small markets (51-210)
- **One-person newsrooms** common
- **General assignment reporters** cover everything
- **Very receptive** to well-packaged local stories
- **Limited B-roll capacity.** Provide everything.

## Timing (all times local)

| Market Tier | Morning Show Pitch | Evening News Pitch | Weekend Pitch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Top 10** | 1-2 weeks out | 1 week - same day | 2-3 weeks |
| **11-50** | 1-2 weeks | 1 week - same day | 1-2 weeks |
| **51-210** | 1 week - 3 days | Same day - 3 days | 1 week |

### Best call/email windows
| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| **5:00-6:30 AM** | Morning show planning meeting. **Don't pitch** |
| **6:30-9:00 AM** | Morning show live. **Don't pitch** |
| **9:00-11:00 AM** | **Best pitch window**. Post-show, pre-noon planning |
| **11:00 AM-1:00 PM** | Midday show planning. Good for midday pitch |
| **1:00-3:00 PM** | **Best evening news pitch window** |
| **3:00-5:00 PM** | Evening show live. **Don't pitch** |
| **After 5:00 PM** | Late news planning. Only breaking news |

## Compliance (US broadcast)

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **FCC Sponsorship ID** | Paid content = "Sponsored by [Company]" on-air + online |
| **Political File** | Political ads = public inspection file |
| **Children's TV Act** | Educational content requirements (if applicable) |
| **Closed Captioning** | Required for all new content |
| **EAS** | Emergency Alert System participation |
