## Selection decision tree

```
| Need | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Public company, SEC filing, or earnings | Business Wire or PR Newswire |
| Investor relations, financial media, or earnings | GlobeNewswire or Business Wire |
| Enterprise, global reach, or disclosure-grade distribution | PR Newswire or Business Wire |
| Budget, SEO, or online visibility | PRWeb, EIN Presswire, or PR Underground |
| International distribution and translation | PR Newswire or Business Wire |
| Multimedia distribution | PR Newswire for lower image costs, or Business Wire for free logos |
| Low-budget SMB or SEO-only distribution | PRWeb, EIN Presswire, or PR Underground |
| Combined strategy | 20% enterprise wire for credibility and 80% SEO wire for volume |
```

## Service deep dives

### PR Newswire (Cision)
- **Network**. 440k+ journalists, 170 countries, 300k+ media outlets
- **Strengths**. Largest legacy network, strong industry targeting, Cision media monitoring integration
- **Weaknesses**. Highest cost, membership fee, expensive overages
- **Best for**. Enterprise, global reach, industry-specific targeting

### Business Wire (Berkshire Hathaway)
- **Network**. 100k+ media outlets, 100+ countries, strong financial/SEC
- **Strengths**. SEC compliance native, free logos, no membership fee, stronger financial media
- **Weaknesses**. Expensive images, smaller legacy network than PRN
- **Best for**. Public companies, SEC filings, earnings, IR, financial media

### GlobeNewswire (Notified)
- **Network**. 150k+ media, strong financial media, 150+ countries
- **Strengths**. Financial media focus, IR/earnings expertise, translation included in some tiers
- **Weaknesses**. Smaller general media network
- **Best for**. IR, earnings releases, financial media targeting

### PRWeb (Cision)
- **Network**. Online, SEO-focused, Google News, 1000+ sites
- **Strengths**. SEO-focused, affordable, Cision backend, good for SMB
- **Weaknesses**. No print/TV/radio distribution, no journalist database access
- **Best for**. SMB, SEO, online visibility, product launches

### EIN Presswire
- **Network**. Online, Google News, 1000+ sites
- **Strengths**. Low cost, simple, good for niche
- **Weaknesses**. Limited analytics, basic distribution
- **Best for**. Budget, niche announcements, SEO

### PR Underground
- **Network**. Online, niche, Google News
- **Strengths**. Lowest cost, simple
- **Weaknesses**. Limited reach, basic features
- **Best for**. Micro-budget, local/niche, SEO

## Compliance checklist (all services)
- [ ] **SEC Regulation FD** (public companies), material info via wire first
- [ ] **CAN-SPAM**, opt-out, physical address, honest subject
- [ ] **FTC**, disclosure of material connections
- [ ] **State privacy laws**, CCPA/CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, UCPA
- [ ] **FCC Sponsorship ID** (if paid placement on broadcast)
- [ ] **Impressum/Contact**, physical address, contact in release

## Wire, regional, and direct outreach

| Pillar | Tool | Role | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Wire** | PR Newswire / Business Wire / GlobeNewswire | Official record + SEO + Broad distribution | Release day 6:00-9:00 AM ET |
| **2. Regional** | State/regional media + local broadcast | Local trust + TV/radio + state govt | Embargo / Same day AM |
| **3. Direct** | Journalist platforms + direct pitch | Exclusives + relationships + trade | Pre-embargo / Post-wire |

Do not rely on wire distribution alone. Use direct and regional outreach when the announcement needs reporter attention beyond the wire.
