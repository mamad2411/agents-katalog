# US journalist request platforms, US market

Use this guide to assess US source-request platforms and their response rules. Pair it with `publicist-us`.

---

## Platform comparison matrix

| Platform | Model | Cost | Best For | AI Policy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Qwoted** | Free for journalists; experts pitch | Free (expert) / Paid (brand) | Expert commentary, media requests | **Bans AI-written commentary** (Community Guidelines) |
| **HARO (Help a Reporter Out)** | Free; 3x daily emails | Free | Broad expert requests, high volume | No explicit AI ban |
| **SourceBottle** | Free/paid; AU/US/UK | Free tier + paid plans | Niche expert requests, media leads | No explicit AI ban |
| **ProfNet (PR Newswire)** | Paid; expert database | Paid (PR Newswire account) | Licensed expert database, credentialed | No explicit AI ban |
| **JournoRequests** | Twitter/X based | Free | Real-time, Twitter-native | No explicit AI ban |

---

## Platform deep dives

### Qwoted (primary recommendation)
- **Model**. Journalists post requests, and experts pitch them on the platform.
- **Cost**. Free for experts; brands pay for dashboard
- **AI Policy**. **Strict**, "Community Guidelines prohibit AI/LLM-generated commentary. Do not draft or submit a Qwoted response using AI."
- **Workflow**. The journalist posts a request. Experts see it in the dashboard or email and pitch on the platform. The journalist reviews responses and connects with sources.
- **Response Time**. Journalists often respond within hours
- **Best for**. Expert commentary, thought leadership, reactive PR
- **AI Risk**. **High**, platform actively detects/filters AI content

### HARO (help a reporter out)
- **Model**. 3x daily emails (5:35 AM, 12:35 PM, 5:35 PM ET) with journalist requests
- **Cost**. Free
- **Volume**. 50-100+ requests per email
- **Competition**. High (hundreds of pitches per request)
- **Response Window**. Often same day, first-come advantage
- **Best for**. High-volume reactive PR, broad expert positioning
- **Quality**. Variable, many low-quality requests mixed with gold

### SourceBottle
- **Model**. Similar to HARO but curated; free/paid tiers
- **Geography**. Strong in AU, growing in US/UK
- **Cost**. Free tier + paid plans for brands
- **Niche**. Better for lifestyle, health, wellness, parenting
- **Dashboard**. Web-based request browsing
- **Best for**. Lifestyle, health, consumer, niche experts

### ProfNet (PR Newswire)
- **Model**. Licensed expert database + request distribution
- **Cost**. Requires PR Newswire account (paid)
- **Credibility**. Journalists trust ProfNet sources (vetted)
- **Integration**. Part of PR Newswire/Cision ecosystem
- **Best for**. Credentialed experts, institutional credibility, enterprise

### JournoRequests
- **Model**. Twitter/X account (@JournoRequests) retweets journalist requests
- **Cost**. Free
- **Format**. Real-time, Twitter-native
- **Speed**. Real-time, first-come-first-served
- **Best for**. Real-time reactive, Twitter-savvy teams
- **Limitation**. No dashboard, manual monitoring required

## Qwoted deep dive (primary platform)

### Expert onboarding
1. **Create profile**. Expertise, credentials, bio, headshot, topics
2. **Verification**. LinkedIn, credentials, publication history
3. **Topics**. Select 10-20 relevant topic tags
4. **Availability**. Set response time preferences

The profile is ready when expertise, credentials, biography, headshot, relevant tags, and response preferences are set.

### Pitch best practices (Qwoted)
| Element | Best practice |
|---|---|
| **Subject line** | Specific to request title + your unique angle |
| **Opening** | "Hi [Journalist Name], saw your request on [topic]..." |
| **Credibility** | Lead with specific credential (not generic bio) |
| **Angle** | One clear, contrarian, or data-backed take |
| **Availability** | Explicit: "Available for call today 2-4 PM ET" |
| **Contact** | Direct phone/email in pitch (not just platform) |
| **AI Check** | **Never use AI to draft**. Platform detects it. |

### Qwoted AI policy
- Platform uses **AI content detection** on all submissions
- **Flagged pitches** = auto-rejected, account risk
- **Response**. Draft Qwoted submissions yourself and use your own words.
- **Policy**. "Do not draft or submit a Qwoted response using AI"

### Qwoted follow-up protocol
- **No off-platform follow-up** unless journalist replies
- **Platform messaging only** until journalist moves to email
- **One pitch per request.** No multiple angles.

## HARO workflow (high volume)

### Daily routine
1. **5:35 AM ET**. Check the morning email and flag relevant requests.
2. **12:35 PM ET**. Check midday email
3. **5:35 PM ET**. Check evening email
4. **Pitch within 30 min** of receipt for competitive requests

### HARO pitch template
```
Subject: HARO Response: [Request Title], [Your Unique Angle]

Hi [Journalist Name],

Saw your request on [topic] in today's HARO.

[1 sentence: your unique credential/angle]
[2-3 sentences: specific insight/data/contrarian take]
[1 sentence: why this matters to their audience now]

Available for [call/email/interview] [specific time windows].
Direct: [phone] | [email]

Best,
[Name]
[Title, Company]
[LinkedIn/Twitter]
```

### HARO success factors
- **Speed**. Pitch within 30 minutes of email
- **Relevance**. Only pitch requests matching exact expertise
- **Conciseness**. 3-4 sentences max
- **Availability**. Explicit time windows

## ProfNet (enterprise)

### When to use
- Client has **credentials** (PhD, C-suite, board seat, recognized authority)
- Need **institutional credibility** with journalists
- Budget allows **PR Newswire retainer**
- Targeting **top-tier outlets** (WSJ, NYT, Bloomberg, etc.)

### ProfNet workflow
1. Client enrolled in ProfNet database
2. Journalist searches ProfNet for expert
3. PR Newswire notifies account manager
4. Account manager coordinates interview
5. **All communication through PR Newswire** (not direct)

### Cost
- Bundled with PR Newswire retainer ($3,000-10,000+/month)
- Not available standalone

## Compliance & ethics

### AI usage by platform
| Platform | AI Policy | Enforcement |
|---|---|---|
| **Qwoted** | **Banned**. "Do not draft or submit using AI" | Automated detection, account ban |
| **HARO** | No explicit policy | None known |
| **SourceBottle** | No explicit policy | None known |
| **ProfNet** | No explicit policy | Human review |
| **JournoRequests** | No explicit policy | None |

### General practice
- Have a human draft every pitch, including on platforms without an explicit AI ban.
- Disclose AI-assisted research when the platform requires it.

### Journalist privacy
- **Never share** journalist contact details off-platform
- **Respect platform boundaries.** No off-platform contact unless invited.
- **Honor opt-outs** immediately
