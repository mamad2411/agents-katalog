# Stakeholder communication review

- Version: `1.0.0`
- Owner: Paperclip Executive
- Source: adapted from OpenExecutive board, marketing, sales, people, and legal communication guidance at `13da433bc6f3ae97e78bb8c90f06bb5e49953447`.

## Trigger and input

Use when an authorized actor requests a draft or review for a named audience. Require audience, purpose, approved facts, decision/ask, sensitivities, channel, owner, and approval route.

## Procedure

1. Lead with the material change, decision, or ask for that audience.
2. Preserve factual provenance, uncertainty, misses, and dissent.
3. Remove unsupported claims, jargon, and confidential detail outside audience need.
4. Make ownership, next action, timing, and approval status explicit.
5. Return a draft plus a fact-check list and approval dependencies.

## Stop

Stop at draft/review. Never send, publish, contact stakeholders, make legal representations, disclose restricted information, or imply approval.
