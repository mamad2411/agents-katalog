# Bellingcat Companies & Finance Investigation Tools

Tools for researching companies, business registries, financial records, sanctions lists, and trade data.

## Choosing Tools

- Start with official registries and filings for a company or officer; use sanctions, ownership, trade, and blockchain sources to extend the picture.
- Match entities by jurisdiction, registration number, and dates rather than name alone.

## Investigation Tips

- Start with OpenCorporates for the most comprehensive global company registry lookup
- Search offshore databases (ICIJ Offshore Leaks Database, OCCRP Aleph) for hidden ownership
- Use EDGAR for US company filings and financial disclosures
- Cross-reference sanctions lists (EU Sanctions Map, OpenSanctions, SanctionsExplorer)
- Check trade data (ImportYeti, UN Comtrade) to trace supply chains and business relationships
- For EU companies, use the EU consolidated corporate registers and North Data
