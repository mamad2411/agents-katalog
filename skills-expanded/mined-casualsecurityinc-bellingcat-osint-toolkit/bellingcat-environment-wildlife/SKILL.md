# Bellingcat Environment & Wildlife Investigation Tools

Tools for investigating environmental issues, wildlife crime, deforestation, climate change, and biodiversity.

## Choosing Tools

- Use satellite and environmental datasets for land, weather, and ecosystem changes; use trade and species databases for wildlife or supply-chain questions.
- Check geographic coverage and update frequency before drawing conclusions from a dataset.

## Investigation Tips

- Use Global Forest Watch to track deforestation and fire alerts in near real-time
- CITES Trade Database and Wildlife Trade Portal for investigating illegal wildlife trade
- Global Fishing Watch uses AIS data to track fishing vessel activity worldwide
- WildEye tracks environmental crime cases and court convictions globally
- BirdNet and Merlin help identify bird species from audio/visual evidence
- Amazonia Socio Ambiental (RAISG) provides critical Amazon rainforest data and maps
