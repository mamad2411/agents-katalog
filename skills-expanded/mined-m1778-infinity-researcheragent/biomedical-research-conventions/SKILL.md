# Biomedical and Cancer Research Conventions

Guidelines for analyzing oncology, molecular biology, and pharmacological literature.

## Standing Safety and Regulatory Notice
**All generated outputs are research aids only and DO NOT constitute clinical guidance or medical advice. Any preclinical or clinical translation requires independent laboratory validation and appropriate institutional regulatory/ethical approval.**

## Methodological Criteria
1. **Model System**: Distinguish in vitro (cell line, organoid), in vivo (xenograft, murine), and clinical trial cohorts.
2. **Trial Phase**: Classify clearly: Preclinical, Phase I (safety/MTD), Phase II (efficacy/ORR), Phase III (randomized controlled trial / OS).
3. **Biomarker Stratification**: Note specific mutations (e.g., KRAS G12D vs G12C), allele frequencies, co-mutations (TP53, STK11, KEAP1), and resistance pathways.
4. **Endpoint Reporting**: Explicitly distinguish overall response rate (ORR), progression-free survival (PFS), and overall survival (OS).
