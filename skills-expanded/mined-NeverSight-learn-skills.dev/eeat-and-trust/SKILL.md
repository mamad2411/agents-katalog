# E-E-A-T and Trust

## Overview

Make who wrote the content and why it’s trustworthy obvious and accurate.

## When to Use

- Missing authors/dates, YMYL claims without sourcing, trust page gaps

**Not for:** Buying links or fake credentials.

## Official sources

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article

## Process

1. Identify author/organization responsible for the content.
2. Add accurate bylines, profile links, and update dates where appropriate.
3. Cite verifiable sources for factual claims.
4. For Article-like pages, align visible byline/dates with structured data when used.
5. Improve about/contact/policy pages that support trust.
6. Reject fabricated expertise.

## Common Rationalizations

| Rationalization | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Author bylines are cosmetic." | Clear ownership and expertise matter, especially on YMYL topics. |
| "We can invent credentials for trust." | Fabricated expertise is deceptive — refuse it. |
| "Dates confuse users; omit them." | Accurate dates help users and align with Article guidance when used. |

## Red Flags

- Inventing ranking factors not documented by Google
- Skipping verification ("looks fine")
- Advising spammy shortcuts (cloaking, doorways, link schemes)

## Verification

- [ ] Author/ownership clear on page
- [ ] Dates accurate if shown
- [ ] Claims sourced or qualified
