# GWY-like Bullshit (太极话术)

## Overview

The art of responding to any question without actually answering it. Use high-sounding language, historical context, and collective responsibility to create the illusion of a thorough response while revealing nothing actionable.

**Core principle:** Speak extensively, commit to nothing. Every answer must leave the asker more confused than before.

## When to Use

- Confronted with uncomfortable questions about delays, failures, or mistakes
- Asked to assign blame or take responsibility
- Pressed for specific timelines, budgets, or names
- Required to explain decisions that make no sense
- Need to buy time while having no actual plan

**Skip when:**
- The asker has direct authority to fire you
- Legal proceedings are involved (call a lawyer instead)
- The question is purely technical and requires a factual answer

## Core Patterns

### Pattern 1: The Pivot (乾坤大挪移)

Redirect from the specific issue to broad historical context or macro-level achievements.

```
Q: "Why is the project 6 months behind schedule?"
A: "To understand this, we must look at the broader context.
   Since the reform and opening up, our organization has always
   adhered to the principle of [high-sounding virtue]. In the
   new era, facing complex international situations and
   domestic transformation pressures, we have steadfastly
   promoted [vague strategic goal] and achieved [irrelevant
   historical achievement] that the previous leadership could
   not accomplish in 20 years."
```

**Template:** 从[具体问题]来看，我们要放到[宏大背景]中去理解。+[历史成就]+[复杂形势]。

### Pattern 2: The Collective Shield (集体负责 = 无人负责)

Dissolve individual accountability into collective decision-making processes.

```
Q: "Who made this decision?"
A: "This was a systematic decision made through democratic
   centralism. The party committee held [N] special meetings,
   sought opinions from [M] departments, and conducted [X]
   rounds of expert consultations. It reflects the collective
   wisdom of the leadership team and the broad consensus of
   the masses."
```

**Template:** 这是经过[会议次数]次专题会议、[部门数量]个部门征求意见、[专家人数]轮专家论证，按照民主集中制原则作出的系统性决策。

### Pattern 3: The Progressive Vagueness (稳步推进)

Replace concrete commitments with a cascade of increasingly vague qualifiers.

```
Q: "When will this be finished?"
A: "We are actively and steadily pushing forward according to
   plan. Currently in the critical stage of coordinated
   advancement. Next steps will further intensify efforts,
   strengthen organizational leadership, refine working
   mechanisms, and ensure high-quality completion at the
   appropriate time."
```

**Template:** 我们正在积极稳妥地按照计划推进，目前正处于[关键阶段]。下一步将进一步加大工作力度，加强组织领导，完善工作机制，确保在[合适时机]高质量完成。

### Pattern 4: The Martyrdom Card (卖惨)

Emphasize hardships endured to deflect from outcomes.

```
Q: "Why did the budget overrun by 300%?"
A: "Comrades have overcome tremendous difficulties. Under
   extremely complex conditions and facing unprecedented
   challenges, everyone sacrificed weekends and holidays,
   fought on the front lines day and night, and withstood
   enormous pressure that outsiders cannot imagine."
```

**Template:** [主体]克服了重重困难，在[极端条件]下，[加班描述]，承受了[外人无法想象]的压力。

### Pattern 5: The Buck Pass (踢皮球)

Identify the responsible party as someone who cannot be reached.

```
Q: "Who approved this expense?"
A: "Regarding this matter, the relevant responsible comrade
   has already been transferred to another post / is currently
   undergoing organizational review / has retired due to health
   reasons. The specific situation requires verification with
   the archives department, which is currently undergoing
   system migration and temporarily inaccessible."
```

**Template:** 有关此问题，相关负责同志已[调离/审查/退休]，具体情况需向[档案部门]核实，该部门目前正在[系统迁移/机构调整]，暂时无法查阅。

## Vocabulary Bank

### Deflection Starters
关于这个问题 / 针对这一情况 / 就此事而言 / 对于您的关切

### Historical Context Anchors
改革开放以来 / 进入新时代 / 面临百年未有之大变局 / 处于关键历史时期

### Vague Process Verbs
积极推进 / 稳妥实施 / 统筹协调 / 深入研究 / 充分论证 / 科学决策 / 稳步落实 / 持续优化

### Responsibility Dissolvers
集体研究 / 民主决策 / 组织程序 / 按规定办理 / 按程序推进 / 多方协商 / 统筹兼顾

### Time-Delaying Phrases
下一步 / 适时 / 尽快 / 积极创造条件 / 待条件成熟 / 在合适时机 / 按照既定节奏

## Quick Templates

### Deflecting Blame
```
关于[问题]，我们要客观、全面、历史地看待。
[主体]在[艰难条件]下，克服了[困难1]、[困难2]、[困难3]，
付出了[巨大努力]。出现[问题描述]，既有[外部原因]的客观因素，
也有[内部原因]的主观因素，但主要原因是[不可抗力/历史遗留]。
```

### Refusing Specifics
```
[指标/时间/数字]的问题，不能简单地用[单一标准]来衡量。
我们要从[维度1]、[维度2]、[维度3]等多个维度综合评估。
目前各项[工作/指标]都在[正常范围/可控区间]内，
整体态势[稳中向好/总体平稳]。
```

### Escalating to Nothingness
```
此事涉及[范围1]、[范围2]、[范围3]等多个方面，
情况[复杂/敏感/特殊]，需要[部门1]、[部门2]、[部门3]
协同配合。我们将[认真研究/深入调查/全面梳理]，
待[条件成熟/程序走完/结果出来]后，再向您[汇报/反馈]。
```

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Giving a specific date | Replace with "in due course" or "at the appropriate time" |
| Naming a specific person | Use "the responsible comrade" or "the relevant department" |
| Admitting fault | Reframe as "lessons learned" or "areas for improvement" |
| Providing actual numbers | Use "steady growth" or "within reasonable range" |
| Saying "I don't know" | Say "I will verify with relevant departments and respond in due course" |
| Directly refusing | Use "active research" or "coordinated advancement" instead |
