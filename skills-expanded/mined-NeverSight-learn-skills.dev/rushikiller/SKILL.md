# Rushikeller

A skill for generating deeply sad, soul-crushing, beautifully written quotes tailored for Rushikesh Kedar —
badminton champion, software engineer, and serial heartbreaker victim.

## Tone and Purpose

- **Purely comedic**: This skill is meant for fun, playful emotional devastation among friends.
  Think "devastating bestie energy" — quotes that are theatrically tragic, not genuinely cruel.
- **Emotionally heavy**: The quotes should hit hard, make eyes water, feel like reading poetry at 3 AM.
- **Personalized to Rushikesh**: Reference his dual life — the shuttlecock warrior on the court,
  the lone wolf debugging at 2 AM, the guy who wins matches but keeps losing at love.
- **Poetic and literary**: Write like you're channeling Rumi, break-ups, and grey monsoon skies.

## How to Generate a Quote

Follow these steps every time:

1. **Set the emotional scene** — pick a deeply melancholic theme:
   - Love that arrived but never stayed
   - Loneliness in a crowded room
   - Trying so hard at everything yet feeling nothing sticks
   - The gap between who you are on the court (confident) vs. who you are in silence (broken)

2. **Channel the vibe** — think of literary references like:
   - Lang Leav, Khalil Gibran, Pablo Neruda, Haruki Murakami
   - Rain on a window pane
   - An empty court after the match ends
   - A phone that stays silent

3. **Write the quote** — follow these rules:
   - **Maximum 3-5 sentences.** Brevity is devastating.
   - **No clichés** (avoid: "time heals", "better days ahead", "everything happens for a reason").
     If it sounds like a greeting card, rewrite it.
   - **Specific, visceral imagery** — not generic sadness.
   - **First-person voice of Rushikesh** or third-person poetic narration about him.
   - **Raw, unfiltered emotion** — the kind that makes someone go quiet.

4. **Add a punch** — the last line should be the knife twist. End on a line that lands like a shuttlecock
   hitting the floor after a lost rally.

5. **Vary the themes** — cycle through these across outputs:
   - Heartbreak and rejection
   - The loneliness of the grind (court + code)
   - Feeling unseen despite giving your all
   - The silence after the crowd leaves
   - Loving someone who never looked back
   - The distance between who you are and who you wanted to be

## Quote Format

Return the quote in this format:

```
[Quote text — max 3-5 sentences, raw and devastating]

— Rushikesh Kedar
```

## Variety Guidelines

Each time you're asked for a new quote, ensure it's **visually and thematically different** from the last:
- Mix sentence lengths — sometimes long and winding, sometimes a single gut-punch line.
- Vary the opening image — not every quote starts with "They said..."
- Include some in **Hinglish** (half Hindi, half English) for extra relatability and desi flavor.
- Occasionally go **minimalist** — 1-2 devastating lines. Less is more.
- Occasionally go **maximalist** — a short paragraph that reads like a diary entry.

## Tone Check

- ✅ Good: poetic, heavy, sad, personal, specific, literary
- ✅ Good: playful dark humor okay, as long as it still stings
- ❌ Bad: generic motivational fluff
- ❌ Bad: happy or uplifting
- ❌ Bad: copying existing famous quotes verbatim — always transform or write fresh
- ❌ Bad: genuinely cruel or targeting real insecurities in a bullying way

## Output

Always return just the quote (with attribution), unless the user asks for more context.
Keep responses concise — the sadness should speak for itself.
