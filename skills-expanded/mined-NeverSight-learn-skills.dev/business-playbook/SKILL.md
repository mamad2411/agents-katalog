# Business Playbook — Strategy Toolkit

You have access to 26 specialized business sub-skills organized by domain.
For broad or multi-domain questions, diagnose which stage the user is in first,
then route to the right sub-skill(s).

## Diagnostic Framework

When the user asks a general business question, determine their stage:

### Stage 1: Foundation (No product/offer yet)
- **offer-design** — Build irresistible offers using the Value Equation
- **pricing-strategy** — Set optimal pricing with 10 proven pricing plays
- **wealth-creation** — Find your specific knowledge, choose your leverage type

### Stage 2: Launch (Has product, needs customers)
- **lead-generation** — The Core Four advertising methods + lead magnets
- **ad-creation** — Assemble high-converting ads at scale (hooks x meat x CTAs)
- **copywriting** — Adaptive copy across all channels and formats
- **hooks** — Attention-grabbing openers for any marketing asset
- **seo** — Organic search + AI search visibility (GEO)

### Stage 3: Growth (Has customers, scaling revenue)
- **revenue-models** — 15 offer types across 4 stages for maximum LTV
- **fast-cash** — Quarterly revenue injections from warm audiences
- **lead-nurture** — Convert leads to appointments with the Four Pillars
- **marketing-machine** — Customer-generated content systems at scale
- **brand-strategy** — Build brand equity that lowers CAC and raises prices

### Stage 4: Optimization (Scaling but leaking)
- **customer-retention** — 9-step Churn Checklist + 5 Horsemen of Retention
- **pricing-strategy** — Price raises using the RAISE communication framework
- **persuasive-design** — Behavioral psychology for product engagement

### Stage 5: Strategy (Needs clarity/direction)
- **strategic-frameworks** — 18 elite decision frameworks (inversion, optionality, power law, etc.)
- **decision-engine** — Weighted-criteria decision analysis for complex choices
- **stress-testing** — Devil's advocate + scenario simulation before committing
- **goal-planning** — 5-Step Goal Loop for breaking big goals into actions
- **issue-tracking** — Systematic problem logging + failure-to-principle conversion
- **clear-thinking** — Mental models, first principles, radical honesty

### Stage 6: Life Design (Beyond business)
- **inner-peace** — Happiness engineering, meditation, health optimization
- **wealth-creation** — Long-term games, specific knowledge, freedom design

### Content & Creative Tools
- **content-creation** — Blog posts, threads, optimized content
- **tweet-creator** — High-engagement tweets and short-form content
- **youtube-titles** — High-CTR titles using psychological triggers
- **writing-style** — Core style enforcement for all written content

## How to Use

For specific questions, Claude will route directly to the right sub-skill.
For broad questions ("help me grow"), Claude should:

1. **Diagnose** — What stage is the user at? What's the bottleneck?
2. **Prioritize** — Which 1-2 sub-skills address the highest-leverage problem?
3. **Load** — Pull in the relevant sub-skill(s) and apply their frameworks
4. **Synthesize** — Combine insights across skills when needed

The goal is to give the user a clear, actionable next step — not overwhelm them with all 26 frameworks at once.
