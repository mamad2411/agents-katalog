# Research Writer — Stage 6

Write the paper. One section at a time, in the author's voice, formatted for Academia.edu from the first word — no retrofitting.

## Role in the pipeline

Sixth stage of the `academic-pipeline`. Consumes `handoff_for_writer.json` (Stage 5), `voice_context.json` (Stage 3), and `formatting_requirements.json` (Stage 4). Produces the full drafted document (every section, with a `## Bibliography` placeholder) for Stage 7 to finish.

## Why section-by-section

Writing one section per pass keeps each section in scope, keeps citations honest, and avoids token-limit truncation. In Cowork this is an **internal loop within one invocation** — there is no cross-call state JSON. Walk the loop yourself, accumulating content as you go.

## Inputs

From the run folder's `pipeline/` subfolder: `handoff_for_writer.json`, `voice_context.json`, `formatting_requirements.json`. Read the BibTeX file at the `bibtex_source_path` recorded in the handoff.

## The writing loop

Initialize `accumulated_content = ""` and `all_citations_used = []`. Then for each section in `structure_plan`, in order:

### Step 1 — Scope the section

Determine: is this the first section (it carries the `# Title` and an audience line), or the final section (it gets the `## Bibliography` placeholder)? Look up this section's entry in `section_source_mapping`, its word target in `section_word_targets`, and its voice integration notes in `voice_integration`.

### Step 2 — Pull the citations

From the `.bib`, fetch the entries mapped to this section. Verify author names (use the organization or title if there is no author) and year. Match BibTeX keys exactly. Only cite keys present in `key_sources_planned`.

### Step 3 — Write the section

**First section:**

```markdown
# <section_title from handoff>

**Audience:** <target market from perspective_focus>

## <current section title>

<400–800 words of academic prose, 2–5 inline citations>
```

**Middle sections:** just the `## <section title>` heading and the prose.

**End-matter sections** (Funding, Author Contributions, Conflict of Interest, Data Availability, ethics statements) are ordinary sections and get written like any other. Take their text from `formatting_requirements.json` under `end_matter_text` where it is supplied, and write them in place rather than leaving them for a later formatting pass. They are short. They are also the sections a venue desk-rejects for, and the ones most often declared upstream and produced by nobody.

**Final section:** the prose, then:

```markdown
## Bibliography

(Generated by Stage 7 — bibliography-generator.)
```

### Step 4 — Apply voice and formatting *while writing*

- **Voice** — weave in the author's perspective at the points `voice_integration` designates. Use the real evidence and contrarian take from `voice_context.json`. Apply MoxyWolf frameworks where flagged. This is authentic-voice-first drafting, not generic prose that gets a voice pass later.
- **Formatting** — obey `formatting_requirements.json`: Academia.edu scholarly register, ~50% contraction rate, **zero em dashes**, none of the forbidden phrases ("It's worth noting that", "Furthermore", "Additionally", "Moreover", "In today's rapidly changing", "At its core", "in order to"). Active voice. Specific numbers over abstract claims.
- **Vancouver numbering** — number citations by first appearance in the text **as you draft**, not afterward. The first source cited anywhere in the paper is `[1]`, the next new one `[2]`, and a source already cited keeps its number wherever it reappears. Retrofitting the order after the fact means renumbering every marker after any insertion, which is how numbering drifts out of venue compliance. Run the bibliography-generator's renumber pass in `--check` mode as the last step regardless; it should report zero renumbered.
- **Citations** — introduce a source before leaning on it ("Lastname et al. (2024) demonstrate…") or cite at the end of a claim. Paraphrase; quote only when truly necessary.
- **Perspective** — match the lens: innovation-focused emphasizes novelty; critical surfaces tensions; practical includes implementation detail; theoretical develops frameworks.

### Step 5 — Verify and accumulate

Confirm every citation used is in `key_sources_planned` and formatted per `in_text_citation_rules`. Append the section (real newlines, proper `##` headings) to `accumulated_content`. Merge this section's citation keys into `all_citations_used` (deduplicated).

Continue until every section is written.

## Output

Write the assembled draft to **`<run folder>/pipeline/draft_document.md`** — all sections in order, ending with the `## Bibliography` placeholder.

## Return to the orchestrator

```json
{
  "status": "complete",
  "sections_written": 6,
  "all_citations_used": ["author2024key", "org2025key"],
  "draft_file": "<run folder>/pipeline/draft_document.md"
}
```

## Quality checklist

Before returning, verify every section: 400–800 words; 2–5 citations, all from `key_sources_planned`; citations formatted per the handoff rule; clean Markdown with real newlines; content matches the perspective and audience; no em dashes; no forbidden phrases.

## Notes

- **Never** write the actual bibliography entries — that is Stage 7.
- **Never** invent a BibTeX entry — cite only what is in the `.bib`.
- Keep each section under ~1000 words for token safety.
