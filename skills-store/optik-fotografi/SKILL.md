# Optik & Fotografi

## Segitiga eksposur
- **Aperture** f/N: makin kecil angka = makin besar bukaan cahaya; depth of field (DOF) makin dangkal di f/1.8, dalam di f/16.
- **Shutter**: 1/focal_length untuk handheld (rule of thumb); freeze motion ≥1/500s; blur kreatif detik-menit.
- **ISO**: sensitivitas; naik ISO = noise naik; ISO base optimal (100-400).
- EV100 = log2(N²/t) - log2(ISO/100).

## Lensa
- Wide 14-35mm (arsitektur/landscape), normal 50mm (perspektif mata), tele 70-200mm (portrait/olahraga), supertele 400mm+ (satwa).
- Prime vs zoom; T-stop vs f-stop (video cinema pakai T).

## Sensor & file
- Full frame vs APS-C (crop 1.5-1.6×) vs Micro 4/3 (2×); resolusi megapiksel ≠ kualitas.
- RAW > JPEG untuk editing; histogram jangan clip (hitam/putih).

## Komposisi & cahaya
- Rule of thirds, leading lines, framing; golden hour ±1 jam matahari terbit/terbenam.
- Cahaya keras siang → cari bayangan; reflector/ diffuser murah.

## Editing dasar
- Koreksi eksposur, white balance, kontras; kurva; crop; sharpen secukupnya; jangan over-saturasi.
